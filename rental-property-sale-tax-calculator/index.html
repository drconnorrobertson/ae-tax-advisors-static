How the model calculates your scenario

  • For each asset: net amount realized = allocated sale price − allocated selling costs.
  • Adjusted basis = basis before depreciation − depreciation allowed or allowable.
  • Gain = net amount realized − adjusted basis. The model stops when an individual asset has a loss because loss netting requires additional return-level analysis.
  • Section 1245 ordinary recapture = the smaller of that asset group’s gain or depreciation. Remaining gain enters the modeled long-term category under the stated Section 1231 assumptions.
  • For the straight-line building, unrecaptured Section 1250 gain = the smaller of building gain or depreciation. Remaining building gain and land gain enter the modeled long-term category.
  • Apply the selected rates to each category, then add the optional NIIT scenario and flat state estimate.
  • Closing cash = net amount realized − debt payoff. Cash after the reserve subtracts modeled tax from closing cash.

The asset rows aggregate only assets with compatible treatment and nonnegative individual gains. A gain on one appliance and a loss on another should not be hidden by entering only a combined number. Return-level calculations may differ because of other gains, losses, deductions and rate stacking.

What to review before relying on a sale estimate

Cost segregation history

Reconcile each disposed asset to its tax classification, remaining basis and sale allocation. Certain land improvements or accelerated real property do not fit the simple building or Section 1245 categories used here.

Loss carryforwards

Suspended passive losses and prior Section 1231 losses can change tax character or total liability. The calculator does not release carryforwards or apply the five-year lookback.

Ownership and transaction form

A partnership-interest sale, corporate owner, 1031 exchange, related-party transaction or installment sale requires a different analysis. Use this model only within its stated assumptions.

Tax versus cash

Loan payoff reduces cash available at closing. It generally does not reduce realized gain in this cash-sale model. An owner can have a substantial taxable gain and limited cash after debt service.

Rental sale calculator questions

Is all depreciation recapture taxed at 25%?

No. Section 1245 recapture is generally ordinary income. Unrecaptured Section 1250 gain is a separate category subject to a maximum federal rate of 25%. Other gain may receive different treatment, and other facts can change the result.

Should I enter depreciation claimed or depreciation I should have claimed?

Reconcile depreciation allowed or allowable with your preparer. Missing depreciation can still affect basis and sale treatment; do not assume unclaimed depreciation leaves basis unchanged.

Does the calculator decide whether I owe NIIT?

No. The optional 3.8% amount is a sensitivity scenario on all modeled gain. Actual net investment income tax requires a return-level calculation involving income, thresholds and activity facts.

Are my amounts sent to AE?

The calculator runs in your browser and its code does not transmit or save the entered amounts. You can print the scenario using your browser. Contact AE separately if you want a review.
