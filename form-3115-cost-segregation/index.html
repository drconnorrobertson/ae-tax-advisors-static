Form 3115 for Cost Segregation and Missed Depreciation

An existing property needs more than a new depreciation report. Establish the correct correction procedure, reconcile the asset history and assign responsibility for the filing.

Book a Call

When a cost segregation lookback needs a filing review

A lookback study revisits depreciation for an existing property. Form 3115 requests a change in accounting method or the treatment of an item, but it is not the correct procedure for every error. Begin with the filed returns, acquisition and service dates, ownership, elections and depreciation actually claimed. See IRS Form 3115 instructions.

AE Tax Advisors can discuss the study, reconciliation and filing work needed for the particular property. The proposal should identify who determines the correction procedure and who prepares the return and any method-change application.

Reconstruct the depreciation history

The review file should reconcile the purchase documents, supported land allocation, building basis, separately purchased assets, improvements and every prior depreciation schedule. Resolve duplicates and missing records before applying a new classification.

  • Original acquisition and placed-in-service dates.
  • Ownership entity and relevant changes in ownership.
  • Depreciation schedules and filed returns for prior years.
  • Improvement invoices, earlier studies and asset dispositions.
  • Any elections or prior correction work affecting the assets.

See the study document checklist and report-to-basis reconciliation.

Understand the Section 481(a) reconciliation

Where the method-change procedure applies, the workpaper compares the relevant prior treatment with the proposed treatment to prevent omission or duplication. The calculation and timing depend on the change and applicable procedure. It is not simply a percentage of the purchase price.

The preparer should use the rules applicable to the assets and tax years involved. Ordering a study today does not replace historical depreciation rules with today’s bonus percentage. IRS Publication 946 provides depreciation guidance.

Agree on the preparation and filing deliverables

Ask the preparer to identify the governing procedure, eligibility conditions, applicable change number, required form sections, statements and adjustment workpaper. Confirm the taxpayer, year of change, return deadline and any duplicate-copy requirement using current IRS guidance.

A completed study is not evidence that the application was prepared or filed. Request a final signed packet, the return attachment and records of the required submissions. See current Form 3115 instructions and updates.

Study pricing and implementation scope

Published standard cost segregation pricing is $1 per square foot, with a $2,000 minimum per study. Complex work may be quoted separately. Advisory, Form 3115 preparation and tax returns require their own confirmed scope. Compare study pricing and deliverables.

Confirm separately whether the engagement includes prior-schedule reconstruction, method-change analysis, Form 3115 preparation, state adjustments and tax-return filing. A catch-up calculation in the study scope does not automatically include every implementation task.

If your existing CPA files the return, establish the preparer handoff before ordering the study. Discuss suspended losses and future disposition consequences as part of the owner-level review.

Bring a property summary to the first call

Summarize when you acquired and first used the property, how it is owned, which returns have been filed, whether a study exists and whether a sale is planned. Identify the preparer who maintains the depreciation schedule. The next step is to define the records and work required, not to promise a refund before reviewing the file.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Form 3115 recover missed depreciation?

It may be the appropriate method-change procedure for the facts. The preparer must establish eligibility, calculate the adjustment and evaluate deduction usability.

Does a new study always require Form 3115?

No. The correction route depends on the prior treatment, returns, assets and applicable procedures.

Is the catch-up amount an immediate refund?

No. An adjustment or deduction must be evaluated with the owner’s return and applicable limits.

Is preparation included in the study fee?

Do not assume it is. Confirm the distinction between a catch-up calculation, Form 3115 preparation and return filing in the written scope.

What if I am selling the property?

Disclose the sale timing before the review. Eligibility, correction procedures and disposition consequences need coordinated analysis.

Practical review: cost segregation on property purchased years ago

An older property's depreciation deserves review when its asset records may not reflect the appropriate classifications. Start with what was acquired, when it entered service, and what has already been reported. The date you order a study does not rewrite the property's history.

Form 3115 requests a change in accounting method or the treatment of an item. Whether a depreciation correction belongs on Form 3115, an amended return, or another procedure depends on the facts and applicable rules. See the IRS Form 3115 resource.

Build the historical record first

Gather acquisition documents, every available depreciation schedule, improvement records, prior elections, and ownership changes. Identify assets previously sold, replaced, or deducted separately.

Do not send only the latest schedule if it omits earlier disposals or corrections. A year-by-year file helps the preparer understand how today's numbers developed.

Use AE's document checklist to organize the packet.

Ask how the catch-up amount was calculated

For an applicable method change, the preparer should explain the Section 481(a) calculation and its connection to prior depreciation. Ask for an asset-level reconciliation rather than a single unexplained adjustment.

The review should identify the original dates, applicable depreciation treatment, amounts previously accounted for, and any excluded assets. A new purchase-year assumption should not be applied to an old asset without analysis.

Clarify the filing assignment

Your proposal should identify who prepares the study, who determines the correction procedure, who calculates the adjustment, and who files the required documents. A report provider may not be your return preparer.

Ask the preparer to check the currently applicable automatic-change procedures and eligibility conditions. A form number alone does not establish that your change qualifies or that the filing is complete. The Form 3115 instructions are a procedural starting point.

Make the planning decision before paying for the work

Explain whether a sale, refinance, ownership change, or filing deadline is approaching. Request an estimate of the full engagement and the information still needed to evaluate current benefit.
