Montana Tax Landscape

Montana offers a unique tax environment for business owners. The state has no sales tax, which simplifies compliance for retail and service businesses. Montana's income tax applies to individuals and C corporations, while pass-through entities flow income to their owners. Understanding how these state-level rules interact with federal tax strategy is essential for minimizing total tax liability.

Ranch and Agricultural Tax Strategy

Montana ranchers and agricultural operators face unique tax considerations. Income timing around commodity sales, equipment depreciation under Section 179, land use deductions, and conservation easements all factor into the optimal strategy. Mark Simonsen works with these clients to build plans that account for the cyclical nature of agricultural income.

Short-Term Rental Tax Planning

Montana's growing STR market in resort and tourism areas creates significant tax planning opportunities. Cost segregation combined with material participation under the 7-day rule can produce substantial deductions that offset active income for qualifying property owners.
