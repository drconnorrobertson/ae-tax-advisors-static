Maryland Tax Landscape

Maryland imposes a corporate income tax rate of 8.25% on businesses operating in the state. Individual taxpayers face state income tax plus county piggyback taxes ranging from 2.25% to 3.20%, pushing combined state and local rates above 8.95% for high earners. Maryland's pass-through entity (PTE) tax election provides a workaround for the federal $10,000 SALT deduction cap.

DC-Maryland-Virginia Corridor Planning

Many Maryland business owners have employees, clients, or operations in DC and Virginia. Christina Nortman and the AE Tax Advisors team evaluate multi-state exposure to minimize aggregate state tax liability across jurisdictions, including income apportionment, nexus planning, and credit optimization for taxes paid to other states.

Maryland Real Estate Tax Strategy

Maryland's real estate market offers significant opportunities for tax-savvy investors. Cost segregation studies on commercial properties, multifamily buildings, and short-term rentals in the Baltimore-DC corridor can produce substantial first-year deductions. The growing STR market in areas like Annapolis, Ocean City, and the Eastern Shore creates opportunities for the material participation strategy.
