Pittsburgh Real Estate Tax Strategy

Pittsburgh's real estate market offers significant opportunities for tax-savvy investors. Cost segregation studies on commercial properties, multifamily buildings, and short-term rentals can produce substantial first-year deductions. The city's growing STR market in neighborhoods like Lawrenceville, the South Side, and the Strip District creates opportunities for the material participation strategy under the 7-day rule.

Pennsylvania Tax Considerations

Pennsylvania's flat 3.07% personal income tax rate is relatively low compared to neighboring states. However, Allegheny County's local earned income tax, business privilege tax, and property tax structure add complexity. AE Tax Advisors navigates these layers to minimize your total state and local tax burden.

Entity Structuring and Retirement Plans
