Frequently Asked Questions

Should I operate as an S-corp or stay as an employee?

Depends on income level, business structure, and goals. S-corp election can save $15K-$100K+ annually on self-employment/FICA taxes.

How much self-employment tax can I save?

Maximum saving is 15.3% on reasonable net earnings above reasonable salary. With proper planning, physicians and attorneys often save $20K-$200K+ annually.

Can I deduct continuing education and conferences?

Yes, but the deduction has been limited. We ensure you capture all allowable professional development and licensing costs.

What about student loan interest and forgiveness?

Student loan interest deduction phases out at higher incomes. Some programs offer tax-free forgiveness. We analyze your best strategy.

How do I handle multi-practice situations?

If you're a partner in multiple practices or locum tenens work, proper allocation and timing can significantly reduce taxes.
