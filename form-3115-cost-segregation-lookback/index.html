Form 3115 Cost Segregation Lookback: Catching Up Missed Depreciation

Review Form 3115 eligibility, Section 481(a) adjustment timing and owner-level deduction limits before claiming missed depreciation.

Schedule Your Free Discovery Call

By AE Tax Advisors Team · Billings, Montana · Updated November 6, 2026

Choose the correction procedure before ordering a lookback

A study can identify depreciation classifications worth reviewing, but it does not determine how a filed return should be corrected. An established impermissible depreciation method may require a method change; mathematical errors, elections and some first-year corrections can follow different rules. Identify the taxpayer, property, acquisition and service dates, prior treatment and requested year of change first.

An eligible Section 481(a) adjustment prevents amounts from being omitted or counted twice when a method changes. It can incorporate cumulative differences originating in earlier years. That does not reopen those years for unrestricted refund claims or waive the scope conditions for the particular change. See Form 3115 versus an amended return.

Reconstruct the adjustment and its timing

Reconcile the old and proposed methods through the beginning of the year of change. Determine the applicable basis, classifications, depreciation conventions, historical bonus eligibility and elections from the records. Today's bonus percentage does not automatically apply to an asset placed in service years earlier.

A negative Section 481(a) adjustment generally reduces income in the year of change. A positive adjustment generally increases income over four years, with exceptions including examination-related and other special rules. Some changes use a cut-off method instead. The actual calculation and applicable procedure determine the sign and timing; not every study creates a deductible negative adjustment.

Illustrative records review

An owner acquired a commercial building several years ago and now commissions a study. The preparer first separates land, reconciles acquisition basis and improvements, and checks prior depreciation and elections. The study supports component classifications. The preparer then determines whether a method change is available and models the adjustment and owner-level limitations. This is a hypothetical workflow, not a client result or projected refund.

Check automatic-change eligibility and filing responsibilities

Use the current IRS List of Automatic Changes and its applicable modifications, not a remembered designated change number alone. Confirm scope, prior changes, examination status, ownership and any disposition-related rules. Automatic consent is conditional on satisfying the procedure; it is not a finding that the proposed deduction is correct. Nonautomatic requests have different timing, requirements and potential user fees.

For an eligible automatic change, the original application generally accompanies the timely filed federal return, including extensions, with a separate duplicate filed as instructed. Verify the current destination or permitted transmission method, timing and required attachments. Keep evidence of both submissions and identify who signs, files and handles follow-up. Retain the study and supporting records; attach the materials required for the particular application rather than assuming every study must be submitted in full.

Renovations and dispositions require separate analysis

A study may identify replaced components still on a fixed-asset schedule. A partial disposition election is not automatically available late through Form 3115. Determine the disposition year, timely-election requirements and whether specific relief exists. Do not assume the old DCN 199 late-election route remains available for a current transaction.

Disposing of property does not create a universal bar to every depreciation correction. Specific procedures can address certain disposed property, while other scope restrictions apply. Have the preparer examine the applicable change before concluding that selling either forfeits or guarantees the opportunity.

Model the owner's ability to use the loss

A current-year negative adjustment may still be limited by basis, at-risk rules, passive activity rules or Section 461(l). Real estate professional status requires the relevant qualification tests, and rental material participation must also be established under the applicable activity or grouping rules. Qualification alone does not automatically release previously suspended passive losses. A short customer-use period also does not replace material-participation analysis.

Review state conformity, anticipated sale timing and recapture. Accelerated depreciation changes timing and can affect later gain character and tax; it does not promise permanent savings. A supported study should explain methods, classifications and evidence rather than rely on a universal reclassification percentage.

What to request in the preparer handoff

  • Reconciled basis, historical schedules and the study's asset classifications.
  • A written correction-procedure and eligibility conclusion.
  • The Section 481(a) calculation, sign and adjustment period.
  • Owner-level loss and state-treatment analysis.
  • Assigned preparation, signing, filing and follow-up responsibilities.

Primary sources

  • IRS Form 3115 instructions and current automatic-change references
  • Revenue Procedure 2015-13: general method-change procedures
  • IRS Publication 946: depreciation
  • IRS Publication 925: passive activity and at-risk limits

Frequently Asked Questions

How far back can a Form 3115 cost segregation lookback go?

An eligible method change can include cumulative differences from earlier years in a Section 481(a) adjustment. This is not an amended refund claim reopening every year. The applicable procedure, ownership, records, method history and scope restrictions determine eligibility.

Do I need to amend prior year returns?

It depends on the correction. An established depreciation method may require Form 3115, while some errors, elections or first-year corrections follow other procedures. Have the preparer identify the correct route before filing.

Is the catch-up deduction spread over multiple years?

A negative Section 481(a) adjustment is generally taken in the year of change; a positive adjustment is generally included over four years. Exceptions apply. Owner-level loss limits can defer use of a negative adjustment.

Where do I file Form 3115?

For an eligible automatic change, generally attach the original to the timely filed return, including extensions, and submit the required duplicate under current IRS instructions. Confirm the applicable address or permitted transmission method and retain filing evidence.

Will a large adjustment offset my other income?

Not automatically. Review basis, at-risk, passive-activity and excess-business-loss limits. Real estate professional status alone does not establish material participation or release previously suspended losses.
