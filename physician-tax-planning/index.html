Tax Planning for Physicians and Medical Practice Owners

Build a tax plan around how you earn your income: medical practice profits, independent contractor work, employment, or a combination of these and investment property.

Book a Call

Medical practice owners and 1099 physicians

AE Tax Advisors serves physicians who own practices or have business income that needs coordinated advisory and compliance work. A doctor running a practice has different decisions from an employed physician with only W-2 income. Start by mapping income sources, ownership, payroll, retirement arrangements and major transactions.

This service can be relevant when starting independent contractor work, buying into a practice, changing an entity, hiring staff, buying a practice building or preparing for a sale. The objective is a plan with defined actions and responsibilities, rather than an advertised savings amount.

Match the planning review to your income

Physician situationPlanning questions
Practice ownerHow do owner pay, staff costs, retirement funding and distributions fit the practice’s cash flow?
1099 or locum tenens workWhich expenses, entity choices, estimated payments and retirement options need review?
W-2 employment plus a businessWhich income and benefits belong to the employer, and which belong to the separate business?
Practice owner with rentalsWho owns each asset, and how do rental activity and owner-level limits affect the combined return?

W-2 wages do not become business receipts simply because a physician forms an LLC. Separate the employment relationship from any actual independent business.

Entity choices and S-corporation owner pay

Before an entity election, compare projected compensation, payroll administration, state treatment, benefits, distributions and longer-term ownership plans. The IRS requires reasonable compensation for services by S-corporation shareholder-employees before non-wage distributions. See IRS guidance on S-corporation compensation.

Use the S-corp owner salary guide to assemble the role and pay evidence. A target tax saving does not establish the appropriate salary.

Retirement planning without ignoring the practice budget

Compare available plan designs with employee eligibility, expected contributions, administration and cash commitments. A larger deduction can require a larger funded commitment. Coordinate the practice’s plan with other arrangements and the responsible plan administrator. See IRS retirement plans for self-employed people.

Discuss retirement and exit planning before adopting a plan or making a transaction commitment. Tax modeling should state the year and assumptions used.

Practice buildings and investment property

Keep the practice operating business, property ownership and rental activity distinct in the records. A building purchase may call for basis reconciliation and a separately scoped depreciation study. Rental losses require their own analysis; professional status as a physician says nothing about real estate participation.

Review medical-office cost segregation or residential STR cost segregation for the property involved. AE’s core study audience is residential investment property; confirm whether a medical-office project fits the current scope.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this service for employed doctors?

The focus is practice owners and physicians with business or investment complexity. A W-2-only situation may need a different scope; discuss fit on the first call.

Do all 1099 physicians need an S corporation?

No. Evaluate the facts, compensation, benefits, state rules and administration before choosing an entity.

Can you coordinate practice income and rental properties?

That can be part of a scoped advisory engagement. Property study and return-preparation fees are separate and should be confirmed.

Do you guarantee six-figure savings?

No. Results depend on income, records, eligibility, implementation and timing. A written model should distinguish deductions, current savings and deferrals.

Can I retain my current accountant?

Discuss the coordination arrangement and make sure the engagement assigns implementation and filing responsibilities.
