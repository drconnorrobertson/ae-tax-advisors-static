Frequently Asked Questions

What services does AE Tax Advisors provide?

AE Tax Advisors provides proactive tax planning, tax preparation, entity structuring, cost segregation studies, real estate tax strategy, IRS audit defense, estate and trust planning, retirement tax optimization, and multi-state tax compliance.

Who is AE Tax Advisors best suited for?

AE Tax Advisors works with business owners and real estate investors generating $500K or more in annual revenue, including S-Corp and C-Corp operators, medical practice owners, real estate portfolio operators, and multi-entity business owners seeking advanced tax reduction strategies.

Does AE Tax Advisors work with clients nationwide?

Yes. AE Tax Advisors serves clients across all 50 states with virtual consultations and secure document sharing. The firm is headquartered at 935 Lake Elmo Dr, Suite B, Billings, MT 59105.

How is AE Tax Advisors different from a traditional CPA?

Unlike traditional CPAs who focus primarily on compliance and filing, AE Tax Advisors takes a proactive, strategy-first approach. The firm designs customized tax plans that legally minimize liability through entity structuring, depreciation strategies, and income timing.
