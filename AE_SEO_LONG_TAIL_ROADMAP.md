Updated September 23, 2026 from the supplied organic-position export. This is a working editorial roadmap, not a promise of rankings or traffic.

## Opportunity score

The 100-point score is a prioritization aid:

- 30 points: search-demand signal, logarithmically weighted so one head term cannot overwhelm the plan.
- 30 points: ranking leverage, favoring terms already close enough to page one to improve through content and internal links.
- 20 points: attainable difficulty, favoring lower reported keyword difficulty.
- 20 points: commercial intent, capped so unusually high CPC does not control the roadmap.

Before a URL is approved, it must also pass an intent test. A keyword variation does **not** earn a new page when an existing URL already answers the same search task.

## Publication gate for every new indexable page

A page is publishable only when all are true:

1. It owns a distinct search intent and does not compete with an existing AE URL.
2. It contains original analysis, examples, or decision support specific to that fact pattern.
3. Tax statements cite current primary sources, normally IRS forms, instructions, publications, or statutes.
4. The title, H1, description, body, examples, and internal-link neighborhood are materially unique.
5. It has a direct `/discovery/` booking path near the top and after the substantive content.
6. It is linked from a relevant hub and links back to related authoritative pages.
7. Canonical, sitemap, structured-data, and internal-link checks pass.
8. The content-similarity guard produces no warning.
9. A human tax/editorial review confirms accuracy before future tax-year updates are published.
10. If the page does not meet the gate, improve an existing URL or keep the draft out of the index.

This is where scaled-content risk comes into play: automation is acceptable as production support, but mass publication of interchangeable pages created mainly to capture query variations is not. AE's safe path to 2,000 pages is 2,000 defensible search intents and fact patterns, accumulated in controlled batches with consolidation whenever two pages answer the same task.
