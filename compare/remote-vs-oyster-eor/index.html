Remote vs Oyster EOR: Planning an Existing Workforce Transfer

Compare Remote and Oyster EOR proposals for an existing-team transfer, including records, local support and unresolved payroll items.

An existing workforce needs a transition plan

Moving established employees between service arrangements is different from making a first hire. Remote and Oyster both publish EOR offerings, but the transition must be reviewed for the specific employees and locations. Identify the current agreements, service end dates and unresolved payroll or benefits items before asking for a new start date.

The providers describe different operating networks. Remote emphasizes owned entities and in-house teams; Oyster’s materials recognize an Oyster or local partner employer. Ask each provider to name the proposed employer and explain the support chain for your location. Neither description alone establishes that one arrangement is preferable.

A fictional three-person transfer

An owner plans to move three employees from an existing EOR after a service review. One employee has a pending expense, another has approved leave and the third has a compensation change under discussion. Build a sample handoff list containing those unresolved items, without using real employee details.

Ask each provider how the new arrangement would receive the records, identify missing information and coordinate the first payroll. Have advisers review employment continuity and any necessary consent or documentation. The software demonstration should reveal who owns each handoff, not imply that uploading a spreadsheet completes the employment transfer.

Compare exceptions and exit records

Use the same pending expense and leave examples to examine employee support. Determine whether the employee contacts the platform, the local employer or the customer, and how the customer sees an unresolved case. Ask what records can be exported before the old arrangement ends and after the new one begins.

The proposal should identify implementation work, recurring service scope and responsibilities for correcting a problem spanning two service periods. Confirm location-specific eligibility and contractual terms directly. This comparison makes no promise of uninterrupted benefits, automatic contract transfer or reduced liability; those matters require their own documented review.

Decision checklist

  • Name the outgoing and proposed employing entities and identify the local advisers reviewing continuity.
  • List unresolved leave, pay and expense items with a responsible person and evidence reference.
  • Demonstrate how an employee support question crosses the provider/customer boundary.
  • Confirm export access, correction responsibilities and the first accepted payroll before the transition closes.
