A large study deduction is only the starting point

A property report identifies supported classifications. The owner’s tax position determines when deductions produce a usable benefit. Bring existing depreciation, acquisition records, property use, ownership structure and your expected holding period to the planning conversation. A proposed deduction should be evaluated separately from a projected reduction in current tax.

For example, an illustrative report may identify $120,000 of accelerated deductions. Multiplying that figure by a 35% assumed rate gives $42,000 before considering limitations and timing. That arithmetic alone does not establish current savings: some losses may be deferred, and the calculation still needs incremental depreciation, fees, state treatment and eventual disposition effects. Neither firm’s brand resolves those taxpayer-specific facts.

When AE may be the better engagement fit

Consider AE when your decision involves more than ordering the study: you need to coordinate rental and business income, review a prior depreciation schedule, decide whether implementation costs are worthwhile, or assign work among multiple professionals. Ask AE to specify the planning deliverable and implementation responsibilities before engaging. An existing CPA may remain involved; coordination should make the handoff explicit.

When to request an Ambrose proposal

Request a proposal when you want to evaluate its study approach or its broader Texas real estate services. Compare the same property facts, deliverables and deadlines. We have not commissioned or audited an Ambrose study and do not claim its reports or outcomes are inferior.

Bring these questions to both discovery calls

  1. Who signs off on classifications and what qualifications does the assigned reviewer have?
  2. How are land, building basis, renovations and previously depreciated assets reconciled?
  3. What inspection method applies to this building, and what happens when records are missing?
  4. Who prepares any required method-change work and the final depreciation schedule?
  5. What is the all-in fee for the study, implementation and agreed support?
