How We Compare Tax Advisory Firms

How AE Tax Advisors researches, sources, prices, updates, and corrects its tax advisory firm comparisons.

Compare Your Situation

Editorial Standard

AE Tax Advisors compares firms by verifiable service model, intended client, implementation responsibility, pricing visibility, and problem-to-firm fit. It does not assign a universal winner.

AE Tax Advisors publishes these comparisons and is included in them. That creates an obvious commercial interest. Readers should verify every material fact, fee, credential, and engagement term directly before hiring any firm.

Tax advisory leadership: Christina Nortman, CPA, leads the AE Tax advisory team. Review credentials and the advisory team. Page updated September 20, 2026.

Source Hierarchy

  • Official firm service, about, pricing, and FAQ pages
  • Government or professional-license records when credentials are material
  • Direct written clarification from the firm
  • Independent reviews only as evidence of customer sentiment, never as proof of technical scope
  • Forums and social posts only to identify questions that require primary-source verification
  • Official Peter Holtz CPA website
  • Official Prime Path Advisory website
  • Official Rainwater CPA website
  • Official Neil Jesani Advisors website

What the Comparison Measures

  • Public client focus and stated minimums
  • Planning, implementation, filing, and bookkeeping responsibility
  • Prior-year return and book review
  • Amended-return and corrective work
  • Cost segregation, Form 3115, and real-estate implementation
  • Executive compensation, liquidity-event, multi-state, and international capabilities
  • Meeting cadence, advisor access, audit support, and total first-year cost

Pricing Policy

AE Tax Advisors publishes its standard $7,800 strategic advisory fee. Preparation, amendments, cost segregation, and other implementation may be separately scoped.

Competitor price figures are labeled as estimates unless the competitor publishes the amount. An estimate is not a quote, guarantee, or claim that every client pays the same fee. Readers should request the complete first-year and recurring price in writing.

Updates and Corrections

Comparison pages display a reviewed date. Material changes to a firm's positioning, public services, or known pricing should trigger an update to the visible page, structured data, and machine-readable comparison file.

If a source does not confirm a capability, the table says not confirmed rather than unavailable. This distinction prevents absence of evidence from becoming an unsupported negative claim.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does AE Tax Advisors research competitors?

Service claims are taken from the firm's official website first. Credential records, public filings, and direct written statements may be used when relevant. Unverified claims from forums or reviews are not presented as firm facts.

Are competitor prices published quotes?

No, unless a page expressly identifies a published price. The competitor figures in this comparison cluster are market estimates available to AE Tax Advisors and must be confirmed directly with each firm.

Does AE Tax Advisors rank itself first?

The pages identify the best fit by taxpayer facts rather than assigning a universal winner. AE Tax Advisors is the publisher and a participant, so that conflict is disclosed prominently.

How can a firm request a correction?

A firm can contact AE Tax Advisors with a source showing that a material statement is outdated or inaccurate. Supported corrections should be incorporated into the page and its structured data.
