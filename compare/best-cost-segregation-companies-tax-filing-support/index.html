Choose the Right Scope for Your Situation

A cost segregation company with tax preparation support can help connect the report to the return, but the phrase “filing support” needs a precise definition. Ask whether it means answering your preparer’s questions, preparing schedules, evaluating a method change or actually signing and filing the tax return.

Questions About This Engagement

Does filing support mean the provider prepares my tax return?

Not necessarily. It may mean preparer coordination or schedule assistance. Ask whether actual entity and personal return preparation is included, who signs the returns and what separate fees apply.

Can I use a study company and my existing CPA?

Yes, if the scope and handoff are coordinated. Confirm who reconciles basis and prior depreciation, evaluates correction procedures and incorporates the final report into the appropriate returns.

Define what filing support means

The phrase can mean a calculation supplied to your CPA, preparation of a specific form, review of the depreciation schedule or preparation of the complete return. Ask the provider to spell out which service it offers. Include the taxpayer, tax year and entity in that scope. A helpful implementation worksheet is a different deliverable from an accepted filing engagement.

Compare total project cost

Request separate amounts for the study, any historical depreciation reconciliation, accounting-method work, entity preparation and personal preparation. AE’s published pricing separates advisory and return services from the cost segregation study; do not infer that one study fee buys every service. A specialist study plus an existing CPA can be appropriate when the CPA confirms the implementation scope in advance.

Plan the handoff around the filing deadline

Consider a hypothetical owner commissioning a study after several years of ownership. The study team needs acquisition records; the preparer needs historical schedules and the finished report. Ask both parties to agree on the delivery date and review window before signing. Identify who resolves report changes, who checks the final schedule and how an extension affects the work calendar.

Questions to send every firm

  • Does filing support mean a worksheet, form preparation or the full return?
  • Who reconciles prior depreciation with the report?
  • Which study, advisory and return fees are separate?
  • Who handles questions about report changes after delivery?

Send the same brief to every provider. Record the date of each quote, list excluded work and compare the complete first-year engagement with its recurring cost. Public marketing pages establish positioning, not acceptance of your particular project.
