1-800Accountant Pricing: Packages and Annual Cost

Review 1-800Accountant’s published package prices, annual billing, tax filing scope, bookkeeping limits and the questions to ask before buying.

Book a Call

What the published monthly prices mean

The official pricing page displays monthly rates with annual billing. Tax Advisory is $209 per month, Starter is $299 per month, and Business Complete is $469 per month. Multiplying those rates by twelve gives $2,508, $3,588 and $5,628 respectively. These are annualized advertised rates, not a quote for every business or a statement that installment billing is available.

Software access and bookkeeping service are different purchases

Starter lists a ClientBooks bookkeeping software subscription. Business Complete separately lists full-service bookkeeping with a dedicated bookkeeper. Do not assume that software access means someone will categorize, reconcile and close the books for you. Ask which tasks your team must complete, how errors are handled and what reports arrive each month.

The Business Complete description starts at 75 monthly transactions. Ask what counts as a transaction, which accounts are covered, and how higher volume affects the fee. Its payroll description refers to you as the business owner; employee payroll, additional owners or multi-state payroll need explicit confirmation.

Confirm the filing boundaries before comparing totals

Starter describes personal filing and the annual return for one entity, with Schedule C, partnership, C-corporation or S-corporation forms depending on the business. A second entity, extra filing state, amended year or overdue return should be named in the proposal rather than assumed to fit that package.

  • List each entity, owner, state and tax year in the quote request.
  • Separate historical cleanup and late filings from current-year work.
  • Confirm the annual payment date, renewal terms and cancellation process.
  • Ask what document or plan you receive after a quarterly review.
  • Confirm which specialist assignments, if any, require an additional engagement.

A practical annual budget example

A fictional owner with one operating entity and current books might compare Starter’s $3,588 annualized rate with Business Complete’s $5,628 rate if the owner also wants recurring bookkeeping and owner payroll. The $2,040 difference describes these advertised package rates only. It does not establish the owner’s final price or imply that both packages contain identical work. Send the same records and task list to each provider before choosing.

For a separate advisory project, compare the proposed deliverables with AE’s published service fees. Keep return preparation, bookkeeping and other separately scoped work in the total.
