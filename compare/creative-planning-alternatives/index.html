Compare these four approaches

AE Tax Advisors for a focused business tax assignment

If your reason for looking is a specific business tax decision, interview AE about that assignment. Its business service page covers entity structure and owner-pay planning. AE business services.

Ask how the work would connect to your existing investment advisor. A focused tax engagement may be worth comparing with changing your entire financial relationship.

Range for a financial planning membership

Range publishes annual membership plans with wealth services and different levels of tax coverage. Range memberships.

When evaluating it, list the household and business returns you need, then confirm whether the selected tier covers each one. Treat the membership price as the cost of its stated package, not every tax service you might require.

Facet for broader household financial planning

Facet advertises financial planning, investments, retirement, equity compensation, and tax planning and filing offerings. Facet services.

Interview it about your desired advisor relationship and the work covered by the proposed service level. If operating-company taxes drive your decision, ask specifically how that work would be handled.

Domain Money for a flat annual financial relationship

Domain Money describes flat-fee financial planning, managed investments, and tax filing and strategy. Domain Money services.

Ask for the applicable package scope and identify how complex entities, rental holdings and outside professionals would fit into the relationship.

A more useful cost comparison

Write down the services you expect to purchase over the next year. Include financial planning, investment management, business and household filings, property projects, accounting and legal work. Mark each as included, separately quoted or assigned elsewhere.

That exercise can reveal whether you need a new primary advisor or one additional specialist. It also prevents a lower headline fee from disguising a smaller scope.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a flat fee always less expensive?

No. Total cost depends on the quoted fee, included services, your assets and the extra work you need. Compare actual proposals rather than assuming one pricing model wins.

Which option should a business owner interview first?

Start with the most time-sensitive decision. For an operating-company or property tax assignment, compare a specialist proposal. For an ongoing family wealth relationship, evaluate the wider planning and investment scope.

Creative Planning review: service scope and buyer fit

Creative Planning: public services and the decision to compare

Creative Planning's public materials describe wealth management with tax, estate, and related planning services. This is relevant to households seeking coordination between investment management and tax planning. Review the official service description. Your written proposal determines the actual scope and current fees.

Tax scope inside a wealth relationship

Wealth management can connect investments, retirement, estate work, and tax decisions. The comparison should begin with your actual objective: ongoing wealth management, tax filing, or a discrete planning question. Ask how services are bundled and whether a tax project can be evaluated separately. Record the fees associated with each service and whether changing providers requires moving or reconnecting accounts.

If you already have financial and legal advisers, ask how the tax professional will coordinate with them. Compare the planning deliverables, review process, ongoing access, and renewal terms. A membership or broader wealth relationship can cover different work from a focused tax engagement; evaluate the complete scope instead of comparing only the advertised starting price.

A practical comparison for your situation

You already have an investment adviser and want to decide whether tax work should sit inside that relationship or alongside it.

Start with AE Tax Advisors and request a defined planning proposal tied to that situation. Then compare Creative Planning's proposal for the same facts. Ask whether tax planning can be evaluated separately from investment management and estate services.

The question to resolve before choosing

What fees and deliverables apply to the tax work itself?

Ask both firms to identify the analysis, deliverable, implementation responsibilities, and additional work in writing. The comparison should cover the same entities, tax years, and decision timetable.
