TurboTax vs FreeTaxUSA: Compare Personal-Filing Costs and Help

Compare TurboTax and FreeTaxUSA by return eligibility, federal and state prices, expert support and who prepares the personal return.

Book a Call

Compare the same personal return

TurboTax and FreeTaxUSA offer different combinations of software and support. Begin with the tax year, income types and forms you need. A free tier may have narrower eligibility than a paid self-preparation product, and professional preparation is different from expert help while you prepare the return yourself.

Use current displays rather than an old price table

FreeTaxUSA shows free federal filing and $15.99 per state for 2026 returns. TurboTax’s reviewed DIY display spans $0–$139 federally, with state charges for paid products. Its Free Edition has specific simple-return eligibility rules. Confirm the exact product and tax year rather than assuming these ranges are a quote for your facts.

For a fictional filer with wage income and a rental, check the required schedules in each product. Do not assume eligibility based only on income amount. Keep the feature and price selections together so an upgrade or new form does not silently change the comparison.

Decide how much professional involvement you need

FreeTaxUSA’s optional Pro Support is assistance within self-preparation. TurboTax separately markets Expert Assist and Full Service. Compare the actual work each option promises, including whether a professional prepares and signs the return. Avoid treating all expert-access labels as equivalent.

Review current terms for amendments, post-filing questions and payment methods before checkout. If you need a separate business-entity return or a prospective planning project, identify that work independently. This guide does not test the accuracy of either product or promise a particular refund.
