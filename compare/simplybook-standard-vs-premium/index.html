SimplyBook.me Standard vs Premium: Expanded Capacity and Payment Administration

Compare SimplyBook.me Standard and Premium through capacity, payment administration and accounting-record connections.

Separate capacity from the handoff you need

Standard and Premium address both capacity and administration. More bookings or providers may justify evaluating Premium, but its listed payment-administration and accounting connections also deserve a specific workflow test. Start with the actual constraint rather than describing Premium as universally better.

SimplyBook.me’s pricing page distinguishes free custom features from premium custom features. Unlimited premium features does not mean every external service, transaction or communication has no separate charge. Keep the subscription allowance and outside operating costs distinct.

A fictional training business with an accounting handoff

A training business has several instructors and wants office staff to reconcile booked sessions with payment records. Build a fictional month containing completed sessions, a moved booking and a refund requiring review. Demonstrate the Standard process the office would use today.

Then demonstrate the proposed Premium connection to the chosen accounting system and the relevant Payments PRO functions. Ask the demonstrator to show which records move, their identifiers and what staff must still check manually. The existence of an integration name is not evidence that every required field or exception is handled automatically.

Inspect failures before choosing the plan

Have the office locate a booking with no matching payment and an imported record that needs correction. Confirm who reviews it and which system owns the final decision. Premium’s higher provider and feature allowances should be evaluated separately from this accounting acceptance test.

Request the current setup requirements, eligible account region and external-product subscriptions for the proposed integration. Verify booking-count definitions and separately billed items such as messaging. No scheduling plan guarantees reconciliation accuracy, payment success or tax compliance. Choose a documented configuration that the team can support and verify after a service, provider or accounting connection changes.

Use a short mapping sheet with the booking identifier, payment reference, exported record and review outcome. Run it once with a complete payment and once with an exception so the office can demonstrate what the connection actually supplies.

Decision checklist

  • List the capacity constraint separately from integration requirements.
  • Demonstrate moved, unpaid and refunded fictional bookings.
  • Identify records, fields and exception ownership in the accounting connection.
  • Confirm external subscriptions and separately billed services.
