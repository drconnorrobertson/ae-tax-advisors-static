Graphite Financial vs indinero: Custom Back-Office Scope and Accounting Tiers

Compare Graphite’s custom-scoping process with indinero’s named accounting tiers and clarify the work included in each proposal.

Book a Call

Separate accounting needs from broader operations

Graphite’s current pricing page describes a service mix built after reviewing the business, systems and required support. It can involve Accounting, Finance, Tax, Payroll and HR. Indinero publishes Essential, Growth and Executive accounting offers, with additional capabilities customized to the engagement. Start by deciding which responsibilities the business intends to outsource.

A provider handling payroll administration and HR coordination is doing different work from one supplying a monthly accounting close. List existing internal roles and the handoffs between them. Ask for a responsibility map rather than assuming a broad back-office description means every function is included.

Test the handoffs with a real workflow

For a fictional company hiring across several locations, ask who collects payroll changes, approves them, reconciles payroll and prepares management reporting. Identify whether the proposed provider owns each step or coordinates with someone else. Use the same scenario in both scoping calls so the answers can be compared.

Request the recurring calendar, client input deadlines and escalation process. If finance forecasts or management meetings are included, specify frequency and output. An advertised capability is not the same as a named person accepting responsibility under your agreement. Avoid comparing an accounting-only price with a proposal that includes extra operating functions.

Make transition and cost visible

Both proposals should identify cleanup, system implementation, retained software access and any historical-data work. Graphite’s current page emphasizes custom scoping; do not substitute an older advertised fee for today’s quote. Indinero’s starting tiers likewise do not establish a complete budget for all services.

Compare the fee, payment schedule, renewal and exit deliverables after the responsibilities match. Ask what the company will receive if it later brings accounting in-house. AE publishes this provider comparison as a commercial participant and has not reviewed private proposals or independently measured either firm’s service outcomes.
