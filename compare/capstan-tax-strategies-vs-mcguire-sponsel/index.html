How these two options change your shortlist

Investigate Capstan Tax Strategies first when your immediate assignment matches this situation: Your building project could involve more than depreciation, so the service proposal needs to distinguish each technical analysis.

Investigate McGuire Sponsel first when this is the closer description: Your company needs a specialist project while owner-level planning remains with another adviser.

If both situations apply, ask whether one lead professional can coordinate the work or whether separate engagements are needed. A stated specialty is a reason to investigate fit, not proof that the other provider lacks that capability.

Two questions that make the comparison concrete

For Capstan Tax Strategies: Which analyses are separate from the cost segregation study?

For McGuire Sponsel: What handoff documentation will my existing advisers receive?

Ask both teams to return a written scope identifying the assigned professional, the information needed, the result you will receive and the completion date. Compare answers against your actual entities and income sources, rather than counting the number of services on a website.

Compare fees on the same assignment

This comparison does not assert a current numeric fee for either provider. Request a dated proposal from each. Separate the initial review, recurring accounting, written planning, implementation, business returns, personal returns, state filings and any specialist work. An advertised starting price, consultation fee or subscription is not the complete price for every business.

Put both proposals on the same time horizon. Include first-year onboarding or cleanup, outside-professional costs, required renewals and cancellation terms. Request an explanation of whether a projected benefit is a permanent tax reduction, a timing benefit or simply a deduction. No provider’s fee or service model guarantees savings.

Where AE belongs in the comparison

AE Tax Advisors is another option for business owners, real estate investors and high-income professionals seeking a defined tax advisory engagement. The standard published advisory price is $7,800, payable in two $3,900 installments 30 days apart. Tax returns, amendments, property studies and additional work are separately scoped and priced. The standard engagement does not require an annual planning renewal; confirm the actual duration and deliverables in your agreement.
