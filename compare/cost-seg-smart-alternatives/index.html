Digital workflow, review, and CPA acceptance

Convenience is useful when the inputs and deliverable fit the project. Before purchasing an online product, ask what information you must supply, how exceptions are handled, what professional review occurs, and whether the output is an estimate, educational analysis, or final study. Have your return preparer assess the proposed deliverable and the information needed to implement it.

Compare the complete workflow rather than delivery speed alone. A property with renovation history, incomplete records, or unusual features may require additional analysis. Ask what support is available when the preparer has questions and who evaluates the broader owner-level tax position. A digital report and a personalized tax-planning engagement can have different scopes and prices.
