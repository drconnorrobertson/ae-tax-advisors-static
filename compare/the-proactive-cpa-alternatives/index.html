Questions to ask before signing

  • Determine which work the primary firm performs and which specialists are separate engagements.
  • Which entities, individual returns, state returns, meetings, and written deliverables are included?
  • Who will do the work and sign each tax return?
  • Will the firm review old filings and depreciation schedules, and what happens if it finds an error?
  • What is the complete first-year and renewal cost, including implementation?

What to bring to the comparison call

Bring the most recent business and individual returns, a current entity chart, year-to-date financials, and any property depreciation schedules. Describe the decision you need to make this year: an acquisition, entity change, owner compensation, a sale, or a prior-year correction. Ask both firms to specify the tax year, records needed, expected work product, deadline, and person responsible for each action. A proposal that answers those points is more useful than a projected savings figure by itself.
