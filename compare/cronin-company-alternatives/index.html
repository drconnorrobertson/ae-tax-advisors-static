Cronin & Company Alternatives for Profitable Business Owners

Compare alternatives to Cronin & Company by business owner needs, existing CPA coordination, advisory scope and implementation.

Book a Call

Alternatives depend on the job you need done

Owners searching for Cronin & Company alternatives often need one of three things: a second opinion on filed returns, a forward-looking business tax plan, or a team to execute changes. Establish which outcome matters before choosing a provider.

Cronin & Company, LLC describes a Birmingham, Alabama practice serving small to mid-sized business owners, including real estate investors, contractors and growing companies. Its official materials describe proactive tax planning, tax preparation and compliance, and IRS problem resolution. The firm also describes using AI tools in tax planning. These public descriptions establish advertised services, not the exact terms of your engagement.

Three practical routes

  • AE Tax Advisors: consider a discovery call for coordinated business owner and real estate planning. Compare the written proposal and separately scoped execution costs.
  • Your existing CPA plus a planning engagement: ask whether your preparer offers written projections, entity modeling and an implementation calendar. This can preserve continuity when the preparer already understands your books.
  • A specialist for a defined issue: when the need is representation, international reporting or a specific transaction, verify that the assigned professional has relevant experience and that the specialist and preparer will coordinate.

These are service routes, not rankings. Confirm availability, qualifications and scope directly. Cronin may remain a suitable choice if its proposal addresses your needs.

What to bring to the first conversation

Bring your recent business and personal returns through the provider’s secure upload process, current profit and loss and balance sheets, entity ownership, payroll details, depreciation schedules and any IRS or state notices. Explain whether you want to retain your preparer. Ask who owns each recommendation and how a proposed change will reach the actual return.

AE publishes a standard $7,800 advisory engagement, payable in two $3,900 installments 30 days apart. Returns, amendments, cost segregation and additional services are separately scoped and priced. There is no required recurring annual planning fee for the standard engagement; that does not promise unlimited future work. Confirm the current proposal and signed scope.
