Incite Tax: firm overview and services

Incite Tax & Accounting serves business owners nationwide from Utah. Its public offerings include proactive tax planning, bookkeeping, Profit First cash management, CFO consulting, and IRS tax resolution.

Who might compare this firm with AE?
Owner-operated businesses seeking tax planning together with cash management, bookkeeping, and CFO guidance.
Key scope question
Will one engagement cover bookkeeping and cash management as well as tax planning, or are they separate packages?

When profit and cash visibility are the primary problem, compare the monthly financial work alongside the tax plan. A business with clean books but a major tax transaction may need a narrower engagement. Ask whether historical cleanup, management reporting, and IRS case work are included.

What to compare in a written proposal

  • Accounting work: Is bookkeeping cleanup, monthly close or management reporting included, or does the firm need clean books supplied by your team?
  • Tax plan: Will you receive a written forecast, entity and owner-pay analysis, implementation calendar and meeting cadence?
  • Returns: Which business and personal filings are included and who signs them? Are state returns and amended years additional?
  • Prior years: Who reviews basis, depreciation schedules, missing elections and carryovers, and what happens if a correction is needed?
  • Real estate: If you own rentals, who analyzes passive-loss usability and any cost segregation or Form 3115 work before assuming the deduction helps this year?

Where AE Tax fits

AE publishes a $7,800 advisory engagement and separately scopes returns, amendments and cost segregation. The starting review considers current and prior returns, books, entities and depreciation. Owners with substantial rental activity can pair business planning with property analysis. Ask for the named adviser, timelines and excluded work in the engagement letter.

If bookkeeping operations and day-to-day management reporting are the main need, make them explicit in both proposals. If a historical tax position is the main need, bring the affected returns and depreciation schedules. Compare Incite and AE on the same set of facts and complete first-year price.
