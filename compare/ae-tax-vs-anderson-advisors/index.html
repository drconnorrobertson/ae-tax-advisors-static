AE Tax Advisors vs Anderson Business Advisors

A factual, side-by-side look at two firms that serve real estate investors and business owners -- and how their approaches differ on tax planning, cost segregation, entity structuring, and overall advisory scope.

By AE Tax Advisors Team · Updated September 2026

Two Firms, Two Philosophies

Choosing the right advisory firm can shape your tax outcomes for years. Both AE Tax Advisors and Anderson Business Advisors have built strong reputations in the real estate investor community, but they approach the work from fundamentally different starting points. Understanding those differences will help you decide which firm aligns with your goals.

Anderson Business Advisors, founded by attorney Clint Coons, has become one of the most recognized names in asset protection and entity structuring. The firm has built a loyal following through educational content, real estate investor courses, and a deep focus on LLC formation, land trusts, and series LLC structures designed to shield property owners from liability. If your primary concern is structuring entities to protect your assets, Anderson has a well-earned reputation in that space.

AE Tax Advisors, headquartered in Billings, Montana, takes a different approach. Rather than leading with entity formation, AE Tax starts with the tax code itself -- identifying every available deduction, credit, and planning strategy before layering entity structuring, retirement plan design, and compliance support on top. The result is a full-spectrum advisory engagement that treats entity setup as one tool among many, not the centerpiece of the strategy.

This page compares the two firms across the categories that matter most to real estate investors and business owners. Every comparison below is based on publicly available information and our understanding of each firm's published service offerings.

Where Anderson Business Advisors Excels

Credit where it is due -- Anderson Business Advisors has built one of the strongest brands in the real estate investor education space. Clint Coons has produced hundreds of hours of educational content covering LLC structures, land trusts, series LLCs, and asset protection strategies. For investors who are just starting to build their entity framework, Anderson provides a clear, well-organized path to getting structures in place.

Anderson's focus on asset protection is genuine and deep. The firm understands the legal side of entity structuring -- how to layer LLCs, where to domicile them, and how to use land trusts to add privacy. For investors whose top priority is lawsuit protection and liability separation across a growing portfolio, Anderson offers a specialized skill set that has served thousands of clients.

The firm also deserves recognition for making complex legal and tax concepts accessible through its educational platform. Many real estate investors first learned about entity structuring and asset protection through Anderson's courses and YouTube content, and that kind of investor education raises the bar for the entire industry.

Where AE Tax Advisors Goes Deeper

The core difference between the two firms comes down to scope. Anderson leads with entity structuring and asset protection. AE Tax leads with tax savings -- then builds the entity structure, retirement plan, and compliance framework around those savings. For most real estate investors and business owners, the tax savings are where the largest dollar impact lives.

In-House Cost Segregation at $1 per Square Foot

Cost segregation is one of the most powerful tax strategies available to property owners, yet many advisory firms either skip it entirely or refer clients to third-party cost seg providers at $5,000 to $15,000 per study. AE Tax Advisors maintains a dedicated in-house cost segregation team that delivers engineered studies at $1 per square foot -- a fraction of what most providers charge. Because the cost seg team works alongside the tax advisory team, studies are tightly integrated with each client's overall tax plan. There is no gap between the study and the strategy.

100% Bonus Depreciation Under the OBBBA

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act restored 100% bonus depreciation for qualifying property, creating a significant planning opportunity for real estate investors who act on it. AE Tax Advisors has built its cost segregation and depreciation planning around current bonus depreciation rules, helping clients accelerate deductions into the current tax year. This level of depreciation expertise goes well beyond what an entity structuring firm typically offers.

Amendment Recovery -- the Three-Year Lookback

Many real estate investors have overpaid their taxes in prior years simply because their previous advisors did not apply every available strategy. AE Tax Advisors reviews up to three years of prior returns to identify missed deductions, unclaimed cost segregation, and other recoverable savings. Amendment recovery frequently puts five and six figures back into clients' pockets -- money that would otherwise remain with the IRS permanently. This is not a service Anderson Business Advisors is known to offer as part of its core model.

Real Estate Professional Status Documentation

Qualifying as a real estate professional under IRC Section 469 can unlock the ability to deduct rental losses against ordinary income -- but the documentation requirements are strict, and the IRS scrutinizes REPS claims closely. AE Tax Advisors provides hands-on guidance to help clients build and maintain the time logs, activity records, and supporting documentation needed to defend their real estate professional status in an audit. This kind of granular compliance support is a natural extension of full-spectrum tax advisory.

Retirement Plan Design

Entity structuring without retirement plan integration leaves significant tax savings on the table. AE Tax Advisors designs Solo 401(k) plans, defined benefit plans, and cash balance plans that work in coordination with a client's entity structure and income profile. The goal is to shelter as much income as possible through tax-advantaged retirement vehicles -- a strategy that compounds over decades and delivers long-term wealth building alongside short-term tax reduction.

The Bottom Line

Anderson Business Advisors and AE Tax Advisors serve overlapping client bases, but they solve different problems. Anderson is a strong choice for investors who need specialized asset protection, series LLC structures, and land trust expertise. The firm has deep roots in the real estate investor community and a proven track record in entity formation.

AE Tax Advisors is the stronger choice for investors and business owners who want to maximize their total tax savings -- not just build an entity structure. With in-house cost segregation at $1 per square foot, 100% bonus depreciation planning, amendment recovery across three years of prior returns, real estate professional status support, retirement plan design, and proactive entity structuring all bundled into a single $7,800 annual engagement, AE Tax delivers a more comprehensive advisory experience.

The best firm for you depends on your priorities. If your primary concern is asset protection and legal structuring, Anderson has earned its reputation. If you want a single advisory team that handles your entire tax picture -- from depreciation acceleration to entity optimization to retirement planning -- AE Tax Advisors is built for that.
