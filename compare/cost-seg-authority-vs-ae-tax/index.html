Choose a study scope and an owner-level scope

The current CSA Partners website describes engineering-based cost segregation, property-specific documentation, site verification, audit support, and coordination with CPA and real estate clients.

Treat these as the provider’s service descriptions. Confirm the site-verification method, retained evidence, CPA handoff, and audit-support limits in the engagement; they do not establish IRS approval or a guaranteed tax result.

For a business owner with real estate, the decision has two parts: which assets belong in shorter recovery periods, and when the owner can use the resulting deductions. A well-supported study does not, by itself, settle the owner’s passive-loss position, estimated taxes, entity coordination, or sale plan.

CSA Partners describes cost segregation work and coordination with the client’s tax advisor. Its About Us page says it does not prepare returns. AE offers owner-level advisory and separately priced study and filing services. You can keep a capable existing preparer and hire a study specialist; you do not need a broad advisory engagement merely to obtain a report.

Put the handoff in writing

  • Identify the property owner, purchase allocation, land exclusion, placed-in-service date, and improvements included in the study.
  • Require a reconciliation from the original basis and depreciation schedule to the proposed classifications. Ask how missing records and prior improvements will be handled.
  • Name who prepares any Form 3115, return, amendment, state adjustment, or partner-level work. Specify whether review and follow-up questions are included.
  • Agree on the delivery date relative to the actual filing deadline and extension. A report delivery target is not a filed-return commitment.
  • Describe audit support, response time, representation, and additional charges in the proposal rather than assuming a website description covers every dispute.

Check the benefit before authorizing the study

Give your preparer the expected holding period, planned renovations, ownership structure, prior depreciation, and income picture. Ask for an estimate of deductions usable this year and any suspended amounts, then consider study and implementation fees plus future sale tax. Do not treat the study’s gross deduction as a dollar-for-dollar tax saving.

Review sale timing and depreciation after a study and compare study evidence and engineering scope.

Frequently asked questions

Is Cost Segregation Authority the same business as CSA Partners?

CSA Partners’ terms identify Cost Segregation Authority, LLC as the business operating under the CSA Partners name. This comparison uses both names so buyers can match the provider to the engagement letter.

Does CSA Partners prepare my tax return?

Its About Us page says it does not prepare tax returns. Its cost segregation page describes coordination with your CPA or tax advisor. Confirm who prepares, reviews, and files each return or Form 3115; support for implementation is not the same as taking responsibility for the filing.

Is the $3,000 starting study fee comparable with AE’s $7,800 advisory fee?

They purchase different scopes. CSA’s published starting fee is for a cost segregation study, while AE’s $7,800 is its standard advisory engagement. Add any separately quoted study, filing, amendments, and owner-level planning before comparing matching proposals.

Can a profitable business owner always use rental depreciation against business income?

No. Basis, at-risk, passive activity, and other applicable loss limitations can delay or restrict use. The owner’s participation, rental facts, and other income need review before treating a projected depreciation deduction as current cash savings.

Does an older property always require Form 3115?

No. The correct procedure depends on the depreciation history, type of correction, applicable accounting-method rules, and current filing requirements. A preparer should determine whether Form 3115 and a section 481(a) adjustment, an amended return, or another permitted procedure applies.
