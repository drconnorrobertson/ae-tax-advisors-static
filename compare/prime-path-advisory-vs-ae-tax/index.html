Prime Path Advisory vs AE Tax Advisors: Scope and Fit

Published by AE Tax Advisors, a competing provider. Not an independent rating, endorsement or verification of outcomes. Prime Path service facts reviewed November 6, 2026; the budget estimate retains its original October 24, 2026 review date.

Pricing and feesAlternativesCompare with AE

Choose between these two firms by the decision you need resolved and the work each proposal commits to deliver. An executive with equity compensation and an owner with operating entities and rental property may need overlapping expertise, but not the same engagement.

Prime Path baseline: published services and engagement limits

Prime Path’s homepage advertises year-round planning for founders, executives and high-RSU earners, with $1M+ positioning. Treat that as its advertised audience and confirm current intake criteria, not an eligibility rule established by this comparison. Its process page describes diagnostic review, strategy, implementation and ongoing review.

The team page describes internal accountants and access to an outside network of attorneys, financial advisers and wealth strategists. It identifies a senior tax adviser whose biography includes amended-return experience. This supports asking about prior-year work rather than assuming it is unavailable; it does not establish that amendments or property studies are included in your proposal.

The FAQ says tax preparation is offered and many clients keep their CPA for filing. Ask which returns and implementation tasks are actually included. The disclosures identify Prime Path Advisory, Inc. as a California corporation and state that it is not a law firm or investment adviser. Audit-support scope, duration, eligibility and exclusions depend on the engagement; protection is not a promise of no examination or a favorable outcome.

Prime Path service facts reviewed November 6, 2026. This review uses published identity and descriptions; it does not independently verify licenses, client results or suitability. Public marketing and a professional biography do not substitute for your signed scope.

Put the same assignment in both proposals

Use the tax advisor engagement scorecard or download the blank CSV worksheet. A blank answer is a question to resolve, not evidence that a firm lacks the service.

  • Name the lead professional and who reviews technical conclusions.
  • List the income, entities, properties and prior years covered.
  • Specify the written plan, assumptions, implementation steps and deadlines.
  • Assign elections, payroll, studies, amendments and every affected return.
  • Identify audit-support limits and any separate legal or investment relationship.
  • Compare total first-year cost, future commitments and excluded work.

A qualified planning example

Illustrative situation: an owner has practice income, rental property and an existing return preparer. Ask each firm how it would connect owner compensation, rental-loss eligibility, any property study and the affected filings. Require records, assumptions, an implementation owner and dates before relying on a projected deduction.

This is a proposal-testing scenario, not a client result or evidence of what Prime Path would recommend. A deduction can be deferred or limited, and a written plan does not prove an election was completed or a tax saving achieved.

Frequently asked questions

Which firm is better for my situation?

Compare actual income sources, entities, property and desired support with written proposals. Prime Path advertises a high-income executive and founder focus; AE emphasizes business-owner and real-estate planning. Neither is universally better.

Is Prime Path tax preparation automatically included?

No inclusion should be assumed. Its FAQ offers preparation and says many clients retain their CPA. Confirm the specific returns, signer, price and deadlines in the engagement.

Does Prime Path handle amended returns?

Its senior adviser biography describes amended-return experience. Ask which years, filings, review and implementation are included in your proposal and whether any work is separately priced.

What does this page verify about Prime Path?

It summarizes published identity, service descriptions and disclosures. It does not independently verify professional licenses, client outcomes or the suitability of a strategy for you.

Discuss the scope of an AE engagement. Obtain a signed scope and current fees before committing.
