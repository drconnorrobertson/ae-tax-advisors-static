Tax Strategists of America vs AE Tax Advisors: Implementation Handoff

Published by AE Tax Advisors, a competing provider. Service sources reviewed November 6, 2026. This is not an independent ranking, credential verification or audit of client outcomes. Existing estimate and benchmark dates remain as labeled.

If you already have a return preparer, the key buying decision is whether a planning engagement produces work the preparer can use and accepts responsibility for unresolved questions. Compare the handoff, not the number of strategies advertised.

What Tax Strategists of America describes

The official services page describes initial information gathering, monthly updates, implementation documentation and a year-end summary for the client’s accountant. It also advertises audit defense for its work. These are published descriptions, not verification of completed client work, savings or examination results. Confirm scope, timing and limitations in the agreement.

Require a preparer-handoff acceptance checklist

Acceptance itemEvidence to request before filing
Facts reconciledIncome, entity ownership, payroll, property use and payment records agree with the books and returns.
Strategy supportedWritten eligibility analysis, assumptions, supporting contracts and required records are supplied.
Action completedSigned documents, payment evidence and election confirmations show what actually happened and when.
Return treatment mappedPreparer knows the entity, year, schedule and amount affected, with any state difference identified.
Open issues assignedA named person answers questions; disagreements and missing records are resolved before signature.
Retention and examination supportSpecify who retains records and responds to which notices, for how long and at what cost.

A short year-end summary can organize the handoff, but does not replace technical support or the preparer’s review. Do not assume a fixed brief review time or universal three-year retention period is appropriate for every document. Property basis and carryforward records can matter much later.

How to put AE on the same comparison

Ask AE to quote the same covered entities, years and property questions. Identify whether AE or your existing preparer will implement each item, prepare returns or handle amendments. The scope should name separately priced studies, filings and additional work. Neither firm’s marketing establishes that the other lacks a capability.

AE publishes $7,800 Strategic Tax Advisory and $9,800 Complex Tax Advisory. Returns, amendments, cost segregation and additional services are separately scoped and priced. The standard advisory engagement has no required annual planning renewal; it does not include unlimited future work or free annual returns. See current AE pricing.

Illustrative owner-and-property handoff

An owner plans a property improvement while changing payroll and retirement funding. Request separate evidence for asset service dates, compensation and plan deadlines. The accountant must then reconcile all three to the business and personal returns. This is an evaluation scenario, not a prediction of savings or a claim about either firm’s client results.

Ask each firm for a redacted sample deliverable and its escalation process when the return preparer needs clarification. Resolve that workflow before paying for recommendations you cannot implement.

Compare written proposals using the engagement scorecard and blank CSV worksheet. Request a signed scope identifying the reviewer, implementation owner, covered years and entities, fees, deadlines and excluded work.

Frequently asked questions

Does a year-end summary replace preparer review?

No. The preparer still needs the facts, support, completed-action evidence and a defensible return treatment. A concise summary is a handoff tool, not automatic acceptance.

What should I confirm about audit defense?

Ask which work and years are covered, who represents you, the authorization required, limits and exclusions, and any additional fees. Advertising audit defense does not guarantee a favorable result.

How should I compare AE with Tax Strategists of America?

Request the same written deliverables, supporting records, implementation owners and filing responsibilities. Compare the total fee and ongoing commitments for that assignment.

Discuss a scoped AE engagement. Confirm written deliverables and fees before committing.
