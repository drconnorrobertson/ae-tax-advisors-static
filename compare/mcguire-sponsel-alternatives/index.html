When to keep McGuire Sponsel on your shortlist

Keep McGuire Sponsel on the shortlist when you need a specialty team working alongside your CPA, especially for a building purchase, construction project, renovation or substantial asset schedule. Its published approach combines engineering and tax expertise.

Read the provider’s own description. The listed prospect criteria describe typical opportunities, not guaranteed eligibility or tax benefits. Confirm the scope of any related fixed-asset or incentive work rather than assuming it is included in the study.

A situation worth comparing

Suppose you bought a manufacturing building with a long fixed-asset ledger and several prior renovations. Ask each bidder to explain the site investigation, reconciliation of improvements and report package for your existing CPA.

Choose the firm whose investigation, asset reconciliation and accountant handoff fit that project. A custom site-inspected study should be evaluated against another matched engineering proposal.

Compare total cost before switching

McGuire Sponsel pricing and quote checklist. A software report, an engineering study and a tax advisory engagement buy different work. Compare inspection method, report detail, travel, CPA questions, support and any separately priced Form 3115 or return work.

Request a property-specific study quote and identify the report deliverables, preparer handoff and audit-support terms. Confirm any Form 3115 preparation and return filing separately; a study fee is not an all-inclusive tax engagement.

Ask for a proposal you can compare

  • Does the fee include a site inspection for every building in this engagement?
  • How will existing asset records and renovation costs be reconciled?
  • Which schedules, draft findings and CPA responses are delivered?
  • Are method-change preparation, return work or related incentive projects separately priced?

Give each firm the same property address, square footage, closing statement, placed-in-service date, improvement records and existing depreciation schedules. Identify the owner entity, return preparer and deadline. Request a written scope for inspection, engineering analysis, report schedules, support and separately billed implementation. Send sensitive records through the firm’s secure intake process.
