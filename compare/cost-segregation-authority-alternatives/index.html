Choose the missing service, then choose the provider

Start with the work your current team cannot cover. If your CPA has already tested deduction usability and can implement a study, a specialist engagement may be enough. If no one owns the broader business-and-property plan, a report alone will leave that gap open.

Three different buying paths

1. Renovation evidence and tangible-property work: Capstan

Capstan describes pre-renovation cost segregation, unit-of-property designations, and tangible-property analysis. Consider this path when you need to preserve information about assets being removed and distinguish the old assets from replacement costs. Ask which of those deliverables are included, what records are needed before demolition, and who makes the tax elections.

Capstan’s renovation discussion · Capstan’s unit-of-property service · Compare the renovation scope with AE

2. A study alongside other building incentives: Engineered Tax Services

ETS describes cost segregation and related incentives including 179D and 45L. Consider this path when a building project warrants a separate eligibility review, engineering or certification work, and coordinated basis records. Ask for distinct fees and responsibilities rather than assuming every listed service is included or still available for a new project.

3. Business and real estate tax coordination: AE Tax Advisors

Consider AE when the unresolved question is whether and when property deductions improve the owner’s overall tax plan. Advisory, the property study, and filing are separate scopes. You can ask about coordinating with an existing study provider or preparer without assuming AE replaces every member of the team.

Ask every candidate the same five questions

  • What exact property and decisions does this engagement cover, and what deliverable answers each question?
  • Which records must be collected before construction, removal of assets, or the filing deadline?
  • Who evaluates deduction limits and current eligibility, and who reconciles report findings to the existing tax schedules?
  • Who prepares and files each form, handles follow-up questions, and maintains the asset records after delivery?
  • What is the complete price for the matching scope, including optional work, exclusions, and future commitments?

Screen current eligibility before buying adjacent work

Keep the property study decision separate from energy-incentive eligibility. IRS guidance says 179D does not apply to property whose construction begins after June 30, 2026, and 45L does not apply to qualified new homes acquired after June 30, 2026. Earlier qualifying projects may still warrant review. Neither cutoff is a general deadline for obtaining a cost segregation study.

For prior-year depreciation, the return preparer should choose the applicable correction or accounting-method procedure. A provider’s general discussion of catch-up deductions does not establish Form 3115 eligibility for your facts.

Frequently asked questions

Which alternative should I consider if my CPA already handles my tax plan?

Consider a study specialist with a clear report and implementation handoff. CSA may still fit, and Capstan or Engineered Tax Services can be candidates based on the additional work your property needs. A change of advisor is not required solely to buy a study.

When should Capstan be on the shortlist?

Consider Capstan when a planned renovation makes pre-removal asset records, unit-of-property designations, and tangible-property analysis important. Confirm the quoted deliverables and have the preparer decide repairs, capitalization, and disposition treatment.

When should Engineered Tax Services be on the shortlist?

Consider ETS when you want to evaluate a cost segregation study alongside separately scoped building incentives. Confirm eligibility, evidence, fees, and filing responsibilities for each service. A menu of incentives does not establish that every project qualifies.

When is AE’s advisory engagement relevant?

AE’s advisory work is relevant when the owner needs a coordinated plan across profitable business operations, real estate, deduction limits, and implementation. The standard engagement is $7,800, with studies, returns, amendments, and additional services separately priced.

Does AE require an annual planning renewal or include all tax work?

The standard $7,800 advisory engagement has no required recurring annual planning fee and can be paid in two $3,900 installments, 30 days apart. It does not include unlimited future planning or free annual filing. Studies, returns, amendments, and additional services need their own scope.
