A situation worth comparing

Suppose you own several rental buildings and want reports delivered to your CPA ahead of the return deadline. You need separate fees and schedules for each property, a clear inspection plan and a way for your preparer to resolve report questions.

Consider CSA Partners when its engineering and CPA-coordination scope fits the portfolio. Consider AE when its standalone study quote and separately agreed implementation meet your needs. Ask for property-level schedules and a documented handoff rather than relying on a headline minimum.

Compare total cost before switching

Cost Segregation Authority pricing and quote checklist. A software report, an engineering study and a tax advisory engagement buy different work. Compare inspection method, report detail, travel, CPA questions, support and any separately priced Form 3115 or return work.

Request a property-specific study quote and identify the report deliverables, preparer handoff and audit-support terms. Confirm any Form 3115 preparation and return filing separately; a study fee is not an all-inclusive tax engagement.

Ask for a proposal you can compare

  • Who handles each CPA question and any method-change or return work?
  • What are the duration, limits and cost of audit support and other follow-up?
