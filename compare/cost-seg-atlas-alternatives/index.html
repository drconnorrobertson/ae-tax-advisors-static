When to keep Cost Seg Atlas on your shortlist

Keep Cost Seg Atlas on the shortlist if you want to evaluate an evidence-linked analysis and export workflow with a qualified preparer. Confirm how uploaded records, photographs, methodology choices and gaps will be investigated before a report is used.

Read the provider’s own description. The pricing page describes educational analyses and says a qualified preparer must complete remaining work before a filing position is taken. Professional review is marked paused; verify availability directly rather than assuming a future review service can be purchased now.

A situation worth comparing

Suppose you generated an analysis for a renovated rental but the completeness report lists unresolved items. Ask your preparer what evidence, investigation and sign-off remain, then compare the full cost with an engaged study provider.

Choose based on the completed evidence and professional responsibilities, not solely the download price or an automated completeness score. A score does not establish a tax filing conclusion.

Compare total cost before switching

Cost Seg Atlas pricing and quote checklist. A software report, an engineering study and a tax advisory engagement buy different work. Compare inspection method, report detail, travel, CPA questions, support and any separately priced Form 3115 or return work.

Request a property-specific study quote and identify the report deliverables, preparer handoff and audit-support terms. Confirm any Form 3115 preparation and return filing separately; a study fee is not an all-inclusive tax engagement.

Ask for a proposal you can compare

  • Is independent professional review available now, and who signs the final work?
  • What property evidence and unresolved gaps require additional investigation?
  • What does the $395 product include versus separately engaged professional work?
  • Who prepares any Form 3115 and evaluates how the report is used on the return?

Give each firm the same property address, square footage, closing statement, placed-in-service date, improvement records and existing depreciation schedules. Identify the owner entity, return preparer and deadline. Request a written scope for inspection, engineering analysis, report schedules, support and separately billed implementation. Send sensitive records through the firm’s secure intake process.
