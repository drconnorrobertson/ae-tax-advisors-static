What Rainwater CPA publicly describes

Rainwater CPA’s tax-planning page describes formal customized plans, implementation guidance and different meeting schedules for business owners and high-net-worth individuals. Business-owner planning includes quarterly meetings; its individual offering describes a dedicated annual meeting with ongoing support. An owner with operating companies and rentals should ask which engagement and cadence apply.

Its compliance page lists business and individual preparation. Its homepage describes coordinating cost segregation studies with engineering providers and incorporating results into projections. These descriptions do not establish that every study, form, return or owner-level analysis is included in a particular quote.

Rainwater also advertises exit planning and tax resolution. Identify a sale or IRS matter separately from ordinary annual planning and preparation.

What to establish in an AE proposal

AE’s engagement can address business income, real estate, prior returns and implementation responsibilities. Specify the entities, owners, properties, states and tax years involved. A lookback review, study, Form 3115, amendment and annual return are different deliverables; the advisory fee is not an all-inclusive price for them.

Use the preserved pricing information and AE’s published pricing as context, then confirm the written scope. Ask who prepares each filing, maintains supporting schedules and coordinates with your existing CPA or bookkeeper.

Compare five decisions before signing

  • Meeting cadence: Rainwater publishes quarterly business-owner meetings and a separate annual-meeting model for high-net-worth individuals. Ask both firms how acquisitions, sales and compensation changes are handled between meetings.
  • Projection inputs: Identify who supplies current books, payroll, rental results, K-1 estimates and state information, and who resolves missing records.
  • Property-study handoff: Identify the engineering provider, report recipient, asset-schedule format and responsible preparer. Confirm Form 3115 eligibility review, preparation and filing separately.
  • Entity and owner returns: List each business, individual, trust and state return included. Clarify K-1 delivery, owner basis, passive-loss carryforwards and corrections affecting later years.
  • Total commitment: Separate planning, recurring advisory, preparation, bookkeeping cleanup, studies, amendments and representation. Compare cancellation, renewal and additional-work terms.

Example: an operating-company owner buys another rental

Consider an owner with an S corporation, a jointly owned rental LLC and a planned acquisition. This is a proposal scenario, not a client outcome. Ask each firm how updated business profit, payroll, purchase records and the property’s service date reach the projection.

If a study is proposed, identify who orders it, reconciles basis to closing records, checks elections and evaluates whether deductions are currently usable. Then identify who updates entity and owner returns. An estimated study deduction is not the same as an owner’s cash-tax reduction.

Questions to send with the same facts to both firms

  1. Which service and meeting schedule apply to an owner with operating businesses and rentals?
  2. What triggers a projection update outside scheduled meetings, and is it additional work?
  3. Who performs the study, and who prepares and files any Form 3115?
  4. Who reviews owner basis, at-risk amounts, passive losses and state differences?
  5. Which entity and household returns are included, and when are K-1s handed off?
  6. Are earlier returns reviewed, and how are corrections separately approved and priced?
  7. What records, written deliverables and implementation deadlines will the proposal specify?

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Rainwater CPA work with business owners who also own rentals?

Rainwater’s public materials describe business-owner planning, services for high-net-worth individuals and cost segregation coordination with engineering providers. Ask which engagement covers your operating companies, rentals and household returns.

How often does Rainwater CPA meet with planning clients?

Its planning page describes quarterly meetings for business owners and a dedicated annual meeting with ongoing support for high-net-worth individuals. Confirm which model applies to your facts and how decisions between meetings are handled.

Is cost segregation coordination exclusive to AE Tax Advisors?

No. Rainwater’s website also describes coordinating engineering studies and integrating results into projections. Compare who performs the study, applies it to the returns, evaluates owner-level limitations and handles any Form 3115.

Does a planning fee include every return and property study?

Do not assume it does. Request a written list of included entities, owners, states, returns, studies and implementation work. AE’s returns, amendments, cost segregation and additional services are separately scoped and priced.

How should I compare Rainwater CPA’s fees with AE’s?
