Three alternatives for a general business and property shortlist

AE Tax Advisors: start with the business-and-property decision

Interview AE when you want a business or real estate planning engagement. Its public pages describe those service areas. AE business planning, AE property planning.

Give the team a specific brief: the purchase, ownership change, sale or planning question you need resolved. Ask for a proposed deliverable and the additional work needed to carry it out.

WCG CPAs & Advisors: compare the recurring business workflow

WCG advertises tax planning alongside accounting, payroll and business advisory. WCG practice.

If your reason for looking includes monthly reporting or payroll coordination, request a quote that identifies those tasks separately from annual filing and planning. Ask how the team escalates a financial change that affects your projection.

Hall CPA: compare a property-focused practice

Hall CPA advertises tax advisory, accounting, preparation and cost segregation for real estate investors. Hall CPA services.

If your portfolio is the complex part, ask how the engagement covers historical property records, recurring reporting and owner-level coordination. Include every entity and property in the same interview brief.

What if your priority is founder equity or a planned exit?

You can also interview P4 Tax & Consulting, which publishes founder, QSBS and capital-gains planning services. P4 planning. Ask for the relevant transaction experience of the professional assigned to you and the documents that need review before signing.

These are possible interview fits. They are not findings that Delerme lacks these capabilities.

Keep specialized relocation work on a separate shortlist

If Puerto Rico relocation is your main goal, request a specialist proposal that addresses the relevant jurisdictions and ongoing responsibilities. This general shortlist does not verify any listed alternative as a substitute for Delerme’s Act 60 offering. A mainland planning engagement and a relocation project should not be treated as equivalent purchases.

Decide whether to switch or add a specialist

Write one sentence explaining the gap: “I need a documented review before buying another property,” or “I need monthly reporting that feeds a reliable tax projection.” Then ask your current provider and each alternative to scope that same work.

Compare the response, assigned professional, deadline and complete price. If the gap can be solved through a limited project, evaluate that before transferring the whole relationship.

A decision guide for comparing Delerme CPA with AE

Delerme CPA: public services and the decision to compare

Delerme CPA's public materials describe accounting and tax advisory services for business owners. This is relevant to entrepreneurs considering planning and accounting support with a defined service scope. Review the official service description. Your written proposal determines the actual scope and current fees.

Business accounting and owner planning

An operating business needs usable records before its owner can make informed tax decisions. Ask whether the engagement starts with books that are already current or includes cleanup. Prepare a year-to-date profit-and-loss statement, balance sheet, entity chart, payroll information, and major planned transactions. The proposal should explain how business results will be connected with the owner’s household forecast.

Recurring bookkeeping, payroll, tax preparation, and a written planning engagement serve different purposes. Compare the work you actually need rather than assuming that any accounting package includes all four. Ask which meetings result in updated projections and which implementation steps remain with your payroll provider, attorney, bookkeeper, or return preparer.

A practical comparison for your situation

Your business is changing markets or locations and you need to compare ongoing accounting support with a discrete tax-planning project.

Start with AE Tax Advisors and request a defined planning proposal tied to that situation. Then compare Delerme CPA's proposal for the same facts. Ask which jurisdiction-specific work requires its own specialist and whether implementation and filings are included.

The question to resolve before choosing

Who reviews the state and jurisdiction consequences before a move?

Ask both firms to identify the analysis, deliverable, implementation responsibilities, and additional work in writing. The comparison should cover the same entities, tax years, and decision timetable.

Evaluate AE's services and current pricing and book a call with AE. We recommend AE first for a focused planning project that fits your needs. Additional accounting, filing, legal, wealth, or specialist work should have an explicit scope.
