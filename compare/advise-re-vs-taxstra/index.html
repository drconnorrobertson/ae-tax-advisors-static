Advise RE vs Taxstra: Property Purchase and Owner Tax Decisions

Published by AE Tax Advisors, a competing provider. Service sources reviewed November 6, 2026. This is not an independent ranking, credential verification or audit of client outcomes. Existing estimate and benchmark dates remain as labeled.

Start with the decision that must be made before you sign a purchase agreement, begin construction or change a practice’s finances. The two firms advertise overlapping tax services with different emphases; neither description proves the other cannot handle your situation.

Scenario 1: acquisition or development before closing

Advise RE’s official site emphasizes property investors and developers, with buyer advisory, construction advisory, tax planning and preparation. Its buyer offering describes underwriting, structure and tax modeled before closing; construction advisory describes feasibility and budgets. Ask whether the proposal covers your acquisition, geography and project stage.

For a property purchase or renovation, request a written comparison of ownership structure, land/building allocation, planned improvements, financing and exit assumptions. Clarify whether the work includes underwriting or construction review, rather than assuming those services are bundled with a tax return.

Scenario 2: physician or business income plus rentals

Taxstra’s official site lists physician, business-owner and real-estate investor services alongside tax planning, preparation, accounting and payroll. Investigate this emphasis when a rental purchase must be coordinated with practice income, wages, owner compensation, estimated payments and household cash needs.

Ask for a projection showing the effect of the purchase across the relevant entities and household. Identify who checks participation records and loss limitations, updates payroll or estimates, and handles property depreciation and filings. A large property deduction does not automatically offset clinical wages or operating profit.

Choose a deliverable, not a label

For a time-sensitive acquisition, prioritize a defined pre-closing deliverable and availability. For recurring practice-and-rental coordination, prioritize the projection cadence and preparer handoff. If both needs apply, ask whether one engagement can cover them or separate specialist work is required. These scenarios are illustrative and are not verified client outcomes.

Fees and commitments

No current numeric fee for either firm is established here. Request dated proposals covering the same properties, entities and time period. Separate acquisition advice, construction review, planning, bookkeeping, payroll, returns and any specialist study. Compare first-year work, renewal terms and cancellation provisions. Public service listings do not prove inclusion in an individual engagement.

Where AE belongs

AE is another option to investigate for business-owner and rental-property tax coordination. Ask for the same decision-specific scope and retain any legal, transaction or construction specialist the facts require. This comparison does not represent that AE provides every service described above.

AE advisory fees are separate from filing and study work

AE’s standard advisory engagement is $7,800, payable in two $3,900 installments 30 days apart, with no required recurring annual planning fee. Returns, amendments, cost segregation and additional services are separately scoped and priced. No required renewal does not mean unlimited future planning or free annual filing. Confirm the actual written deliverables and duration. Review current AE pricing.

Compare written proposals using the engagement scorecard and blank CSV worksheet. Request a signed scope identifying the reviewer, implementation owner, covered years and entities, fees, deadlines and excluded work.

Frequently asked questions

Which firm should I consider before buying property?

Ask both for a pre-closing proposal. Advise RE publicly emphasizes buyer and construction advisory; Taxstra lists real-estate planning alongside physician and business services. Select on the assigned deliverable and availability.

Does Taxstra’s physician focus mean it cannot advise developers?

No. A stated emphasis is not evidence that another service is unavailable. Ask directly about your project, assigned professional and scope.

Does Advise RE’s property focus replace household tax planning?

Do not assume the proposal covers every household or operating-business issue. Specify all income sources, entities and required projections in the written scope.

Discuss a scoped AE engagement. Confirm written deliverables and fees before committing.
