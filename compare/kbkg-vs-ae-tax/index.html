Which proposal fits this situation?

You want to compare a specialty-tax engagement with a household or business-wide planning engagement.

Compare professional services with professional services and software with software. The lowest software price does not establish the price of KBKG’s full-service engineering work.

Ask what technical deliverables the quote includes and whether broader planning is separately available.

Separate the study from advisory and filing

AE’s published standalone study rate is $1 per square foot with a $2,000 minimum. At that rate, a 1,500-square-foot study has a $2,000 base price; a 3,000-square-foot study has a $3,000 base price. These are arithmetic examples before separately scoped advisory, filing, amendments, or other work.

Check AE’s current study and service prices. Add the corresponding work to the other proposal before comparing totals.
