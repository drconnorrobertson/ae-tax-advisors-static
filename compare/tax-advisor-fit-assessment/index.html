  • Compare the complete first-year and recurring price
  • Ask for a sample deliverable and references from clients with similar facts

Why Fit Matters More Than a Generic Ranking

Proactive tax firms often solve different problems despite similar language. A firm designed for executive equity compensation is not automatically the best firm for a real-estate operator. A CFO-oriented accounting relationship is different from a corrective tax engagement. A private-client model may include services an owner does not need.

The strongest shortlist begins with the taxpayer's facts and required work product, then tests delivery quality, credentials, and price.
