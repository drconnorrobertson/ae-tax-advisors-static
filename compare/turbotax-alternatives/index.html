TurboTax Alternatives: DIY, Expert Help and Prepared Returns

TurboTax alternatives should match how you want to file: prepare the return yourself, get help while you prepare it, or hire someone to prepare it. TurboTax itself now offers all three routes, so first identify what you want to change. FreeTaxUSA and H&R Block are filing options to investigate; a local credentialed preparer or a separately scoped planning engagement may address different needs.

FreeTaxUSA: a DIY filing option with separately priced support

FreeTaxUSA publishes free federal individual filing, a separate state-return charge and optional support tiers. Verify that it supports your required forms and the year you need. A low filing price does not mean a professional prepares the return or provides a customized business-planning engagement.

H&R Block: online filing and professional preparation routes

H&R Block offers online filing and professional preparation options. Compare the exact route, not just the brand. For a business return, review the business-services scope and confirm the entity type, state filings and support included. Its retail and online products are not one interchangeable package.

A credentialed local preparer: compare the actual engagement

If you want a person responsible for preparing your return, interview a CPA, enrolled agent or other appropriately qualified preparer about the forms and states involved. Verify credentials and who signs the return. Request an itemized quote covering records cleanup, preparation, amendments and notice help rather than assuming all are included.

AE Tax Advisors: planning is a separate purchase

AE is a candidate when the central issue is a business-owner or real-estate planning decision with a defined analysis and implementation scope. That engagement is not a like-for-like substitute for a DIY individual-return subscription. Annual returns, amendments and additional work are separately priced. A routine filing need may call for a narrower service.

Compare against the TurboTax route you would actually use

TurboTax’s current materials offer DIY, expert-assisted and prepared-return options, as well as business-related services. This page does not assume TurboTax is only self-service software or that its experts cannot address a particular issue. Identify the available product and request its exact scope before replacing it.

Use the same tax-return facts for every quote

  • List the tax year, federal forms, states, rental activity and self-employment information.
  • Separate software access, review assistance, return preparation and forward-looking planning.
  • Include state charges, support upgrades and payment-related fees in the total.
  • Confirm who corrects errors, handles notices and retains access to the completed return.
  • Do not choose from a maximum-refund promise or an unsourced customer ranking.
