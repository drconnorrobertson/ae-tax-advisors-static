When AE deserves consideration

Consider AE if you want a defined advisory engagement evaluating your business and owner-level tax position, with any preparation, amendments or property studies identified separately. Ask for individualized assumptions, substantiation requirements, deliverables and execution fees for each proposed strategy.

The other firm may fit better when its relevant specialty, local access or ongoing accounting arrangement matches the work you need. Confirm specialty services directly with either provider; this comparison does not imply identical capabilities.

AE publishes standard advisory pricing of $7,800, payable in two $3,900 installments 30 days apart. Returns, amendments, cost segregation and additional services are separately scoped and priced.
