Seneca Alternatives: Commission the Study and Tax Handoff

Published by AE Tax Advisors, a competing provider. Service sources reviewed November 6, 2026. This is not an independent ranking, credential verification or audit of client outcomes. Existing estimate and benchmark dates remain as labeled.

Begin by deciding what you are commissioning: a property study for your existing preparer, a study plus a separately assigned implementation engagement, or a broader owner-and-property tax review. Those are different purchases. Compare the study deliverables before comparing advisory packages.

Seneca’s published study scope

Seneca’s official site describes engineering analysis, a PDF report and a fixed-asset schedule. It describes liaison with the client’s CPA and virtual or on-site property tours depending on circumstances. Its audit-support language concerns questions about its study and report; the published refund condition is tied to a Seneca-caused issue producing a depreciation adjustment greater than 5%. Obtain current written terms and exclusions.

Those offerings support a study commissioning discussion. They do not by themselves establish that owner-level loss analysis, Form 3115, amended returns or complete tax-examination representation are included. Ask who handles each task rather than treating unconfirmed work as unavailable.

Ask for these study acceptance items

  • Property-specific scope and agreed delivery date.
  • Land exclusion and depreciable-basis reconciliation to closing and improvement records.
  • Inspection method, engineer/reviewer responsibilities and supporting documentation.
  • Report and fixed-asset schedule your preparer can reconcile and use.
  • Process for questions, corrections and examination requests about the study.

Assign the tax work before the report arrives

Your return professional should review acquisition and service dates, existing depreciation, elections, state differences and applicable loss limits. A study can accelerate deductions without producing an immediately usable tax benefit. Decide who determines whether an accounting-method change is appropriate and who prepares Form 3115 or any affected return. Study support is not automatically representation for unrelated owner-return issues.

The IRS cost-segregation guide discusses study issues; it does not endorse a particular provider. Marketing estimates are not a determination of your eligible deduction or savings.

Bring one property brief to every bidder

Include acquisition and service dates, property use, closing statement, improvement invoices, prior depreciation, ownership and planned holding period. Ask each bidder to identify missing records and estimate assumptions. Request a current study quote; the AE advisory fee below is not a Seneca quote or an AE study price.

Review AE’s cost-segregation service scope and confirm the current study, advisory and filing proposals separately.

Compare written proposals using the engagement scorecard and blank CSV worksheet. Request a signed scope identifying the reviewer, implementation owner, covered years and entities, fees, deadlines and excluded work.

Frequently asked questions

What is an alternative to a Seneca study?

Compare another study proposal, a study paired with your preparer, or a broader advisory engagement such as AE. The right comparison depends on whether you need only the report or also owner-level analysis and implementation.

Does Seneca’s report include my Form 3115?

The reviewed site describes a report, fixed-asset schedule and CPA liaison. Ask explicitly who prepares Form 3115 and affected returns; do not assume those tasks are included or unavailable.

Is the AE $7,800 advisory fee a study price?

No. It is the standard advisory fee. Cost-segregation studies, returns, amendments and additional services are separately scoped and priced.

Does study audit support cover my entire tax return?

Do not assume that. Confirm support for the study, owner-level representation, years covered, conditions and exclusions separately in writing.

Discuss a scoped AE engagement. Confirm written deliverables and fees before committing.
