A specific owner scenario

A small packaged-goods brand is growing wholesale orders while inventory absorbs cash. The owner wants tax planning but also needs reliable product margins and lender-ready records. These assignments should be priced and scheduled separately.

Illustrative scenario, not a reported client result. Send the same background and target decision date to both firms so you can compare proposed work on equal terms.

The issue to resolve before choosing

A business can report earnings while struggling to fund inventory. Ask the provider to separate inventory accounting, operating cash forecasting and tax projections so each decision uses the right report.

  • Who reconciles inventory and product cost?
  • Which reports are prepared for lenders or investors?
  • What is the current contracting and service arrangement?

Inventory accounting and planning-quality financials

A product business needs financial records that reflect inventory movements, supplier arrangements, and distributor deductions. Before asking for a planning model, identify whether the accounting records reconcile those items consistently. Explain any major purchasing or distribution changes. Ask what accounting cleanup is required and which reports the tax adviser will receive.

Compare the recurring accounting scope with the additional planning deliverables. Ask who coordinates inventory information with the preparer and when reliable year-end estimates will be available. A proposal for business bookkeeping can address different work from a personal owner planning engagement. Make the connection explicit so both teams use consistent financial information.

A practical comparison for your situation

Your product brand has distributor deductions, co-packer payments, and inventory changes that complicate a usable profit forecast.

Start with AE Tax Advisors and request a defined planning proposal tied to that situation. Then compare Accountfully's proposal for the same facts. Ask how operational accounting reconciles those items before the tax adviser models planning options.
