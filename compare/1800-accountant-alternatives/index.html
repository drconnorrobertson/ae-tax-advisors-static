1-800Accountant Alternatives: Advisory, Filing and Bookkeeping

Compare alternatives to 1-800Accountant by advisory access, tax filing, bookkeeping and handover requirements. Match the proposal to the work you need.

Book a Call

Start with the part of the service you want to change

An alternative to 1-800Accountant could be a different recurring accounting service, a separate tax preparer, or a defined advisory assignment added to your current team. Those are different decisions. Write down the unresolved task before comparing names: a late month-end close, an unanswered planning question, an additional entity, or a property decision that needs specialist analysis.

The official packages distinguish Tax Advisory, Starter and Business Complete. Advisory access, filing and full-service bookkeeping should therefore be compared separately. A lower price is useful only when the proposal covers the required work.

Use a shortlist organized by deliverable

Alternative arrangements to evaluate
NeedArrangement to considerEvidence to request

Pilot accounting alternatives provide another way to frame a recurring company-finance purchase. AE’s advisory pricing describes a defined competing engagement. These are interview starting points, not endorsements or an independent ranking.

Ask the current provider before arranging a replacement

1-800Accountant’s advisory page describes a dedicated advisor and year-round planning. If the concern is access or an unanswered question, ask which person owns the file, how requests are scheduled and what happens between quarterly reviews. A revised scope may address the problem without moving records. If the answer remains unclear, ask replacement firms the same questions.

For a fictional owner adding a second entity and a rental property, the first task is to map the new returns, books and planning questions. Do not treat the one-entity Starter description as coverage for the entire structure, and do not assume that another provider’s advisory proposal includes all filings.

Plan the handover before cancelling

  • Agree which firm completes each open return and extension.
  • Export the ledger, reconciliations, fixed-asset schedules and supporting workpapers through secure channels.
  • Assign responsibility for payroll, estimated payments and incoming notices.
  • Confirm access end dates and the incoming team’s acceptance of the records.
  • Price transition overlap and cleanup separately from the recurring fee.

Choose the proposal that documents the required work, responsible people and complete cost. Public descriptions do not establish whether an unlisted service is available; ask directly before drawing a conclusion.
