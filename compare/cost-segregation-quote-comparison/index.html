Cost Segregation Quote Checklist: Fees, Scope and Filing

A useful cost segregation quote identifies the property, study deliverable, reviewer, fee and support scope. Send each provider the same property facts, then compare what the price includes. A lower study fee or larger projected deduction does not establish a better result, and a study price does not automatically include tax-return implementation.

Give every provider a consistent property packet

Include purchase and closing records, land allocation, renovation costs, square footage, building use, placed-in-service information, existing depreciation schedules and any earlier study. Ask how missing information affects price and whether additional site or engineering work may be required. Keep assumptions separate from verified documents so that two estimates are not based on different property costs.

Ask about analysis, review and the report

Request a sample deliverable and the name or role of the person reviewing classifications. Ask how costs are identified or estimated, how the provider checks the information and how it handles a later correction. The IRS Cost Segregation Audit Techniques Guide discusses study methods and report evaluation; it is an examination guide, not an IRS approval of a provider or a guarantee that a particular allocation will be accepted.

Separate projected deductions from usable savings

A projected reclassification or depreciation amount is not automatically current tax savings. Ask the tax preparer to evaluate the owner’s facts, applicable limitations, elections and state treatment. Compare any forecast using the same assumptions, and label it as an estimate. Do not select a study solely from the largest number in a marketing illustration.

Make the filing and support handoff explicit

The tax professional should determine the appropriate reporting procedure, including whether any accounting-method analysis or amended return is relevant. Ask who supplies usable schedules, who answers the preparer’s questions and who is responsible for filing. For audit support, distinguish answering report questions, providing records, correcting work and representing the taxpayer; record limits and additional fees.

Before accepting the proposal

  • Confirm the property and cost basis used in the written quote.
  • Identify deliverables, reviewer, timetable and missing-record assumptions.
  • Itemize study, filing, amendment and other implementation fees.
  • Have the preparer review the handoff requirements before commissioning the study.
  • Keep the signed scope, report, schedules and support terms with the property records.

Request a property-specific quote

For an AE proposal, provide the property type, purchase and renovation costs, location, placed-in-service dates and existing depreciation schedule through the consultation process. Ask for a written study scope and a separate identification of filing or other implementation work.
