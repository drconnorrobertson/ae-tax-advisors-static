How the prices and estimates were calculated

Published prices come from official provider pages reviewed on October 24, 2026. Starting prices are floors, not fixed quotes. Promotional prices may expire. Published examples and averages are identified separately. Revenue thresholds, projected tax savings, deposits, and marketing “values” are not substituted for engagement fees.

For providers without a verified fee, AE supplies an estimate for a defined comparable scope. The estimate comes from peer fee schedules or an explicit hours-times-rate calculation. It is not an estimate based on a private quote from the named firm, not a statistical market average, and not evidence that the named firm is expensive. The actual proposal may be higher or lower.

Planning estimate: $4,000–$20,000+ per planning engagement

A defined tax-planning project for a business owner or investor; filings, studies, legal work, and new projects are extra.

AE’s rounded budgeting band uses published planning tiers from Anomaly and Investor Friendly CPA and Keystone’s higher planning range. It is a comparable-service budget, not a measured average or this firm’s fee.

National estimate: $6,000–$45,000 illustrative project budget

A hypothetical 20–100 hour tax advisory project, using $300–$450/hour for budgeting; enterprise audits, transactions, international work, and ongoing compliance excluded.

Calculation: 20 × $300 = $6,000; 100 × $450 = $45,000. The rates are external published planning benchmarks from Bright Hill, not this national firm’s rates. Actual engagements may fall outside this scenario.

Legal estimate: $3,000–$14,000 illustrative tax-work budget

A hypothetical 10–40 hour tax/accounting advisory scope using $300–$350/hour; legal documents, court representation, government fees, and entity formation excluded.
