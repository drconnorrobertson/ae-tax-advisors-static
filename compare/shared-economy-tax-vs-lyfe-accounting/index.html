Shared Economy Tax vs LYFE Accounting: Bookkeeping Scope and Quotes

Compare Shared Economy Tax’s agreed bookkeeping components with LYFE’s monthly starting range, record workflow and matched proposal questions.

Book a Call

Quote the same bookkeeping assignment

Shared Economy Tax addresses freelancers, independent contractors, Airbnb hosts, rideshare operators and other self-employed clients. LYFE describes small-business bookkeeping with a workflow from consultation to account connections, reconciliation and accountant review. These descriptions frame questions; they do not establish either firm as the best fit for a particular business.

Give both firms the same account inventory, transaction volume, reporting periods and required outputs. Say whether records are current or need cleanup. Monthly maintenance cannot be directly compared with rebuilding prior periods. Separate initial work from recurring service so the first-year total is understandable.

Interpret the prices correctly

LYFE publishes a general starting range of $250–$700 per month and says it may increase with bank accounts, transaction volume and additional services. Twelve payments at those endpoints would be $3,000–$8,400 before additional work. That calculation is an illustration, not a personalized annual quote or proof of a contract term.

Shared Economy Tax displays no numeric bookkeeping fee on the reviewed page. An undisclosed amount is not evidence that it costs more or less. Request its billing period, included volume and cleanup charges before calculating a total. Compare the same twelve months and identify one-time work separately.

Match the review and communication process

Shared Economy Tax lists potential components such as separating personal and business transactions, reviewing profit and loss, and discussing scenarios. Ask which appear in the agreement and how often they occur. LYFE describes a bookkeeper reconciling accounts followed by accountant review. Ask what that review covers and which reports reach the owner.

For a fictional business with two activities, three financial accounts and incomplete platform records, send both firms an identical description. Ask who resolves missing payout details and how unresolved items appear in reports. Confirm the main contact, response process and close schedule. A broad service label does not explain how delayed statements are handled.

Keep preparation and transitions explicit

Shared Economy Tax’s overview separates bookkeeping, return preparation and its planning program. LYFE links to broader accounting services. Neither fact establishes that a basic quote includes a return or planning project. Ask for the deliverable and responsible professional for every extra workstream.

Before choosing, confirm software ownership, user access, exports, notice requirements and the transition process. Retain reconciliations and supporting schedules with business records. AE is a competing provider, has not obtained private quotes from either firm, and does not assign a service-quality ranking in this comparison.
