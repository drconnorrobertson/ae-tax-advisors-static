An operating business, three rentals and a planned sale

Suppose you own a profitable business, three rentals and a property you expect to sell next year. A useful proposal should name the records needed, the decisions that must happen before the transaction and the team responsible for the affected returns.

Ask for a written sequence: reconcile the records, evaluate the alternatives, identify unresolved assumptions and assign the next actions. Request an explanation of how the proposed work changes if the sale is delayed or your income changes.

This is a hypothetical buying scenario, not a client result or a claim about either firm’s performance.

When to put AE on your shortlist

Interview AE when you want to bring business and property decisions into the same planning conversation. Start with the problem you need solved, then ask AE to show the deliverables, work schedule and separately priced implementation in its proposal.

If founder equity is your central issue, compare P4’s published QSBS focus directly against the specific expertise offered by your assigned AE team. A broad service menu alone does not answer that question.

P4 Tax pricing: what can you compare?

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I keep my existing CPA?

Ask both firms whether they can scope planning around your current preparer. Establish who reviews proposed positions, shares records and signs the returns before work begins.

Which firm should I choose?

Choose the proposal that answers your actual assignment with a named advisor, defined deliverables and complete cost. Compare sample work with client information removed, rather than selecting from a savings headline.
