Real Estate and Business Tax Advisors: Compare 12 Options

Compare 12 tax advisory options for business owners and real estate investors by planning scope, published fees, filing, legal work and implementation.

Book a Call

Choose the work before choosing the firm

A business owner with rental property may need several kinds of help: a household projection, recurring accounting, personal and entity returns, a property study or legal documents. Buying one does not automatically buy the others. This shortlist helps you decide which firms to ask for a matched written proposal.

AE Tax Advisors publishes this commercial guide and competes for advisory work. The options are not ordered by quality, popularity or independent customer scores. Official sources were reviewed November 23, 2026; confirm fees and availability directly.

Browse the full provider pricing directory · How AE researches comparisons

Start with your buying situation

  • Buying, owning or selling rentals: compare the portfolio analysis and the handoff to the preparer.
  • Operating a business and owning real estate: ask for one projection that reconciles payroll, profit, retirement funding and rental assumptions.
  • Replacing an accounting team: include bookkeeping, cleanup and filing in both proposals.
  • Changing entities or estate documents: identify the attorney and legal deliverables separately.
  • Keeping your investment manager: confirm whether tax advice can be engaged on its own.

What an AE proposal should make clear

AE’s standard advisory fee is $7,800 and complex advisory is $9,800. Returns, amendments, studies and additional services are separately scoped and priced. The standard engagement has no required recurring annual planning fee; that does not provide unlimited new planning in future years.

Ask for a defined deliverable, support period, implementation assignments, client dependencies and a combined quote for the actual work. A discovery call helps determine fit before detailed analysis begins.
