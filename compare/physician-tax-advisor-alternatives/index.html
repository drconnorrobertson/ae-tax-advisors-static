Start with the work you need

A physician with a W-2 and a simple return may only need preparation. A 1099 doctor may need entity and retirement-plan operations. A profitable practice owner with rental property may need a written multi-year tax plan, depreciation review, and coordinated business and individual filings. Compare firms on those deliverables before comparing a headline fee.
