FreeTaxUSA vs AE Tax Advisors: Filing Support and Advisory Scope

Compare FreeTaxUSA’s filing software and Tax Pro support with AE’s separately scoped advisory work, including costs, responsibilities and planning deliverables.

Book a Call

Two purchases with different responsibilities

FreeTaxUSA is tax-filing software with optional professional support. AE’s standard offering is a defined advisory engagement, with preparation and other work separately scoped. The useful comparison is who will do the task you need, not whether a free federal return appears less expensive than a project fee.

FreeTaxUSA’s official Pro Support description includes personal tax advice, calls and chat with a Tax Pro, and ProCheck final review. The assisted workflow starts with you preparing the return and entering the information. Confirm the type of help you need and how it fits the filing process.

Compare complete costs without implying equivalent services

FreeTaxUSA’s published 2026 components are $0 for the federal return, $15.99 per state and $44.99 for Pro Support. The one-state example with Pro Support totals $60.98 before other optional products. AE’s standard advisory fee is $7,800, paid in two $3,900 installments thirty days apart. Returns, amendments, studies and additional work have separate scopes.

Those totals are not matched quotes. Do not buy an advisory engagement solely to replace a supported software filing task, and do not assume a filing-support fee includes a separate multi-entity planning project. Ask each provider to confirm the actual assignment.

Decide whether one arrangement or a combination fits

A fictional business owner may be comfortable preparing an individual return but want analysis before admitting a partner or purchasing property. The filing tool can remain part of the workflow if the required forms and facts are supported. A separately engaged advisor can define the planning work and coordinate approved actions with the preparer. Agree who carries each action into the records and return.
