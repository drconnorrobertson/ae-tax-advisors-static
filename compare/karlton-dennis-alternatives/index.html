When to keep this offering on your shortlist

You saw an STR tax video and want an actual loss-offset analysis. Ask who checks customer stays, participation, financing and the return implementation before paying for a planning engagement.

Confirm the exact product, contracting firm, assigned professional and availability of personal involvement before enrolling.

A provider’s public education may help you learn concepts while a separate professional engagement addresses your records. Keep a current relationship when its scope and assigned team meet the requirements; no universal winner is assigned here.

Questions for the actual engagement

  • Does the engagement include a taxpayer-specific loss model?
  • Who checks participation and at-risk limits?
  • Who implements elections and prepares returns?
  • Which support, study or filing work is extra?

Give each bidder the same entity list, prior returns, current books, property details and deadline. Request a complete fee and written assignment for advice, implementation and filing. Share sensitive records only through the chosen provider’s secure intake process.

Compare complete costs and implementation

AE Tax Advisors provides tax planning for business owners and real estate investors. Standard advisory is $7,800 and complex advisory is $9,800. Returns, amendments, property studies and other implementation work are separately scoped and priced. Confirm the support period and responsibilities in the written engagement; the standard advisory engagement has no required annual planning renewal.

Confirm cancellation, renewal and handoff terms before changing firms. Identify unfinished work, upcoming returns and payment deadlines; do not end an engagement until the receiving team accepts the relevant scope.
