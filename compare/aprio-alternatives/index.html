Which part of an Aprio relationship are you replacing?

Aprio publishes business-tax and advisory services. A business that needs bookkeeping, recurring financial reporting and tax coordination is shopping for a different engagement from an owner seeking a one-time decision about an election or property acquisition.

Inventory the work currently performed before requesting alternatives: monthly close, entity returns, payroll coordination, projections, owner returns and specialist projects. A lower quote that omits one of those functions is not a like-for-like comparison.

Build the shortlist around the missing work

  • Recurring accounting support: specify the close schedule, systems, cleanup and management reports. Identify who owns tax-ready balances.
  • Business tax compliance: list each entity, state, filing date and owner-information handoff. Ask who resolves basis and prior-year questions.
  • An advisory project: define the decision, alternatives, written analysis and implementation tasks. Ask AE about a focused owner-level engagement if that is the actual need.

A company may keep its accounting provider while adding a separate planning project. Confirm the handoff before creating overlapping responsibilities or duplicate fees.

Where Aprio may still be the right choice

Aprio may fit especially well when your priority is companies needing broader accounting, M&A, or transaction support. Keep the current provider on the shortlist if it can show a clear scope, a qualified delivery team, and a fee that makes sense for the outcome. If its offer is national accounting and advisory, do not assume a different type of firm supplies an identical service.

Ask whether your account needs a transaction team, recurring tax compliance, specialty studies, or all three.

Compare proposals on the same basis

  • Work product: Ask for the exact report, plan, return, or program access you will receive.
  • People: Identify the lead advisor, engineer, preparer, or coach, and who signs any tax return.
  • Coordination: Confirm who implements recommendations and who supports questions after delivery.
  • Fees: Ask for first-year, renewal, amendment, and project costs in writing.
  • Fit: Share your entities, states, property types, and deadlines before accepting a proposal.

AE Engagement and Renewal Fees

Does AE Tax Advisors charge an annual planning renewal?

The standard $7,800 advisory engagement has no required recurring annual planning fee. Annual returns and any additional work are separately scoped. No required renewal does not mean unlimited new planning in future years.

Is the $7,800 advisory engagement an all-inclusive tax package?

No. Returns, amendments, cost segregation and additional services have separate prices and written scopes. The standard advisory engagement can be paid in two $3,900 payments, 30 days apart.

Aprio service review: continuity and responsibility

Evaluate the proposed working relationship at the engagement level. Ask who reviews the recurring accounts, who signs returns and who is responsible for connecting the business work to the household forecast.

For a change of provider, request an onboarding list: prior returns, fixed-asset schedules, carryforwards, reconciliations, elections and open notices. Confirm the last period covered by the outgoing firm and the first period covered by the new team.

Use the same service period and deliverables when comparing Aprio with another proposal. Neither a public service menu nor a general planning budget establishes the cost for your business.
