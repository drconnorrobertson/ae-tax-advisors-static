Bookkeeper360 vs Pilot: Managed Accounting, Automation and Reporting Cadence

Compare Bookkeeper360’s custom service with Pilot Essentials, Core and Custom by responsibility, accounting method and complete cost.

Book a Call

Choose the working arrangement before the plan

Bookkeeper360’s pricing page describes a dedicated accounting team and custom monthly or weekly plans from $400 monthly. Pilot offers a $99-per-month AI-supported Essentials workflow, Core from a displayed $299 monthly billed annually, and Custom service. This is a comparison of different operating arrangements, not merely a difference in prices.

List which tasks the business expects someone else to own. Transaction classification, reconciliation, adjusting entries, reporting and management explanations can require different levels of involvement. Ask each firm to assign responsibility for every step and identify the work that remains with the owner or internal staff.

Test the handoff with incomplete information

For a fictional consulting firm receiving a payment that covers several invoices, ask each provider how the supporting detail is collected, reviewed and reflected in the reports. Do not assume software can resolve missing facts or that human involvement eliminates the owner’s responsibility to explain an unusual item.

If the owner needs a weekly cash discussion, describe the desired report and decision. Bookkeeper360’s weekly option and Pilot’s various support features should be evaluated against that specific output. A weekly meeting, a real-time dashboard and a completed close are different services.

Build a complete first-year proposal

Bookkeeper360 separately lists catch-up or project work from $1,000. Pilot’s final quote depends on the plan and expense level; Core’s display specifies annual billing. Ask both firms about initial cleanup, integrations, historic data and any separate return preparation. Do not annualize a starting number without those conditions.

Record renewal, notice and export terms along with the recurring scope. Compare the matched responsibility map and complete quote rather than treating an automated entry product as identical to a custom accounting engagement. This AE-published review does not declare a universal winner.

Prepare a comparison brief

Prepare a task-by-task handoff rather than asking whether bookkeeping is included. Use the same two routine entries and two exceptions in both conversations. Have the proposed team explain who performs each step and what evidence the business keeps.

  • Two ordinary transactions with the documents staff currently retain for them.
  • One transfer or reimbursement requiring an explanation from the business owner.
  • One sales settlement needing fee and refund detail beyond the bank deposit.
  • The desired weekly or monthly output and the decision it will support.
  • The internal employee who can respond to questions before the reporting deadline.
