Planning deliverables and implementation

A planning relationship should make it easier to decide what to do, when to do it, and who is responsible. Ask for the actual deliverables: the analysis, assumptions, action list, decision deadlines, and implementation responsibilities. A conversation about possible strategies is a different deliverable from a written recommendation evaluated against your records.

Compare the engagement over the period you expect to use it. Include required renewal, additional implementation work, return preparation, amendments, and any specialist projects. Two proposals with similar starting prices can cover different work. Ask both firms to identify the first milestone and the point at which the initial engagement is considered complete.

Turning tax education into an accountable engagement

Someone arriving from TaxAlchemy's educational content may already know the names of strategies they want to explore. The next useful step is to replace a list of ideas with a list of decisions. For an AE comparison, describe the current business structure, expected profit, rental activity, and transactions under consideration. Ask the adviser to separate recommendations supported by the current facts from items that require more information.
