When to keep Armanino on your shortlist

Keep Armanino on your shortlist when its business tax and specialist service menu fits your operating footprint. Identify the lead team and deliverables for owner and business work.

Read the provider’s own description. The reviewed service menu describes available work, not a complete engagement or verified client outcome. Confirm the current team, availability and contract; do not infer that planning or a specialty is unavailable.

A situation worth comparing

You run a growing company entering a second state and need the owner return coordinated with entity filings. Ask whether the proposal includes nexus analysis, planning and filing rather than treating them as a single undefined service.

Choose the proposal with the technical scope, implementation assignments and service cadence this situation requires. A public service menu or lower starting fee alone does not establish the better fit.

Compare total cost before switching

Armanino pricing and quote checklist. A planning fee, a recurring membership and a full accounting relationship buy different work. Add filing, amendments, studies, legal work and future support to the same scope before comparing totals.

The standard advisory engagement has no required annual planning renewal. Confirm the support period, implementation assignments and exclusions in the written agreement; future work can require a new engagement.

Ask for a proposal you can compare

  • How are tax credits or international projects separately scoped?
  • What planning meetings and implementation support does the proposal buy?
