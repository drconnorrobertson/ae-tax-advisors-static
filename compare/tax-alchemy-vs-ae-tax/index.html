Tax Alchemy vs AE Tax Advisors: Which Tax Advisory Firm Is Right for You?

Tax Alchemy built one of the largest tax education audiences in the country. AE Tax Advisors is a hands-on advisory and preparation practice with cost segregation in house. Here is where the two differ, and who each one actually fits.

Request Your Free Tax Assessment

The short version

If you are searching "Tax Alchemy vs AE Tax Advisors," you are almost certainly deciding between two different models, not two versions of the same thing.

Tax Alchemy, founded by Karlton Dennis, is an education-forward tax company. Its reach comes from a very large YouTube and social audience, and the business is built around teaching high earners how tax strategy works, then offering programs and services to implement it. The typical Tax Alchemy client is a high-income W-2 earner, creator, or business owner who found the brand through content and wants a name they already trust.

AE Tax Advisors is a national tax advisory and preparation practice based in Billings, Montana. We do not run a course. Our model is a year-round advisory relationship with a named advisor, with cost segregation performed in house and the tax returns prepared by the same team that wrote the plan. Our typical client earns between $250,000 and $2 million and owns either an operating business, rental real estate, or both.

Both models work. They just solve different problems. Below is the detail.

Who Tax Alchemy is

Tax Alchemy is a California-based tax company associated with Karlton Dennis, an Enrolled Agent who is one of the most-followed tax educators online. The brand's strength is genuinely real: the content is clear, the explanations of depreciation, entity selection, and income shifting are accurate, and an enormous number of business owners learned that proactive tax planning exists because of that content.

The commercial side of the business centers on structured programs and advisory services aimed at high earners, frequently described in their marketing as serving the top of the income distribution. If you are a $500,000-plus earner who wants a recognizable brand, an educational framework you can follow yourself, and a community of other high earners doing the same thing, that is a coherent and defensible choice.

One practical note: program structures, pricing tiers, and exactly which services are delivered in house versus referred to partners change over time at every education-led firm, Tax Alchemy included. Ask directly and get the scope in writing before you sign.

Who AE Tax Advisors is

AE Tax Advisors is a tax advisory and preparation firm serving clients nationwide from Billings, Montana. The team includes IRS Enrolled Agents and licensed CPAs. We work with two overlapping groups: operating business owners taxed as S-Corps or C-Corps, and real estate investors who hold long-term rentals, short-term rentals, or commercial property.

The distinguishing feature of our model is that the strategy and the execution live in the same place. When we tell a client that a cost segregation study will produce a first-year deduction, our own team performs that study at $1 per square foot. When that study requires a Form 3115 change in accounting method to capture catch-up depreciation under Section 481(a), we prepare the Form 3115. When the plan calls for amending prior returns, we amend them. When filing season arrives, the return is prepared by the people who built the plan.

That matters because the most common failure in tax planning is not a bad idea. It is a good idea that never gets implemented correctly, because the person who wrote the plan and the person who files the return are different people who never speak.

Pros and Cons of Each

Every firm has a profile it serves well and a profile it does not. Here is an honest read on both.

Tax Alchemy

Education-led tax company

Strengths

  • Outstanding free educational content, genuinely one of the best explainers of tax strategy available anywhere
  • Strong, recognizable brand with a large community of high earners
  • Built for the $500K+ and top-1% earner profile
  • Frameworks are teachable, so you understand the strategy rather than just receiving it
  • Good fit if you want to be an active participant in your own planning

Limitations

  • Program-first model means part of what you buy is education you may not need
  • Cost segregation engineering is not the core of the business, confirm whether studies are performed in house or referred
  • Pricing is quoted per engagement rather than published, so comparison shopping takes a phone call
  • Less oriented toward mid-market earners in the $250K to $500K range
  • Brand-scale practices can mean less time with the specific advisor you met first

AE Tax Advisors

Advisory and preparation practice

Strengths

  • Cost segregation performed in house at $1 per square foot, $2,000 minimum
  • Strategy and tax preparation delivered by the same team, so plans actually get filed
  • Published pricing: $7,800 strategic advisory, $1,500 entity returns, $1,000 personal returns
  • Entity structuring, Form 3115, and prior-year amendment recovery all handled internally
  • Dual focus on operating business owners and real estate investors, which is where the biggest savings usually are

Limitations

  • No public course, community, or certification program if that is what you are looking for
  • Smaller brand footprint than a firm with millions of social followers
  • Not the right fit below roughly $250,000 of income, the advisory fee will not pencil out
  • We are selective about engagements, so there is a discovery call before we quote
  • Based in Montana, so most client contact is by video and phone rather than in person

Which One Should You Choose?

Choose Tax Alchemy if

You earn well above $500,000, you learn best from structured education, and you want a widely recognized brand behind your planning. If you enjoy understanding the mechanics yourself and want a framework plus a community, the education-led model is a real advantage rather than a compromise.

Choose AE Tax Advisors if

You want the plan and the filing handled by the same team, you own real estate where cost segregation is on the table, or your entity structure needs actual restructuring rather than explanation. If you would rather hand the whole thing to an advisor and get a return that reflects the strategy, that is the model we run.

Honestly, either works if

You simply want proactive planning instead of a preparer who shows up in March. Both firms will beat a compliance-only CPA by a wide margin. The wrong choice here is not Tax Alchemy versus AE Tax, it is staying with someone who only fills in the forms.

Why Clients Choose AE Tax Advisors

  • Cost segregation is in house, not referred out. Our team produces the study at $1 per square foot with a $2,000 minimum. A 3,000 square foot short-term rental costs $3,000, not the $5,000 to $15,000 that standalone engineering firms typically quote. Because we also file the return, the deduction lands where it is supposed to.
  • Advisory and preparation are bundled. Strategic advisory is $7,800, with a split-pay option. Entity returns are $1,500 and personal returns are $1,000. You are not paying one firm to design a plan and a second firm to file a return that ignores it.

Amendments are $2,500 each and frequently return several multiples of that.
  • Business owner and real estate investor in one practice. The largest savings usually come from coordinating the two: the operating business generates the income, the real estate absorbs it. A firm that only understands one half of that will leave money on the table.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tax Alchemy legitimate?

Yes. Tax Alchemy is a real tax company associated with Karlton Dennis, an Enrolled Agent, and its educational content is accurate and well regarded. The question is not legitimacy, it is fit: it is an education-led model aimed at very high earners, which suits some people better than others.

How much does Tax Alchemy cost compared to AE Tax Advisors?

Tax Alchemy does not publish standard pricing; fees are quoted per engagement, so you need to book a call to get a number. AE Tax Advisors publishes pricing: $7,800 for strategic tax advisory with a split-pay option, $1,500 per entity return, $1,000 per personal return, $2,500 per amended return, and cost segregation studies at $1 per square foot with a $2,000 minimum.

Is Tax Alchemy worth it for a real estate investor?

It can be, particularly if you want to understand the strategy yourself. But if the core of your plan is cost segregation, Form 3115 catch-up depreciation, and material participation documentation, you want a firm that performs the engineering study and files the return. That is the specific gap AE Tax was built to close.

What is the best Tax Alchemy alternative?

It depends on what you liked about it. If you wanted education, Mark Kohler and WCG CPAs both publish enormous free libraries. If you wanted done-for-you implementation with real estate depth, AE Tax Advisors, Hall CPA, and Keystone CPA are the firms most often compared against it.

Do I need to earn $500,000 to work with a tax advisory firm?

No. AE Tax Advisors works with clients in the $250,000 to $2 million range, which is where proactive planning tends to have the highest return per dollar of fee. Below roughly $250,000 of income, a good preparer plus a few targeted moves is usually the more rational spend, and we will tell you that on the call.

Can I switch tax advisors in the middle of the year?

Yes, and mid-year is often the best time. Switching before year-end means the strategy can still affect the current tax year rather than just the next one. We handle the transition, request prior returns, and review the last three years for missed deductions as part of onboarding.

Does AE Tax Advisors work with clients outside Montana?

Yes. AE Tax Advisors is a national practice. Our office is in Billings, Montana, and the majority of our clients are elsewhere. Meetings are by video, documents move through a secure portal, and we handle multi-state filings routinely.
