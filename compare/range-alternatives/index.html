Looking for Range alternatives? Decide whether you need a different financial planning relationship or additional tax services around the one you already have. That distinction matters especially when you own a business or rental properties.

Range’s public materials describe financial planning memberships, with tax coverage determined by tier. Range overview. An alternative should be evaluated against the exact parts of your financial life you want it to handle.

AE Tax Advisors publishes this guide and is included below. These are service-fit options, not an independent ranking. Public materials reviewed October 6, 2026.

Four options to compare

AE Tax Advisors when tax execution is the priority

Interview AE about a defined tax assignment if entity filings, business-owner decisions or property work drive your search. Its fee schedule separately lists advisory and filing work. AE service scope and pricing.

Ask how it would coordinate with your financial planner and what information each team must exchange. This is a tax-focused option to evaluate, rather than a verified replacement for Range’s entire wealth relationship.

Facet when you want a household financial roadmap

Facet advertises financial planning, investment services and tax planning and filing offerings. Facet overview.

Ask which service level fits your household, who will be your planning contact and how the tax work is scoped. Include equity compensation, rentals and business ownership in your initial brief so you can discuss complexity before joining.

Domain Money when a flat annual model appeals to you

Domain Money describes financial planning, managed portfolios, tax filing and strategy under a flat-fee model. Domain Money overview.

Compare its written package with the services you use at Range. Confirm preparation coverage, account management and any separately handled business work rather than assuming similar marketing means identical scope.

Hall CPA when rental operations need a specialist

Hall CPA advertises accounting, preparation, advisory and cost segregation for real estate investors. Hall CPA services.

It belongs on a tax-and-accounting shortlist for property owners. Ask which property records and entity returns the proposal covers and how it would work with your household advisor.

Keeping Range can also be an option

A missing service does not necessarily require changing every provider. Compare the cost and coordination effort of adding a business tax professional with moving to a new broader relationship. Name one owner for the combined projection and establish a schedule for exchanging records.

This is an arrangement to evaluate, not a claim that any two listed firms have an established partnership.

Use this short interview brief

Tell each candidate what you need completed, list your entities and properties, identify the financial services you want to retain and provide your target dates. Then ask for:

  • The named person responsible for each workstream.
  • The written work product and meeting schedule.
  • The returns and jurisdictions included.
  • The complete annual or project cost and extra charges.
  • The process for coordination with outside advisors.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Range alternative for a business owner?

That depends on whether your priority is financial planning, business tax preparation, accounting or a specific planning project. Build the shortlist around that priority and compare actual proposals.

Should I compare only firms with flat fees?

A fee model is one criterion. Coverage, advisor access and implementation matter too. A predictable fee is useful only when you understand what it purchases.

Range.com: public services and the decision to compare

Range.com's public materials describe wealth management integrating tax, investment, retirement, and estate planning across service tiers. This is relevant to high earners who value a connected financial platform and ongoing advice. Review the official service description. Your written proposal determines the actual scope and current fees.

Tax scope inside a wealth relationship

Wealth management can connect investments, retirement, estate work, and tax decisions. The comparison should begin with your actual objective: ongoing wealth management, tax filing, or a discrete planning question. Ask how services are bundled and whether a tax project can be evaluated separately. Record the fees associated with each service and whether changing providers requires moving or reconnecting accounts.

If you already have financial and legal advisers, ask how the tax professional will coordinate with them. Compare the planning deliverables, review process, ongoing access, and renewal terms. A membership or broader wealth relationship can cover different work from a focused tax engagement; evaluate the complete scope instead of comparing only the advertised starting price.

A practical comparison for your situation

You want a consolidated financial view but your immediate priority is a specific tax decision involving income or a business.

Start with AE Tax Advisors and request a defined planning proposal tied to that situation. Then compare Range.com's proposal for the same facts. Ask which membership tier includes proactive tax planning and filing and what happens at renewal.

The question to resolve before choosing

Does my selected tier include the tax work I actually need?

Ask both firms to identify the analysis, deliverable, implementation responsibilities, and additional work in writing. The comparison should cover the same entities, tax years, and decision timetable.
