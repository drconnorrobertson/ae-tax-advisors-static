KPMG Alternatives: Compare Enterprise Tax and Owner Planning

Alternatives to KPMG depend on the work you are replacing: a company tax function, a transaction, cross-border compliance or a focused owner-planning project. Deloitte, PwC and BDO offer broad tax practices to investigate. AE is a separate option for a defined business-owner or real-estate tax assignment. The list below explains those different routes before you request proposals.

Deloitte: direct tax and connected specialist work

Deloitte publishes direct tax, indirect tax, global employer and tax-technology services. It is a candidate to investigate when the proposal needs several tax disciplines. Ask which named team would deliver your assignment, how cross-border work is coordinated and what remains outside the engagement.

PwC: business tax and private-company needs

PwC publishes tax services alongside private-company, family-enterprise and private-wealth offerings. Investigate it when business and ownership questions need coordination. Request separate responsibility for the company, shareholders, trusts and transactions rather than assuming one engagement covers every related taxpayer.

BDO: tax advice, compliance and specialist projects

BDO publishes tax services and specialist advisory offerings. It belongs on a shortlist when its proposed team can demonstrate the relevant industry and technical scope. Compare the actual deliverable and review process with KPMG, not the overall size or visibility of either brand.

AE Tax Advisors: a focused owner-planning alternative

AE offers separately scoped tax-planning work for business owners and real-estate investors. This may be a distinct assignment alongside an existing enterprise tax relationship. AE does not present its standard engagement as a substitute for every audit, international tax function or transaction project. Confirm the entities, years, deliverables and implementation work in the written scope.

What KPMG itself offers

KPMG’s current tax materials cover a broad range of tax services. Keep its actual proposal in the comparison if the required team and scope fit. A company can retain a broad tax provider while commissioning a separately defined owner-level project; the advisers need an agreed information handoff.

Request comparable written proposals

  • Provide the same entities, countries, years and decision deadline to each candidate.
  • Identify the lead professional, specialist involvement and person responsible for each final deliverable.
  • Separate recurring compliance, transaction work and owner planning in the fee schedule.
  • Ask how scope changes, missing records and post-delivery questions affect cost.
  • Obtain the actual quote; an external hourly benchmark does not establish KPMG’s or another firm’s fee.

Example: business sale with separate owner planning

A private company preparing for a sale may need transaction tax diligence and structuring while its owner needs a personal cash-flow and planning review. Request two connected scopes with consistent assumptions and a named coordinator. Comparing those scopes is more useful than treating a focused owner engagement as the same purchase as company-wide transaction support.
