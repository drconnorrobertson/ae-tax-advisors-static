A business owner who needs both clean books and planning

Suppose your company is profitable, your reporting is inconsistent and you are considering buying a rental. You have at least two assignments: produce reliable financial information and evaluate the planned purchase in your wider tax position.

Ask whether one provider can own both assignments under a clear scope or whether separate teams should coordinate. Request a data cutoff, a process for correcting discrepancies and a planning deadline before you commit to the purchase.

The scenario is hypothetical. It is designed to make the interview useful, not to illustrate a promised result.

When to interview AE

Put AE on your shortlist when a business and property planning decision needs focused attention. Bring the entity information, available financial reports and transaction timeline. Ask AE to define what it can conclude from those records and what remains unresolved.

If the central assignment is Puerto Rico relocation or dual-jurisdiction compliance, evaluate providers against that specialized work directly. Do not assume a general tax planning package covers it.
