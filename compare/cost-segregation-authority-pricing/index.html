How the prices and estimates were calculated · AE’s pricing and separately scoped services

What changes the quote?

The published starting fee is a screening point. A useful quote identifies the property and the exact work to be delivered. Ask the provider to distinguish the original building, later improvements, prior-year depreciation work, and any renovation or disposition analysis.

Build a complete project budget

Request separate amounts for the property study, any record reconstruction, tax-return implementation, amendments if appropriate, state work, and owner-level planning. Mark an item as included only when the proposal says so. Do not add a second fee for a task already included, or omit it merely because another professional is expected to do it.

For an older property, have the preparer determine the correct procedure before accepting a blanket “catch-up” promise. Form 3115 eligibility, a section 481(a) adjustment, amended-return procedures, and filing deadlines depend on the facts and applicable IRS rules. The study fee alone does not resolve them.

Ask when payment is due, what triggers an added fee, and what happens if you pause before the report is completed. Get the agreed deliverables and changes in writing; the website starting price does not establish the terms of your contract.

Use a benefit test that fits your ownership

First estimate the deduction that survives basis, at-risk, passive activity, and other applicable limits. Then estimate the timing of tax savings, remaining depreciation, and possible tax on a sale. Compare those cash flows with all project fees. If the deduction is suspended or a sale is near, a low-priced study can still be a poor use of cash.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Cost Segregation Authority study cost?

CSA Partners publishes a starting study fee of $3,000 as verified November 6, 2026. It says pricing varies with property size, complexity, available documentation, and engagement scope. That starting amount is not a guaranteed quote for your property or an all-inclusive tax-advisory fee.

Why is the older $2,500–$5,000+ figure still shown?

It is AE’s historical comparable-work budget reviewed October 24, 2026, not a published CSA price. It is retained with its assumptions and benchmark sources separately from CSA’s subsequently verified $3,000 starting study fee.

Does the study price include tax returns or Form 3115?

CSA says it does not prepare tax returns and describes working with the client’s CPA or tax advisor. Get a written division of duties for Form 3115 assistance, preparation, filing, and state adjustments. Do not assume either that all implementation is included or that the study provider offers no support.

How should I judge the fee if the depreciation estimate is large?

Compare the complete study and implementation cost with estimated tax cash flows you can actually use, after applicable loss limitations and the planned holding period. A large first-year deduction does not guarantee an immediate refund or permanent savings.

What should I send to obtain a useful quote?

Start with property type, approximate size, acquisition and placed-in-service dates, improvement history, the decision deadline, and whether a preparer will implement the study. Ask which detailed records are needed and arrange secure transfer for tax returns and financial documents.
