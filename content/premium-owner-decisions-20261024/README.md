# Premium owner decision guides

Five new guides and three expanded existing destinations focus on decisions that can justify coordinated advisory: transactions, multiple businesses, employee-plan design and established rental portfolios.

The keyword map is editorial targeting, not a Search Console or search-volume report. No rankings, demand estimates or savings outcomes are represented. Existing CPA-coordination, portfolio-prioritization and engagement-deliverable routes were retained to avoid duplicate search destinations.

Each guide includes a concrete illustrative scenario, required records, professional responsibilities, published advisory fees, related service/case links and primary-source references. Illustrations are not client results. No individual professional review is claimed. The existing engagement article was rewritten to remove unsupported universal turnaround, commission and savings assertions and to align the page with published scope boundaries.

Generation: `python3 scripts/premium-owner-decisions-20261024.py`.

Validation: `node scripts/build-release.cjs`, `python3 scripts/verify-premium-owner-decisions.py public`, and `node scripts/check-sitemaps.cjs`. The production build includes the required 100-provider/300-page verification and public-content exclusion checks. The release-specific verifier checks canonical routes, redirect/review-hold conflicts, indexability, structured data, content, booking paths, sitemap membership, and internal link destinations. Cost-segregation booking follows the site's existing routing rules.

After deployment: `python3 scripts/verify-premium-owner-decisions.py public --live`.

The manifest records the eight target routes, search themes, source URLs and nine existing discovery destinations. IRS examination manuals and audit technique guides are background guidance, not binding law or approval of a provider.
