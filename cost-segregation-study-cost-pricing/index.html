Cost Segregation Study Cost and Pricing

Compare the total cost of a completed, implemented study, not just the price of a report. Start with AE’s published fee, then confirm the property-specific scope in writing.

Book a Call

AE Tax Advisors cost segregation pricing

$1 per square foot, with a $2,000 minimum per study. This is AE's published standard pricing, not an industry-wide average. Complex or specialized work may be quoted separately. Confirm the square footage used for billing and the final scope before work begins.

  • A 1,500-square-foot property reaches the $2,000 minimum.
  • A 3,000-square-foot property has an illustrative standard study fee of $3,000.
  • An 8,000-square-foot property has an illustrative standard study fee of $8,000.

These examples show the pricing formula only. They do not estimate deductions or confirm eligibility. Cost segregation is separate from the $7,800 Strategic Tax Advisory or $9,800 Complex Tax Advisory engagements. View all published service fees.

What should the proposal include?

Identify the property address, ownership entity, relevant tax years, acquisition history and improvements. A recently purchased single building and an older portfolio with multiple renovations have different records and reconciliation needs.

  • Engineering and documentation: who performs and reviews the analysis, what records are required, and whether inspection or other documentation is included.
  • Report: asset classifications, allocation of basis, depreciation schedules and supporting rationale.
  • Implementation: who updates the return and asset register, and whether Form 3115 preparation or state adjustments are separately priced.
  • Support: the scope and limits of report corrections, preparer questions and audit support.
  • Timing: the deadline, dependencies, review steps and treatment of incomplete records.

AE's published study scope includes an engineering-based analysis and a supporting report. A catch-up calculation should not be mistaken for a commitment to prepare and file the method change or every related return.

Compare proposals using the same facts

Give each provider the same property details and ask them to state assumptions. If one estimate uses acquisition price including land and another uses depreciable basis, their projected percentages cannot be compared directly. The same is true when an estimate includes new renovation costs that another omits.

Ask for the total engagement cost, not a fee disconnected from return implementation. Confirm whether discounts, travel, additional buildings, amendments, state schedules or missing-record reconstruction change that cost.

Review AE's study process and deliverables before comparing proposals. Use the documents checklist to identify information a provider still needs.

How to evaluate whether the study is worth the fee

Evaluate incremental tax benefit relative to the depreciation available without a study. A projected deduction is not itself tax savings, and suspended deductions do not generate the same immediate cash result as currently usable ones.

For example, if a reviewed projection showed $12,000 of current incremental tax benefit and the combined study and implementation cost were $4,000, the initial net cash benefit would be $8,000 before later-year effects. This is arithmetic using hypothetical inputs, not an AE client result or a forecast for your property.

Model the holding period and eventual disposition as well. Accelerated deductions change timing, and future deductions or sale taxes can offset some of today's benefit. The calculator provides a limited first-year scenario; a full engagement should examine the broader facts.

When a low quote may become an expensive decision

A low report price is less useful if the basis does not reconcile, the return preparer cannot implement the schedule, or the proposed loss cannot be used. Conversely, a higher price does not establish better quality. Compare the documented work and accountable deliverables.

Be cautious about choosing a provider solely because its forecast has the largest reclassification percentage or because it promises that the report is IRS-approved. Ask for the basis of the classification and the specific support promised under the engagement.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is $2,000 the fee for every property?

No. It is the minimum under the published $1-per-square-foot formula. A larger property may have a higher fee, and complex work may be quoted separately.

Does the advisory fee include the study?

No. The website prices cost segregation studies separately from Strategic and Complex Tax Advisory.

Does the cheapest study offer the best return?

Not necessarily. Compare the fee, report quality, implementation responsibilities, usable deduction and expected holding period together.

Can a previously acquired building be quoted?

Yes, a lookback review can be scoped, but the original service dates, prior asset schedule and improvement history are needed. Confirm any additional reconciliation and filing work.

Compare written scope before comparing the headline fee

A useful price comparison lists the property, assets, tax years and deliverables covered by each proposal. A study quote and a return-preparation quote solve different parts of the project. Confirm whether prior depreciation reconciliation, Form 3115 preparation, owner-level loss analysis, state adjustments and responses to later questions are included or separately billed. Cost segregation is separately priced from AE tax advisory; the signed engagement controls the work.

Illustrative decision

Suppose Proposal A covers the report only while Proposal B also includes a preparer handoff and a prior-year schedule reconciliation. Comparing the two prices without assigning those remaining tasks can understate the total project cost. Ask both providers to complete the same scope list rather than assuming that a longer report includes more implementation.

Records and decisions to prepare

  • Identify the property and square footage used in the quote
  • List report, tax filing and advisory work separately
  • Confirm additional-property and revision charges
  • Record who handles questions from the return preparer
