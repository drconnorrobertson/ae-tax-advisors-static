Tax Planning for Business Owners

Make tax decisions while you can still act on them. AE Tax Advisors helps operating business and professional practice owners connect their entity structure, cash flow and major transactions to a coordinated tax plan.

Book a Call

Who this service is for

This service is for owners making decisions about a profitable operating business, professional practice or business-owned real estate. It is especially relevant when owner compensation, multiple entities, new equipment, retirement contributions or a planned sale make a return-only approach insufficient.

The first review should identify your actual decision, its deadline, and the records needed to model it. An S corporation election, a new retirement plan or a building purchase is not automatically the right move because it has a tax benefit.

Published advisory fees: Strategic Tax Advisory is $7,800; Complex Tax Advisory is $9,800. Both are listed as two equal payments 30 days apart. Returns, amendments and cost segregation are separately priced. Review current pricing and the written engagement for the final scope.

Decisions to coordinate in one plan

  • Entity structure and owner compensation: compare the operating and administrative consequences of the existing structure before changing it.
  • Cash flow and estimated taxes: connect projected business results to the owner's expected payments.
  • Equipment and real estate: review purchase timing, service dates, financing and depreciation before committing funds.
  • Retirement and employee benefits: evaluate the business's contribution capacity and obligations to eligible employees.
  • Acquisitions and exits: involve the tax team before purchase-price allocation, ownership changes and sale terms are finalized.
  • Prior-year issues: distinguish amendments, accounting-method changes and forward-looking corrections rather than assuming every issue uses the same filing.

Each recommendation should state the assumptions, expected benefit, implementation cost, deadline and responsible party. The goal is a plan the owner and preparer can execute.

What an advisory engagement should deliver

AE's published advisory scope includes strategy and planning, entity review, prior-year amendment analysis, return preparation oversight and ongoing advisory support. Complex advisory covers additional multi-entity and real estate planning needs. The engagement should identify which of these apply to you.

  1. Review: establish the ownership structure, financial baseline and upcoming decisions.
  2. Model: compare viable choices using your records and assumptions.
  3. Assign: document the actions, elections, payroll or legal coordination and filing responsibilities.
  4. Revisit: update the analysis when income, ownership or plans change.

Confirm which filings and implementation services are included; oversight is not the same thing as preparing every return. Read what a tax advisory engagement includes.

Prepare for an effective first review

Outline your business activities, entities, ownership percentages, approximate revenue and profit, payroll arrangements and major upcoming decisions. Note the deadlines and whether another CPA, bookkeeper or attorney is already involved.

After scope is established, the review may require recent returns, year-to-date financials, payroll reports, depreciation schedules and relevant agreements. The value of the analysis depends on complete records and the time available to implement it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to replace my current CPA?

The engagement should specify how AE and your existing preparer will coordinate. Assign responsibility for returns, implementation and ongoing questions before work starts.

Is this for employees without a business?

This service is designed for business and practice owners. Real estate owners can review the separate real estate tax planning service.

Are tax returns included in the advisory price?

The website lists return preparation and amendments separately. Your written scope should identify all included and separately priced work.

How much will I save?

A reliable estimate requires your records, alternatives and implementation costs. No fixed savings amount applies to every owner.
