Renting Your Home to Your Business: Augusta Rule Implementation

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-09-06

Updated November 8, 2026.

Section 280A(g) can exclude rent from a dwelling used as a residence when it is rented for fewer than 15 days during the tax year. The homeowner's exclusion and the business's deduction are separate questions. A payment between related parties does not automatically satisfy either side.

Confirm the dwelling qualifies as a residence

The statutory residence-use test generally looks to personal use exceeding the greater of 14 days or 10% of days rented at a fair rental. Review all uses of the dwelling and the relevant tax year. A property marketed as an investment is not automatically outside or inside the rule; actual use controls.

Count all rental days for the dwelling, including rentals unrelated to your business. The threshold is fewer than 15, not 15. Do not count only the days paid for by your own corporation.

Identify a real tenant and a real expense

A sole proprietor cannot create a federal deduction merely by paying rent to themselves. A disregarded entity generally does not create a separate federal income-tax tenant when its owner is on the other side of the arrangement. Corporations and partnerships need their own analysis of ownership, use and deduction rules.

The business must establish that the expense is ordinary, necessary and reasonable for its activities. An agenda labeled board meeting is not enough if the event was personal or the amount is unsupported. Related-party timing and reporting also require review.

Support rent for the actual use

Compare genuinely similar meeting or event space: location, capacity, hours, facilities and included services. A nightly vacation rental or luxury event quote may not support a short meeting in a smaller part of your home. Keep dated quotes and explain adjustments.

There is no universal IRS-approved daily rate. Avoid choosing a round annual tax-savings target and dividing by 14. The fair rental value of the actual use is the relevant starting point.

Document and pay the arrangement consistently

  • Record the property, legal owner, tenant and agreed dates.
  • Keep the business purpose, agenda, participants and evidence the event occurred.
  • Retain the rate comparison, rental agreement, invoice and payment record.
  • Reconcile all rental days, including outside bookings.
  • Coordinate the company's expense with the homeowner's reporting and personal deductions.

Rental-specific expenses generally are not deducted on the homeowner's side under the short-rental exclusion. Deductions available independently of rental use have their own limits. Do not claim the same cost twice.

Model one benefit without double counting

Illustration: a corporation pays $1,200 for a substantiated business event at a qualifying residence. Assume the amount is reasonable, the business deduction is allowed and the homeowner qualifies for the exclusion. The payment is $1,200 of rent, not $1,200 of tax savings. The company's deduction reduces taxable income; the owner's exclusion does not create a second cash payment.

The actual tax effect depends on entity type, usable deductions, tax rates, state treatment and other facts. Compare it with the business's legitimate venue alternatives.

Handle changes before filing

If total rentals reach 15 days, the short-rental exclusion does not apply; determine income and deductions under the applicable rental and personal-use rules. If the business paid excessive rent or the event did not occur as described, evaluate the correct reporting rather than manufacturing documentation later.

This is the implementation checklist. For the basic eligibility explanation, read what the Augusta Rule requires. For reimbursements of a continuing workspace, see the separate S corporation home-office guide. Request a review before relying on the proposed rental payment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does any payment from my LLC qualify?

No. Tax classification and the identity of the owner and tenant matter; an owner cannot create a federal rent deduction simply by paying themselves.

Can I exclude the first 14 days if I rent the home for 20 days?

The fewer-than-15-day exclusion does not apply once the dwelling reaches 15 rental days. Review the full-year rules.

Is there an IRS-approved $2,000 daily rate?

No universal rate applies. Support a reasonable amount for the actual business use using relevant comparable space.
