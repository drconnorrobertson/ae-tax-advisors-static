SaaS Founder Tax Planning: QSBS, Research Costs and Compensation

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-08-10 · Updated November 8, 2026.

SaaS tax planning should compare operating needs with a realistic exit scenario. A potential stock exclusion is important, but it does not by itself determine the best entity or eliminate every future tax.

Document stock eligibility before projecting QSBS

Section 1202 requires review of the issuer, original issuance, holder, active business and other conditions. Stock issuance and acquisition dates matter because the 2025 changes do not apply identically to every share. Keep capitalization, asset and redemption records.

The applicable gross-asset threshold, holding period and exclusion cap depend on those dates and rules. A financing valuation is not the same as the statutory gross-asset calculation. State treatment and the form of a future sale also require review.

Compare entity structures using more than the exit headline

Model corporate tax, distributions, compensation, investor requirements and transaction alternatives. A conversion needs a transaction-specific analysis of basis, holding period and eligible stock; it does not retroactively make all prior ownership qualify.

Separate research-cost treatment from the credit

Domestic research or experimental costs can fall under Section 174A for tax years beginning after 2024. Foreign costs, elections and prior capitalized balances need separate analysis. Maintain reliable project and location records.

The research credit has different eligibility and expense rules. Not every engineering wage, contractor payment or cloud bill qualifies. Document the business component, uncertainty, experimentation, contracts and qualifying expenses.

Review payroll and founder reimbursements

Compensation should reflect actual services and the entity's rules. An accountable plan requires business connection, substantiation and return of excess reimbursements. Founder housing, travel or meetings are not automatically deductible because the company paid.

Plan before financing or sale

Update the analysis after new stock, acquisitions, redemptions or changes in operations. Preserve documentation supporting both present deductions and any future stock exclusion.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every SaaS founder qualify for the same QSBS cap?

No. Issuance and acquisition dates, issuer and holder requirements, and other conditions matter.

Does deducting software development costs guarantee a research credit?

No. The deduction and credit have separate requirements.
