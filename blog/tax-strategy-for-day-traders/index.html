Day Trader Taxes: Status, Section 475 and Election Timing

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-08-11 · Updated November 8, 2026.

Trader status and a Section 475 election are separate tax questions. Frequent trading does not automatically establish either one. Review the activity, securities involved, existing positions and filing history before treating losses as ordinary.

Establish the activity first

IRS Topic 429 distinguishes investors from traders using the nature, frequency and continuity of the activity and the intent to profit from short-term market movements. There is no universal trade-count safe harbor. Keep trading history, holding periods, time records and a description of the business.

Understand what a valid election changes

For qualifying securities in an elected trading business, mark-to-market treatment generally changes the timing and character of gains and losses. Positions identified as investments and other categories require separate treatment. Trader status alone does not automatically remove capital-loss or wash-sale rules.

The annual individual net capital-loss deduction against other income is generally limited, but capital losses can offset capital gains before that limitation. A $340,000 loss does not always mean only $3,000 of total losses are used; the rest of the capital-gain calculation matters.

File the election on the proper timetable

For an existing taxpayer, the election statement is generally due by the unextended due date of the prior year's return and is attached to that return or a timely extension request. Thus, a calendar-year individual seeking a 2026 election generally acts at the 2025 return's unextended due date in 2026, not after the 2026 trading year ends.

New taxpayers have a different procedure tied to the start of their tax year. Forming an entity does not retroactively change the tax treatment of trades already made personally. Review the election and any required Form 3115 as separate steps.

Keep separate positions and records

Investment holdings excluded from trading treatment require appropriate identification. Keep trade confirmations, basis, transfers and year-end values. An election does not remove the need to reconcile positions and calculate results.

Futures, options, commodities and Section 1256 contracts can have different rules and elections. Do not assume a securities election is the right choice for every instrument or that one tax character is always preferable.

Apply the remaining limits and entity rules

Ordinary loss treatment does not make every loss immediately usable without restriction. Review the complete return, including applicable excess-business-loss and NOL rules. Business expenses also require their own eligibility and substantiation.

Trading gains generally do not create self-employment income merely because activity is a business. A proposed corporate salary and retirement plan require analysis of actual services, plan compensation, payroll cost and the entity's treatment. Do not establish an entity solely from a promised retirement deduction.

Request a trading-tax review with your trade exports, prior returns, election statements and position classifications before the election deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does trader status automatically create mark-to-market treatment?

No. Status and a valid Section 475 election are separate questions.

Can a new entity change earlier personal trading losses?

Not retroactively merely by being formed. Review the taxpayer, dates and election procedure.

Can capital losses offset capital gains?

Generally yes, before applying the individual limitation on net capital losses against other income.
