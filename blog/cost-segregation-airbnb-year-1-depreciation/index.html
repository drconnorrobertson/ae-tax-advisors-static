Airbnb Cost Segregation: Calculate First-Year Depreciation

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-08-08

Updated November 8, 2026.

A first-year Airbnb depreciation estimate should start with the purchase allocation and asset records. It should not start with a standard percentage of the purchase price. A cost segregation study may identify shorter-life assets, but the study date, listing on Airbnb and seven-day average stay do not by themselves determine the deduction.

1. Establish depreciable basis

Separate land from the building and other acquired assets using supportable values. Allocate qualifying acquisition costs and distinguish loan costs. Land is not depreciable. Furnishings bought separately need their own basis and service dates; do not count the same furniture both in the purchase allocation and as a new purchase.

For a property converted from personal use, depreciable basis can be limited by fair market value at conversion. The original purchase price is not automatically the depreciation starting point. Keep the closing statement, allocation support, improvement invoices and personal-use history.

2. Classify each component

A study identifies assets and supports their recovery periods. Appliance, furniture, land-improvement and structural classifications depend on their function and applicable tax rules. A pool, deck, HVAC component, fence or fixture should not be assigned a short life solely because it appears on a generic list.

There is no fixed percentage of every Airbnb purchase that must qualify. Building type, land value, construction, furnishings and documented costs change the result. Reconcile the study to the fixed-asset ledger, including any assets already depreciated separately.

3. Determine the building's recovery period independently

The average-customer-use test under Section 469 is a passive-activity classification rule. It does not automatically make a building 39-year property. Residential rental property and the transient-establishment exclusion under Section 168 require their own review of use and rental income.

Use the STR building recovery-period guide and the underlying facts before choosing a building class. A shorter-life component study does not eliminate the need to classify the remaining structure correctly.

4. Apply acquisition and placed-in-service dates

Current federal law generally provides 100% bonus depreciation for qualifying property acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025. Binding-contract, used-property, related-party, election and other eligibility rules still matter. Do not apply that rule to every asset from an earlier acquisition just because the study is performed in 2026.

An asset is generally placed in service when ready and available for its assigned income-producing use. Closing, renovation completion, listing publication and first guest stay may occur on different dates. Document the facts rather than assuming the purchase date controls every asset.

Regular building depreciation uses its applicable convention and service month. Dividing building basis by 27.5 or 39 does not generally calculate the actual first-year deduction. Separately classified assets can use different conventions and elections.

5. Calculate deductions before calculating tax savings

Illustration: assume a documented $500,000 acquisition is allocated $100,000 to nondepreciable land and $400,000 to depreciable assets. A hypothetical study supports $80,000 of qualifying shorter-life assets and $320,000 of remaining building basis. These figures illustrate mechanics, not an expected study result.

If all bonus conditions are satisfied and no election changes the treatment, the shorter-life layer may produce an $80,000 bonus deduction. Add the correct first-year building depreciation using its classification, service month and convention. Do not add a full annual building amount or treat the full $500,000 purchase price as depreciable.

Then calculate the net rental result after income and other allowed expenses. A depreciation deduction is not the same as a deductible loss, and neither is the same as tax savings. Compare the complete return with and without the proposed treatment.

6. Test whether the loss is usable now

For an activity with average customer use of seven days or less, the Section 469 rental-activity exception may apply. Material participation is a separate requirement. Other rental-definition exceptions can apply in other fact patterns. Keep actual stay records and participation evidence.

Personal use, basis, at-risk, passive-activity and excess-business-loss rules can limit the deduction. State bonus conformity and future depreciation recapture can change its value. A nonpassive classification is not a promise that every dollar offsets wages immediately.

Already owned the property before this year?

A later study may support a depreciation correction, but it does not create a new acquisition or placed-in-service date. Determine whether an amended return or an accounting-method change is appropriate, apply the original year's depreciation rules, and calculate any eligible Section 481(a) adjustment. Form 3115 procedures and exceptions must be reviewed for the actual filing year.

Use the amendment versus Form 3115 guide before changing prior depreciation. The correct route depends on filing history and the nature of the error.

Build a decision-ready estimate

  • Closing allocation, land support and improvement ledger.
  • Asset classification and study methodology.
  • Acquisition, contract and placed-in-service evidence.
  • Rental stays, personal use and participation records.
  • Prior depreciation, elections and suspended losses.
  • Current federal and state benefit, study cost and expected sale consequences.

Request an Airbnb depreciation review with these records. The useful output is a supported deduction and tax projection, including amounts that may be suspended, rather than a standard savings percentage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does every Airbnb building use a 39-year life?

No. Section 168 building classification is separate from the Section 469 average-stay test.

Does ordering a study in 2026 create 100% bonus eligibility?

No. The original acquisition and placed-in-service facts, asset eligibility and elections still govern.

Can I multiply the study deduction by my top tax rate?

That can misstate the benefit. Model the complete return, including loss limits, state treatment and future depreciation effects.
