Missed Form 2553? Check Late S Election Eligibility

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-08-24

Updated November 8, 2026.

Missing Form 2553 does not always end the possibility of an earlier S corporation effective date. Relief is conditional, however, and the correct route depends on the entity's classification, eligibility and filing history. A late-election request is not proof of a refund.

Reconstruct the original deadline

The general deadline is two months and 15 days after the beginning of the intended tax year, or during the preceding tax year. Calendar-year shorthand such as March 15 must be adjusted for weekends, holidays and the actual first-year facts.

Collect the formation documents, first ownership and asset dates, start of business, tax-year choice and any earlier election or acceptance notice. Do not file another election before checking whether the IRS already accepted one.

Match the facts to the relief procedure

Revenue Procedure 2013-30 addresses specified late S elections and related relief. Conditions include intended treatment, otherwise eligible status, reasonable cause, diligent correction, required statements and the reporting history applicable to the relief route. The general three-years-and-75-days period has specified exceptions; it is not the sole test.

An LLC also needing a late corporate classification election has additional requirements. Do not assume a generic reference to Revenue Procedure 2022-19 replaces the applicable late-election analysis.

Explain the real cause and obtain consents

Prepare a factual chronology showing what happened and what was done after discovery. Reliance on another professional or lack of awareness does not guarantee acceptance. Required shareholder consents and consistency statements can include people who no longer own the entity.

Use the current form instructions for notation, attachments, signature and filing destination. Retain submission evidence and the IRS response. Do not promise a fixed processing time.

Resolve inconsistent returns and payroll

Review entity returns, shareholder returns and payroll filings together. Amending a Schedule C or partnership return does not automatically satisfy the streamlined procedure. The effective date can change which returns and wage reporting are required.

If the facts fall outside available streamlined relief, evaluate a private letter ruling or a prospective election. The applicable current ruling procedure determines any fee; an old published fee should not be quoted as today's amount.

For the detailed workflow see the late-election filing guide. Request a review with all previously filed returns and election correspondence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is filing within three years and 75 days enough?

No. The other eligibility, cause, consent and reporting conditions must also be satisfied, and specified exceptions require separate analysis.

Can I fix inconsistent reporting just by amending?

Do not assume so. Check the specific relief route and coordinate entity, owner and payroll filings.

Is an old private-letter-ruling fee still valid?

Use the current applicable ruling procedure to determine the fee and requirements.
