Form 3115 for Missed Depreciation: Filing & Catch-Up Rules

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-09-06

Updated November 8, 2026.

Form 3115 can correct an eligible depreciation accounting method and bring a cumulative difference into the year of change through Section 481(a). It is not an automatic refund application, and a new cost segregation study does not make every old asset eligible for today's bonus-depreciation rate.

First determine what needs correcting

Separate an accounting-method change from a mathematical error, a change in underlying facts or a missed election. A consistent impermissible depreciation treatment generally needs a method-change analysis, while some errors can be corrected through other procedures. Identify the applicable automatic-change provision, designated change number, eligibility conditions and any exclusions before preparing the form.

Using 27.5- or 39-year depreciation for the structural portion of a building is not inherently wrong. A supported study can identify components that belong in other classes, but classification depends on the actual asset and use. Land is not depreciable.

Recompute the history using the history's rules

Build an asset ledger with cost, land allocation, acquisition and placed-in-service dates, improvements, dispositions, business use, prior elections and depreciation taken. Recalculate each year's proper depreciation under the applicable law. The 100% rate restored for qualifying property acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025 does not automatically apply to an asset purchased years earlier.

The Section 481(a) adjustment generally compares cumulative treatment through the beginning of the year of change. Keep current-year depreciation separate. For illustration, if the supported old-method total is $120,000 and the correct new-method total for that same period is $205,000, the favorable difference is $85,000. That is a deduction amount, not an $85,000 tax refund.

Prepare the form and both filings

  1. Confirm the taxpayer and property qualify under the current automatic-change procedures, including later modifications.
  2. Complete the required Form 3115 sections and depreciation schedule with the reason for change and adjustment calculation.
  3. Attach the original to the timely filed federal return for the year of change, including extensions, when that procedure applies.
  4. Submit the signed duplicate under the current IRS filing instructions. The ordinary automatic-change mailing destination is Ogden, not the Washington address used for non-automatic requests.
  5. Retain the filed form, submission evidence, study and reconciled asset ledger.

Some special procedures have different return, amendment, AAR or timing requirements. Use the IRS filing page at the time of submission rather than relying on an old saved address.

Check how much of the deduction can be used

A favorable adjustment is generally taken in the year of change under the applicable procedure, but basis, at-risk, passive-activity and other loss rules can defer its benefit. Positive adjustments generally have different spreading rules and exceptions. State calculations may differ from federal treatment.

If you are selling or already sold the property

Disposition changes which method-change procedure may apply; it does not justify a blanket statement that correction is always unavailable. Review the specific disposed-property provisions, year of change and exclusions, especially for nonrecognition transactions. Coordinate any correction with the sale-year basis and gain calculation.

For the decision when nothing was ever depreciated, see the missed rental-depreciation guide. For the calculation, see the Section 481(a) reconciliation. Bring prior returns and asset records to a review.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a study completed in 2026 give an older purchase 100% bonus depreciation?

No. Recompute depreciation using the acquisition, service dates, elections and law applicable to that property.

Is Form 3115 always filed with the IRS national office?

No. Current automatic-change instructions generally direct the signed duplicate to Ogden, with other permitted submission procedures. Non-automatic requests have different instructions.

Is the full adjustment immediately usable?

Not necessarily. An eligible favorable adjustment can enter the year of change while loss limitations defer its current tax benefit.
