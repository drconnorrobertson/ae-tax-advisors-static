Partial Asset Disposition: Replaced Components, Basis and Election Timing

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-05-24 · Updated November 8, 2026.

A partial asset disposition can remove the remaining basis of a qualifying retired component from a larger depreciable asset. The deduction is not the replacement invoice. Establish the old component, its basis and the disposition treatment before filing.

Identify what was retired

Keep the project scope, contractor records and evidence that the old component was removed or abandoned. A repair to an existing component is different from retiring it. Review the replacement cost's capitalization treatment separately.

Determine the old component's basis

Use original cost records where available. When the records do not separately identify the component, the regulations permit reasonable methods in specified circumstances. A study, cost allocation or appropriately applied index method requires support; an arbitrary percentage does not establish basis.

Then subtract applicable depreciation through disposition. For example, if a supported old component has $40,000 unadjusted basis and $15,000 accumulated depreciation, its remaining basis is $25,000 before any other required adjustments. The new component's cost is a separate entry.

Check timing and exceptions

The elective partial-disposition rules generally require action on a timely filed return, including extensions, for the disposition year. Special situations and relief provisions must be analyzed separately.

Do not assume Form 3115 automatically permits a late election for any prior replacement. The regulations generally prohibit making this election through an accounting-method application except where an express exception or published guidance applies.

Apply owner-level limits

A properly determined loss may still be restricted by basis, at-risk, passive or other rules. The potential deduction is not the same as a guaranteed current tax saving.

Maintain the revised asset schedule

Remove the retired component consistently, record the replacement and preserve the calculation. Coordinate related disposal and removal costs to avoid duplication. Request a component-disposition review with the old depreciation schedule and project records before filing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the loss equal to the new roof or equipment invoice?

No. It is based on the qualifying retired component’s remaining adjusted basis.

Can Form 3115 automatically fix a missed election?

No. Review the disposition-year election requirements and any specific relief or exception.
