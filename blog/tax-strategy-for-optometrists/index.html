Optometry Tax Planning: Optical Sales, Inventory and Equipment

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-08-11 · Updated November 8, 2026.

An optometry practice can earn professional-service fees and optical retail income. Separate records help evaluate those activities, but bookkeeping labels alone do not establish separate businesses or a larger deduction.

Review QBI using the actual activities

Professional health services can be a specified service trade or business. Optical sales, shared staff and related operations need review under the applicable rules. Owner-level taxable income and other limits also matter; a fixed percentage of retail revenue is not a completed QBI calculation.

Reconcile inventory and receivables

Track frames, lenses, contacts, vendor credits and amounts billed to insurers. Small-business accounting exceptions have eligibility and method requirements. They do not necessarily permit an immediate deduction for every item purchased but still on hand.

A method change requires review of the existing method, applicable procedure and transition adjustment. Do not assume receivables and inventory balances automatically become a deduction of the same amount.

Document equipment and build-outs

Maintain acquisition, financing and service-date records for imaging, diagnostic and office assets. Test asset classifications and any expensing election separately. Coordinate a construction study with existing equipment schedules to prevent double counting.

Price retirement and ownership changes

Use the staff census and eligible compensation to compare retirement plans. A fixed owner contribution or staff-cost estimate is not reliable without plan design. A practice acquisition or partner change can affect compensation, basis and plan obligations.

Request an optometry practice review with service and optical records, inventory reports, equipment schedules and plan documents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do separate optical books guarantee a separate QBI-eligible business?

No. They support the review, but actual activities and the governing rules control.

Does qualifying as a small business automatically expense all inventory purchases?

No. The chosen permissible method and its requirements still matter.
