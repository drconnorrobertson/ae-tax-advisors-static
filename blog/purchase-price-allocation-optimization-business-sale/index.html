Business Sale Purchase Price Allocation: Form 8594 and Tax Character

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-06-19 · Updated November 8, 2026.

Purchase price allocation affects both parties in a qualifying business asset acquisition. It must follow the applicable rules and supportable asset values; it is not permission to move amounts into whichever category produces the lowest tax.

Determine whether Section 1060 applies

Identify what is being sold and whether the transaction is an applicable asset acquisition. A stock sale has a different starting point, although particular elections can change its tax treatment. Confirm the seller, buyer and any separate asset owners.

Build the asset and basis schedule

List cash, receivables, inventory, equipment, real estate and intangible assets. Reconcile adjusted tax basis and prior depreciation or amortization. Book values alone may not establish fair market value.

Apply the residual method

Allocate consideration among the required asset classes in the prescribed order. Goodwill and going-concern value receive the residual after the other applicable allocations. Use appraisals or other support when needed and reconcile the schedule to the purchase agreement.

Model tax character for each party

Inventory and receivables can create ordinary income. Equipment can produce depreciation recapture. Goodwill treatment depends on ownership, basis, holding period and other facts. A C corporation asset sale can also create a separate shareholder-level consequence when proceeds are distributed.

The buyer's depreciation or amortization treatment follows the acquired asset and applicable law. A label in the agreement does not itself establish bonus eligibility, and a covenant allocation requires its own review.

Agree on reporting and later adjustments

Both parties generally file Form 8594 when required. Address how they will share schedules and handle contingent payments, liabilities and later changes to consideration. A later adjustment can require supplemental reporting.

Review a proposed founder-owned goodwill transaction separately; business goodwill is not automatically owned by the individual. Bring contracts and ownership evidence to the review before signing.

Request a sale allocation review with the draft agreement, asset register, prior depreciation and proposed values.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the parties assign any amount to goodwill?

No. The residual method and supportable asset values constrain the allocation.

Does the purchase agreement alone settle the tax treatment?

No. Review the transaction facts, applicable law and required return reporting.
