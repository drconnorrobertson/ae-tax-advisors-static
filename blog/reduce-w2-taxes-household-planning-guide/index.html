Reduce W-2 Taxes: A Household Planning Guide for High Earners

Review workplace benefits, household income, investment decisions and rental loss limits before choosing a W-2 tax strategy.

Book a Call

By AE Tax Advisors Team | Published November 6, 2026

This original AE Tax Advisors guide accompanies Reduce Your W-2 Taxes.

Begin with the income you actually have

W-2 planning starts with employee compensation, workplace benefits and the household’s other income. Salary, bonuses, equity compensation, a spouse’s wages and investment gains can create very different planning needs. Gather pay statements and benefit elections before estimating savings.

Changing Form W-4 withholding adjusts payments toward tax. It does not by itself reduce the underlying liability. Compare total projected tax with withholding and estimated payments so cash-flow changes are not mistaken for permanent savings.

Review workplace benefits before adding complexity

Eligible pretax retirement contributions can reduce current federal taxable wages, subject to the applicable plan and tax-year rules. Roth contributions have a different tax treatment. Health savings account contributions require eligibility, including qualifying coverage and the absence of disqualifying coverage.

Review employer plans, contribution records and household coverage before assuming a deduction is available. Contributions, employer funding and eligibility can interact. A strategy should fit liquidity needs and long-term savings goals as well as the current tax return.

Evaluate side businesses and real estate on their facts

Forming an LLC does not turn employee wages into business income or create deductions for personal spending. A real side business must be evaluated using its actual income, expenses and activities. Entity planning should follow those facts.

Rental depreciation does not automatically offset wages. Passive activity rules often restrict rental losses, and exceptions require careful analysis. Short-term rentals require review of average stays, services, participation and other limitations. A property purchase should also make financial sense before any tax benefit is considered.

Use one household projection to compare alternatives

Investment gains, charitable giving, retirement elections and business activity should be modeled together. A deduction may reduce taxable income without reducing adjusted gross income, and some benefits are limited by income or other requirements. State treatment can differ from federal treatment.

Review the companion Reduce Your W-2 Taxes resource on Google Sites, then bring your pay statements, prior return and upcoming transactions to an AE discovery call. Request a projection showing eligibility, cash required, implementation steps and estimated total tax under each option.
