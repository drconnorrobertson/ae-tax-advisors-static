Tax Planning for Construction Contractors

Equipment depreciation, entity structuring, and strategic tax planning for GCs, subcontractors, and builders

Construction contractors face unique tax challenges. Between heavy equipment purchases, project-based revenue, multi-state job sites, and seasonal cash flow, the tax strategy for a construction business requires specialized knowledge. AE Tax Advisors works with general contractors, subcontractors, and builders to minimize tax liability while supporting business growth.

Equipment Depreciation

Construction equipment represents one of the largest capital expenditures in the industry. Section 179 and bonus depreciation allow contractors to deduct the full cost of qualifying equipment in the year of purchase rather than depreciating it over 5 to 7 years.

  • Section 179 allows immediate expensing of qualifying equipment up to annual limits
  • Bonus depreciation at 100% provides first-year deduction for new and used equipment
  • Heavy vehicle deductions for trucks and vehicles over 6,000 lbs GVWR
  • Listed property rules for equipment with mixed business and personal use

Entity Structuring for Contractors

The right entity structure protects contractors from excess self-employment tax and liability exposure. AE Tax Advisors evaluates S corporation elections, holding company structures for equipment and real estate, and multi-entity frameworks that separate operating risk from asset ownership.

Accounting Method Selection

Construction companies have specific accounting method options that affect tax timing. The choice between cash method, accrual method, percentage-of-completion (PCM), and completed contract method directly impacts when income is recognized and when deductions are taken. AE Tax Advisors evaluates which method produces the best tax outcome for each contractor's situation.

Cost Segregation for Commercial Projects

Contractors who own commercial buildings, warehouses, or office space can benefit from cost segregation studies that reclassify building components into accelerated depreciation categories. For contractors who build and hold real estate, this strategy can produce significant first-year deductions.

Multi-State Compliance

Construction companies that work across state lines face nexus issues, withholding requirements, and varying tax rates. AE Tax Advisors manages multi-state compliance and develops strategies to minimize aggregate state tax liability.
