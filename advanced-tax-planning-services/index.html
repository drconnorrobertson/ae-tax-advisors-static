Frequently Asked Questions

What makes tax planning 'advanced'?

Advanced planning goes beyond standard deductions to restructure business entities, timing, and transactions to minimize taxes while staying fully compliant.

Is aggressive tax planning illegal?

No, when done properly. There's a difference between tax planning (smart structuring) and tax evasion (hiding income). We do the former only.

Can I reduce self-employment taxes?

Yes. Strategies include S-corp election, business structure optimization, and proper employee vs. contractor classification.

What about Opportunity Zones?

Opportunity Zone investments defer and reduce taxes on capital gains. They're complex and require careful planning to maximize benefits.

Should I be concerned about tax audits with advanced planning?

No, if it's done right. We only recommend strategies we'd defend in an IRS audit. Everything is documented and defensible.
