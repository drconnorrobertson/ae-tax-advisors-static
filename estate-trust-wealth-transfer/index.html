Estate, Gift and Trust Tax Planning: 2026 Review

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-01-06 · Updated November 8, 2026.

Estate and trust planning connects ownership, beneficiary choices, transfer taxes and the income-tax consequences for your family. Start with the current documents and asset records before choosing a trust or making a gift.

Use the correct year and jurisdiction

For 2026, the federal basic estate and gift exclusion is $15 million per individual. The annual gift exclusion is $19,000 per recipient for qualifying gifts. These are different rules: a gift above the annual exclusion can require reporting without immediately producing gift tax.

Do not use an old projection that the federal exclusion automatically falls in 2026. State estate and inheritance taxes can apply under different thresholds. Citizenship, residency and cross-border ownership may also change the analysis.

Review portability and prior gifts

A surviving spouse's ability to use a deceased spouse's unused exclusion generally requires a portability election on an estate tax return. Review the actual filing deadline and any available relief; do not assume the election happens automatically or that every missed deadline has the same outcome.

Gather prior gift-tax returns, estate returns and election records. Portability is not a substitute for every trust, asset-protection or generation-skipping transfer-tax objective.

Compare transfer tax with income-tax basis

A lifetime gift and an inheritance can produce different basis results. Appreciated business interests and real estate should be modeled for potential future sale, not only for a reduction in estate value. Prior depreciation, entity structure, debt and special basis rules can affect the result.

A trust does not automatically eliminate tax or guarantee a basis adjustment. The trust terms, retained rights, ownership and applicable law control.

Coordinate business succession and liquidity

Review buy-sell agreements, valuation support, insurance ownership, beneficiary designations and who will control the business if an owner dies or becomes unable to act. Business value should be supported by the facts; no standard minority discount is guaranteed.

Estimate taxes, debt and operating cash needs before deciding which assets to transfer. Keep legal drafting, valuation and tax-return responsibilities clearly assigned.

Define the professional roles

AE Tax Advisors can coordinate the tax analysis with your estate attorney and other advisers. Wills, trust instruments and other legal documents should be prepared or reviewed by qualified counsel. Confirm any tax projections, filings and continuing review in the written engagement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 2026 federal basic estate and gift exclusion?

It is $15 million per individual. Prior taxable gifts, portability and other applicable rules affect the available amount.

Does exceeding the annual gift exclusion always create immediate tax?

No. It may require a gift-tax return and use part of the available lifetime exclusion. Review the gift and applicable rules.

Does portability happen automatically?

Generally an estate tax return must elect portability. Confirm the filing deadline and whether relief is available.
