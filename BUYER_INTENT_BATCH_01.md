# Buyer-intent content: batch 01

Prepared September 25, 2026. These five guides address narrow questions from owners and investors facing material transactions or filing decisions. No search-volume figure is claimed for these exact queries.

| New canonical | Buyer decision |
| --- | --- |
| `/buying-dental-practice-and-building-tax-allocation/` | Allocate a combined practice and real-estate purchase before signing. |
| `/multi-unit-franchise-acquisition-tax-allocation/` | Reconcile unit assets, franchise rights, and the purchase agreement. |
| `/construction-retainage-tax-method-review/` | Determine retainage treatment under the contractor's accounting method. |
| `/self-storage-expansion-cost-segregation-tax-review/` | Scope a study for new units and site work while reconciling replaced assets. |
| `/1031-exchange-replacement-property-new-llc-owner-review/` | Confirm the same federal taxpayer receives replacement property through a new LLC. |

Each guide opens with a buyer question, names the records and tax decisions involved, cites primary IRS guidance, links to a related AE page, and offers a discovery call. The existing `/1031-exchange-guide/` also gained a deadline calendar and a link to the new LLC guide.

Five other draft pages were removed because the current site already answers their questions or the topic requires more source research. They covered seller-note default, assumed customer deposits, phased build-to-rent dates, mixed-use building classification, and landlord-funded tenant improvements. The rejected 500-page industry matrix was removed earlier after the site's scaled-content guard identified duplication.

`research/buyer_question_candidates.tsv` contains 500 distinct candidate questions, split equally between business owners and real estate investors. It is a topic inventory, not 500 approved URLs. Each candidate needs a canonical overlap check, demand evidence, primary-source review, and original analysis before it becomes a page. The target remains 500 useful additions; this batch contributes five.
