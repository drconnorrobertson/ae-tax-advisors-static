Business Tax Return Review: 10-Question Readiness Checklist

Identify records and questions to bring to a second opinion. This checklist does not decide whether your return is wrong, calculate savings or recommend an amendment.

Updated November 6, 2026. Choose Confirmed, Needs review, Unsure or Not applicable for each topic. Responses stay in this page and are not submitted or saved by this checklist. No email is required.

1. Can you identify the taxpayer, entity classification and return for each business and property?

An LLC label does not determine its federal tax classification. Identify ownership changes, elections and which preparer handles each return.

2. Do your books, payroll, K-1s and depreciation schedules reconcile to the filed returns?

Differences need explanation before anyone estimates a refund. Keep prior schedules and carryovers, not only the latest return.

3. Do you have acquisition dates, supported basis, land allocation and a complete depreciation history?

A study may identify a correction or a planning opportunity. Form 3115, an amendment or another procedure depends on the prior treatment and applicable rules.

4. Has your adviser documented participation, grouping and applicable loss limits?

Real estate professional status does not alone make every rental loss nonpassive. Material participation and basis, at-risk and other limitations still matter. Short stays alone do not guarantee usable losses.

5. Is owner compensation supported for the actual entity type and services?

An S corporation requires reasonable compensation for shareholder services. Partnership payments follow different rules. There is no universal salary split or automatic savings from an S election.

6. Do contribution records, employee eligibility and plan deadlines support the claimed treatment?

Traditional and Roth contributions differ. Owner-only plan eligibility, related employers and employee coverage require review. A missed contribution or election cannot always be repaired after filing.

7. Do invoices, business-use records and service dates support depreciation claimed?

Section 179, bonus depreciation and vehicle limitations have separate conditions. Buying a heavy SUV does not automatically allow a full deduction.

8. Do you have records of business purpose and required substantiation?

Personal expenses do not become deductible merely because a business pays them. Home office, vehicle, travel, meals and reimbursement rules require fact-specific review.

9. Has someone identified the available correction route and actual deadline?

Refund periods, method-change procedures, entity elections and partnership AAR rules differ. An amended return is not interchangeable with a superseding return or Form 3115.

10. Is a specific professional assigned to any proposed changes and follow-up?

A written plan, a completed study and a filed return are different deliverables. Confirm fees, scope, responsibilities and secure document exchange before work starts.

What the result means

Any unresolved topic may warrant a defined review, but the number of flagged topics does not measure tax overpayment or eligibility. A review may confirm the existing treatment, suggest future planning, identify a correction or conclude that additional work is not worthwhile. Confirmed answers are not professional validation.

Before authorizing a filing change

Ask the reviewer to identify the affected taxpayer and year, supporting records, available procedure, deadline, estimated effect and implementation cost. For individual refund claims, the general time rule is three years from filing or two years from payment, whichever is later, with payment lookback restrictions and exceptions. It is not simply permission to amend the last three calendar years.

See Form 1040-X instructions, Form 3115 instructions and passive activity and at-risk guidance. Partnership corrections may require a different process.

Use the business tax second-opinion service guide to define the review. Identify your current preparer, entities, main concern and upcoming decision deadline. Arrange secure delivery before sharing returns or financial records.

Frequently asked questions

Does a flagged topic mean I overpaid tax?

No. It identifies an unresolved record or question. It does not establish a refund, filing error or eligibility for a strategy.

Can a review confirm my current return is correct?

Yes. A useful review can validate supported treatment, identify missing evidence or recommend no filing change.

Does this checklist send my answers to AE?

No. Its summary runs locally in this page and does not submit or save the responses. Contacting AE through a separate consultation link is a separate step.

Can every issue be fixed with an amended return?

No. Depending on the facts, a method change, partnership adjustment request, prospective action or no correction may be appropriate. Deadlines and eligibility need separate review.
