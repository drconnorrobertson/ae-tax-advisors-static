Reduced-Basis S-Corp Loan Repayment Review Packet

Pre-payment packet connecting proposed principal repayment to prior basis reductions/restorations and the adviser's written review, without calculating.

Book a Call

Prepare the question before making the payment

Use this packet when an S corporation proposes repaying shareholder debt that may have had tax-basis reductions in earlier loss years. The purpose is to organize the facts for advice before the payment is made. The sheet does not compute taxable gain, authorize payment or decide whether a transfer is principal, interest or a distribution. Keep those questions visible until the appropriate reviewer addresses them.

Collect the relevant history and proposed terms

Identify the particular note or account involved and the proposed payment date. Preserve the proposed principal amount separately from any interest. Locate the prior reviewed loan-basis workpaper and records of subsequent advances, repayments and other relevant activity. Current-year information may still be provisional; label estimates and drafts accordingly. Ask the adviser which additional records are necessary for the proposed timing. A principal balance from the books cannot replace the shareholder’s separate basis history.

Fictional example: a repayment after a loss year

A fictional shareholder wants the company to repay part of an old loan after cash improves. The bookkeeper confirms the outstanding principal, while the shareholder’s prior tax file shows basis reductions. The owner provides both records and asks for review of a proposed payment date. The adviser identifies missing current-year information before offering a conclusion. The owner does not assume that a company liability balance makes the whole payment tax-free, nor does this worksheet recommend delaying or accelerating it.

Keep the advice tied to the facts reviewed

Record the adviser’s response reference and the version of the proposed payment it addressed. If the amount, date or source records change, request an updated review rather than reusing an answer based on different facts. Keep any final payment confirmation separate from the proposal. If the review calls for further analysis, record that next action without entering a guessed tax result. The IRS describes potential consequences for repayment of reduced-basis debt; individual facts still require evaluation.

Handoff checklist

Before a decision, confirm that the packet distinguishes note principal, reviewed tax basis and interest. Identify unreviewed changes since the latest workpaper, including other payments. Ask who will communicate the approved payment instructions and who will record the completed transfer if authorized. Afterward, retain the confirmation with the adviser’s dated analysis. The existing loan repayment workbook can track operational payment records; this narrower packet preserves the tax-basis review surrounding the proposed repayment. Neither document substitutes for the other or guarantees a particular tax outcome. If the corporation proposes several payments, identify which proposal the adviser reviewed. A response addressing one amount on one date should not silently become approval for a later payment series. Keep the decision record specific enough that the bookkeeper can distinguish the reviewed proposal from subsequent changes.
