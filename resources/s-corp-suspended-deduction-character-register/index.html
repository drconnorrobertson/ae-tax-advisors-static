S-Corp Suspended Deduction Character Register

Track the source year and reported character of S-corp suspended deductions without automatically releasing carryovers.

Book a Call

Preserve the type and history of each item

This register organizes S-corporation loss or deduction items reported as limited by basis in earlier workpapers. It keeps the originating year and reported character visible instead of reducing everything to one unexplained suspended-loss total. The worksheet does not decide when a deduction becomes available. It also does not combine basis, at-risk, passive-activity or other limitation analyses into one result.

Use the reviewed schedule as the starting source

Locate the prior preparer’s itemized limitation schedule and connect it to the relevant K-1 and return records. Enter the character exactly as reported, with the source reference beside it. If the file contains only a combined amount, ask for the detail rather than inventing a split. Identify whether an amount is a current-year item, an earlier carryover or an unresolved correction. Keep drafts and estimates labeled so the reviewer can distinguish them from accepted history.

Fictional example: one total conceals two items

A fictional owner provides a spreadsheet showing one suspended amount, while older records separately identify different deduction categories. The preparer logs each available source and asks the adviser to reconcile the categories. They do not treat the entire amount as an ordinary business loss because that is the easiest import field. The reviewer returns an itemized schedule and flags a remaining question about another limitation. The register preserves that unresolved issue instead of marking all deductions usable.

Separate the evidence from a release decision

A later profitable year or cash contribution is a reason to seek review, not a command for this spreadsheet to release every item. Record the current-year information requested by the adviser and the reference to the resulting analysis. Preserve the prior schedule even after a new one is approved. The Form 7203 instructions discuss item character and carryovers under the basis rules, while additional limits can still matter. This resource gathers the source trail rather than applying those rules automatically.

Handoff checklist

Confirm that each row identifies a corporation, shareholder, origin year and source for the stated character. Distinguish missing detail from a confirmed zero carryover. Ask the reviewer which other limitation schedules must remain separate and who will obtain them. If an ownership change or disposition occurred, flag it for advice rather than assuming the register continues unchanged. The existing partnership carryover workbook serves partnership records; this artifact is specifically a shareholder’s S-corporation evidence register. Its completion means the reviewed schedule is traceable, not that the deductions are approved. If an earlier preparer changed an item’s classification, retain the explanation and both versions. A label copied into a later schedule is not a replacement for that history. Ask the adviser which version governs the current review and whether additional source documents are needed before the item is carried forward.

Store sensitive source documents securely and use references in the register rather than account credentials.

  • Corporation/shareholder alias
  • Origin tax year
  • Item character as reported
  • Prior limitation workpaper
  • Current-year source reference
  • Reviewer carryover schedule
  • Review status
  • Unresolved question
