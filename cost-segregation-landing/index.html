What Cost Segregation Actually Does

The IRS lets you reclassify parts of your building -- flooring, cabinetry, landscaping, electrical, plumbing -- into shorter depreciation lives (5, 7, or 15 years instead of 27.5 or 39). That means bigger deductions now, not decades from now.

With bonus depreciation, you can often write off 60-100% of reclassified components in year one. Most property owners save $25,000 to $150,000+ per property.
