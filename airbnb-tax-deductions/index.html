Airbnb Tax Deductions for 2026: Expenses, Depreciation and Records

By AE Tax Advisors

Updated November 8, 2026.

Airbnb hosts may deduct eligible rental expenses, but a booking does not make every property cost deductible. Start with accurate gross receipts, separate personal use, and distinguish operating expenses from assets that must be capitalized. Then determine whether any resulting loss is currently usable.

Reconcile gross bookings before deducting fees

A bank deposit can be net of platform charges, refunds or other adjustments. Reconcile booking reports and information returns to the income reported on the return. If gross receipts include a platform fee, deduct the eligible fee once. Do not report a net deposit and deduct the same withheld fee again. Use the actual platform statement rather than assuming one universal fee percentage.

Repairs, improvements and the de minimis election

A repair label on an invoice does not settle tax treatment. Evaluate betterment, restoration and adaptation for the relevant unit of property. A new roof or a renovation may need capitalization; replacement of a separate appliance needs its own analysis.

The de minimis safe harbor can allow eligible costs to be expensed when its accounting and election requirements are met. It is not automatic, and missing it does not mean every small cost must be depreciated: other expense rules may apply.

Furniture, building basis and cost segregation

Separate land from depreciable property and avoid counting an asset twice. A cost segregation study supports eligible component classifications; the IRS does not preapprove every study or promise a standard reclassification percentage. Existing-property corrections may use Form 3115 when an eligible method change applies, while other errors need a different procedure.

Review acquisition dates, service dates, prior elections and business use before applying bonus depreciation or Section 179. A new study does not give an older asset today's bonus percentage. See cost segregation for Airbnb properties and Form 3115 implementation.

Travel and 2026 mileage

Qualifying rental-business travel must be separated from commuting and personal travel. Under the standard-mileage method, the 2026 business rates are 72.5 cents per mile for January through June and 76 cents for July through December. Keep dates, miles and business purpose; do not also deduct the same vehicle's fuel and depreciation. Lodging and meals have separate requirements and limits. See the rental-travel guide.

Personal use can limit deductions

Track fair-rental days and personal-use days separately. When a dwelling is treated as a home, special expense-allocation and loss-limitation rules can apply. The commonly cited 14-day rental exclusion requires a dwelling used as a residence and rented for fewer than 15 days; it is not a general exclusion for every rental property. Under that rule, rental expenses are not deducted as rental expenses.

Schedule E, Schedule C and the STR loss test

Rental real estate is generally reported on Schedule E. Substantial services primarily for guests' convenience can require Schedule C treatment. A short average stay alone does not decide that reporting question.

The short-term rental passive-loss exception has separate requirements, including material participation where applicable. Basis, at-risk, personal-use and excess-business-loss rules can still limit a loss. A tax loss also does not establish positive cash flow.

Bring a complete review file

Gather booking statements, receipts, mortgage and tax records, management reports, the personal-use calendar, acquisition documents and prior depreciation schedules. Identify who will prepare the study, any method-change application and the return.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Airbnb expenses are tax deductible?

Eligible rental costs can include interest, taxes, insurance, cleaning, supplies, fees and depreciation. Personal use, capitalization, substantiation and deduction limits must be applied.

Can I deduct all furniture immediately?

Not automatically. Review the asset, safe-harbor election, depreciation rules, business use and owner-level limitations.

Is a cost segregation study IRS approved?

A study must support the applicable tax treatment. The IRS does not preapprove each study or guarantee a percentage or refund.

Does renting fewer than 15 days make all rental income tax-free?

The special exclusion requires a dwelling used as a residence, as well as fewer than 15 rental days. It is not a universal rule for every property.

Does an Airbnb tax loss offset my salary?

Only if the relevant passive-activity and other deduction requirements are satisfied. A large depreciation number by itself does not establish usability.
