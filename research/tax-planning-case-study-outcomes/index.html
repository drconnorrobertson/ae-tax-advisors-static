Methodology

  1. We separated the 75 pre-existing case-study pages from 480 deterministic scenario-template pages. Scenario pages are excluded from every statistic on this page.
  2. We included only original pages whose client-profile table contains the field Annual Tax Savings followed by one unqualified dollar amount.
  3. We excluded 21 original pages reporting deductions, offsets, deferrals, recoveries, mixed time periods, or no comparable annual measure.
  4. We calculated the median, arithmetic mean, quartiles, range, and distribution directly from the 54 included headline figures.
  5. We did not independently audit the underlying returns, realization of the savings, or the counterfactual calculation.

How to Interpret the Findings

  • Use the median as a description of this selected cohort, not an expected client result.
  • Larger outcomes often accompany higher income, larger asset basis, multiple entities, or several coordinated strategies.
  • A deduction amount is not the same as tax savings, which is why deduction-only studies were excluded.
  • Deferral is not permanent savings and was excluded from the comparable annual cohort.
  • Every proposed strategy still requires fact-specific tax, legal, substantiation, and implementation review.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the median reported annual tax savings in these case studies?

The median across the 54-case benchmark cohort is $71,500. This describes the published cohort and is not a prediction for a prospective client.

How many case studies are included?

The statistical cohort includes 54 original case studies with one plain annual tax-savings figure. It excludes 21 other original studies whose headline measures were deductions, deferrals, offsets, recoveries, or did not provide a comparable annual figure. It also excludes 480 templated scenarios.

Are these results guaranteed?

No. Tax outcomes depend on income, marginal rates, basis, entity structure, participation, substantiation, timing, state law, and implementation. No case-study result guarantees a similar outcome.

Can the underlying data be downloaded?

Yes. The included rows and methodology are available as JSON and CSV.
