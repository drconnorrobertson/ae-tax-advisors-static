Short-Term Rental Tax Planning for Airbnb and VRBO Owners

Connect the way you operate your short-term rental to a practical tax plan. Review guest stays, participation, property records and filing responsibilities before relying on a projected deduction.

Book a Call

Tax planning and preparation for short-term rental owners

AE Tax Advisors helps property owners coordinate STR strategy with their wider business and real estate tax position. If you are searching for a short-term rental CPA or Airbnb tax accountant, compare who will analyze the activity, prepare the return and implement depreciation work. Those responsibilities should be explicit in the proposal.

This review is useful before buying or opening a property, changing management arrangements, completing a renovation, or filing a return that includes a new study. An existing owner may also need to reconcile prior depreciation or suspended losses.

Start with the rental operations

A platform listing does not determine federal tax treatment. Review actual booking history, guest services, owner stays and the work performed by the owner, spouse and other people. IRS Publication 527 explains rental reporting and personal-use considerations.

Separate the seven-day rule from material participation

For passive-activity purposes, average customer use of seven days or less is one exception to rental-activity treatment. It does not, by itself, establish material participation or make a loss deductible against other income. Evaluate the relevant participation test and other applicable limitations separately. See IRS Publication 925.

Use the STR seven-day rule guide for the tax-rule discussion. For an engagement, prepare booking exports and a record of actual work rather than selecting a desired tax result first.

Coordinate cost segregation with the tax return

A depreciation study belongs in the same file as the purchase documents, land allocation, furnishings and improvement history. Confirm which components are eligible, which tax-year rules apply, and whether additional deductions are currently usable. The study cannot answer the participation question.

Published standard cost segregation pricing is $1 per square foot, with a $2,000 minimum per study. Complex work may be quoted separately. Advisory, Form 3115 preparation and tax returns require their own confirmed scope. Compare study pricing and deliverables.

Review Airbnb cost segregation, the property document checklist and lookback implementation before setting the scope.

What to confirm in an STR engagement

Ask for a proposal that assigns the operational review, depreciation work, owner-level loss analysis, federal and state return preparation, and follow-up support. Identify each property and ownership entity. If another CPA files your return, establish how the analysis and asset schedule will reach that preparer.

  • Property type, ownership entity, acquisition date and opening date.
  • Number of properties, states and upcoming purchases or sales.
  • Current tax preparer and whether a cost segregation report already exists.
  • Availability of booking exports, participation records and prior asset schedules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you help owners who use a property manager?

Yes, the planning discussion can include managed properties. Management arrangements affect the participation analysis; hiring a manager does not establish a particular tax outcome.

Is cost segregation included in advisory?

No. AE publishes cost segregation separately from tax advisory. Confirm report scope, implementation and return fees in writing.

Can short-term rental losses offset W-2 income?

That depends on the activity, participation and applicable loss limits. A short stay or depreciation study alone does not establish eligibility.

Can I keep my current CPA?

Confirm a coordination arrangement that names who performs each analysis, updates the asset schedule and files the return.

What should I bring to the discovery call?

Bring a summary of your properties, ownership, timing and current tax support. Use the agreed secure process for detailed tax documents.
