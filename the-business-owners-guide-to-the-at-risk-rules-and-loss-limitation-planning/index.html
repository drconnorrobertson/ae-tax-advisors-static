At-Risk Rules for Business and Rental Losses: Section 465

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2023-06-30 · Updated November 8, 2026.

The at-risk rules can limit a loss even when a taxpayer has tax basis. Calculate the amount economically at risk in the specific activity rather than assuming the property's value or mortgage balance establishes the allowable deduction.

Start with the actual investment

Review cash and the applicable basis of property contributed, borrowing, distributions and prior income or losses. Purchase price, current market value and outside basis are different concepts. Do not add the same investment twice.

Examine debt terms

Personal liability and certain pledged property can affect at-risk amounts, subject to the rules. Financing protected against loss or involving specified related parties may not qualify. A partnership debt allocation alone does not answer the Section 465 question.

Ordinary nonrecourse debt generally does not make the borrower at risk. Qualified nonrecourse financing for eligible real-property holding activities is an exception with lender, security and other conditions. Not every nonrecourse mortgage qualifies.

Do not treat a guarantee as an automatic addition

Review who ultimately bears the economic loss, rights of reimbursement, other obligors and the guarantee's actual terms. Signing a guarantee does not establish the same at-risk amount in every entity or financing arrangement.

S corporation shareholder debt basis has its own requirements. A corporate bank loan with a shareholder guarantee generally does not create direct shareholder debt basis merely because the guarantee exists.

Apply the limitation sequence

For an owner of a pass-through entity, first determine the applicable entity-basis limit, then at-risk limits, passive-activity rules and any other applicable restrictions. The excess-business-loss limitation can matter after the earlier tests.

A loss can be suspended under one rule even if another would permit it. Keep separate schedules rather than recording one unexplained carryforward balance.

Track changes after the deduction year

Distributions, refinancing, debt changes and protection against loss can alter the amount at risk. A decrease can require recapture under the applicable rules. Review the history before taking cash out or changing financing.

Request a loss-limit review with the loan agreements, guarantees, basis schedules, prior Forms 6198 and 8582, and the proposed transaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is tax basis the same as the amount at risk?

No. Debt and other items can be treated differently under the two rules.

Does every nonrecourse real-estate loan qualify?

No. Qualified nonrecourse financing has specific requirements.

Does a guarantee always permit the loss?

No. Review the economic obligation and the separate entity-basis and other loss limits.
