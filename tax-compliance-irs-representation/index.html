3-Year Tax Lookback: Find What Your Prior CPA Missed

Our 3-Year Tax Lookback regularly uncovers significant errors from prior preparers. Common issues include: consulting income classified as "Other Income" instead of Schedule C (missing SE tax implications and S-Corp opportunity), missed depreciation on rental properties, failure to claim home office or vehicle deductions, and overlooked education or retirement credits. Most business owners assume their returns were filed correctly. In our experience, the majority of new clients have at least one material error in their prior three years of returns.

Amended Returns and Refund Recovery

We have recovered $30,000-$50,000 for individual clients through amended returns, and our Form 3115 filings for missed depreciation have generated single-year catch-up deductions exceeding $300,000. Amended returns can be filed for any open tax year (generally 3 years from the original filing date), and the refund claim process is straightforward when properly documented. If you have rental properties that never had cost segregation, business expenses that were never deducted, or income that was misclassified, there is likely money sitting with the IRS that belongs to you.

Income Misclassification: A Costly and Common Error

Income misclassification is more common than most business owners realize. We have seen consulting income reported as "Other Income" on Line 8 of Schedule 1 instead of Schedule C, which not only causes SE tax miscalculation but also prevents the client from considering an S-Corp election that could save $20,000-$40,000 per year in self-employment tax. We have also seen rental income reported on the wrong schedules, capital gains mischaracterized as ordinary income, and K-1 income allocated incorrectly. Each of these errors compounds over time and creates both overpayment risk and audit exposure. Our lookback process catches these issues and corrects them through properly documented amended returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I get an IRS audit notice?

Do not respond on your own. Contact us immediately. We will review the notice, assess your exposure, and represent you throughout the audit process.

Can you help me if I owe back taxes?

Yes. We evaluate your situation and pursue the best resolution path, whether that is an installment agreement, Offer in Compromise, penalty abatement, or Currently Not Collectible status.

How far back can the IRS audit?

Generally 3 years from filing, but 6 years if there is a substantial understatement of income (>25%). There is no limit for fraud or unfiled returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does IRS representation cost?

It depends on complexity. Typically flat fee for simple audits, hourly for complex cases. The cost is usually a fraction of what audits cost without representation.

Can I appeal an IRS decision?

Yes. We can file a protest to IRS Appeals or pursue litigation if necessary. Most taxpayers settle at Appeals level.

What's the difference between a tax return and tax compliance?

A return shows what you owe. Compliance ensures you've met all filing, payment, and documentation requirements. We handle both.

What records should I keep for an audit?

Keep receipts, invoices, bank statements, and detailed documentation for all deductions claimed. The 'contemporaneous written acknowledgment' is critical for charitable deductions.
