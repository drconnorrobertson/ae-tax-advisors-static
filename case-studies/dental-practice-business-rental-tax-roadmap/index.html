A Dental Practice Owner Coordinates Business and Rental Planning

Coordinating the practice and rentals helps prioritize the next decision by usable benefit rather than deduction size.

Book a Call

The Starting Point

A dental practice owner had both practice income and rental investments. The business and property decisions needed to be considered together, with attention to compensation, state elections and whether rental deductions could be used.

The Documented AE Work

AE prepared a proposed roadmap covering an entity-level state tax election, business reimbursements, compensation and rental-property planning. The value of the review was the connection between the operating practice, rental holdings and the household return.

  • Evaluate available state tax elections using the entity’s actual facts.
  • Review compensation and properly documented business reimbursements.
  • Assess rental depreciation together with loss limitations.

Where the Work Stands

The available document is a tax plan with projections. This summary does not report completed elections or verified savings.

The Next Opportunity to Evaluate

Build a property-by-property loss-usage model before ordering more depreciation studies. Confirm ownership, basis, at-risk amounts, participation and applicable state treatment so projected deductions are tied to the return where they can actually be used.

What to Gather Before Taking That Step

Property depreciation schedules, ownership records, participation records and current-year federal and state projections.

Why This Approach Matters

Coordinating the practice and rentals helps prioritize the next decision by usable benefit rather than deduction size.
