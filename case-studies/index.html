250 tax planning case studies for business owners and real estate investors. These distinguish documented plans and engagement scopes from completed work, with an additional opportunity to evaluate in each story.

Every documented case study below reflects the kind of work we do: cost segregation and depreciation strategy, entity design, reasonable compensation, retirement plan structuring, pass-through entity tax elections, and prior year recovery. All studies are anonymized, all figures are rounded, and no client names or identifying details appear anywhere. Strategies depend entirely on facts and circumstances and are not universal recommendations. Educational case studies use illustrative facts and figures and do not document specific client results.
