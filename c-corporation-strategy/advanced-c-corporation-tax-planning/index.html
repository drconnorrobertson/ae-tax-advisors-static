Advanced C Corporation Tax Planning: Related Entities and Retained Earnings

By AE Tax Advisors · Published 2026-06-09 · Updated November 8, 2026.

Advanced C corporation planning starts with the existing operations and ownership. Adding a holding company or service entity can change taxes and reporting, but a separate legal entity does not automatically create a deduction, eliminate a tax layer or move income out of a state.

Map the business before the structure

Document the employees, assets, customers, intellectual property, financing and risks in each entity. Identify which activity earns the income and which entity pays each expense. Model owner cash needs and a future sale alongside annual business taxes.

The federal corporate rate does not represent the owner's final tax on distributed earnings. Include shareholder taxation, state rules, administrative cost and the timing of cash distributions.

Support transactions between related companies

Management fees, rents, loans and royalties require real transactions and supportable terms. Section 482 permits adjustments between commonly controlled taxpayers to reflect income properly. An agreement and invoice do not prove that a service was performed or that its price was appropriate.

Maintain evidence of the work, pricing method, comparable terms, actual payment and business purpose. Review deduction timing and related-party rules in addition to price.

Review group rules separately

Common ownership can trigger controlled-group or affiliated-service-group rules. These can affect benefit plans and tax limitations. The applicable ownership tests vary by provision; one simplified ownership percentage does not answer every question.

A dividends-received deduction and a consolidated return are also distinct issues. Ownership, holding periods, debt financing, eligibility, elections and other limitations require review. Neither treatment follows automatically from calling a company a subsidiary.

Analyze state activity and reporting

A no-income-tax-state address does not by itself remove another state's taxing jurisdiction. Employees, property, sales, economic nexus, combined reporting, apportionment and related-party addbacks can affect the result.

Calculate both entities' state consequences before treating an intercompany payment as a saving. Include registrations and recurring compliance costs.

Document why earnings are retained

Accumulated earnings tax and personal holding company tax are different regimes. Review ownership, the character of income, distributions and the applicable computations. They should not be reduced to a blanket rule that any balance above a fixed dollar amount is prohibited.

Keep specific budgets, financing needs and business plans supporting retained funds. Board minutes should reflect the actual plan; they do not automatically eliminate exposure.

Check current credits and international provisions

Research credits require qualifying activities, costs and substantiation. A broad industry label or annual spending total is not enough to establish the credit. Coordinate expense treatment, elections and any payroll-tax credit eligibility.

Cross-border ownership and foreign-market income require a tax-year-specific analysis. Section 250 provisions changed under 2025 legislation; older GILTI and FDII effective-rate examples should not be reused for 2026 without checking current law, definitions and limitations.

Compare a proposed structure with the current business

  • Annual federal, state and owner taxes.
  • Cash movement and distribution tax.
  • Related-party documentation and group rules.
  • Benefit, credit and financing effects.
  • Exit, conversion and implementation costs.

Continue to business succession and exit planning, review corporation planning examples, or return to the C corporation guide. Request a structure review with ownership charts and recent returns.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a holding company automatically reduce tax?

No. Analyze actual activity, related-party pricing, group rules, owner distributions and state treatment.

Can old international effective-rate examples be used for 2026?

Not without checking the applicable 2025 legislation, current definitions and tax-year-specific rules.
