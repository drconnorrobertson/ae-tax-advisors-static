Cost Segregation Studies in Florida for Rental Property

Residential rentals, Airbnb and other short-term rentals, long-term rentals, and small to mid-sized multifamily.

Discuss Your Rental Property

Rental-property study coverage in Florida

AE Tax Advisors provides nationwide cost segregation studies coordinated around your property's records and tax situation. Florida has no state income tax, so all rental income, capital gains, and depreciation recapture flow through to federal returns without state taxation. The effective property tax rate averages 0.80%, and the statewide sales tax is 6.0% with local surtaxes pushing combined rates to 6.5-8.5%. Florida charges a 6% state Tourist Development Tax plus local county surcharges that vary from 2-6% additional, making combined lodging taxes 10-13% in most counties. Florida fully conforms to federal MACRS depreciation and bonus depreciation under the OBBBA.

Orlando and Central Florida

Orlando's median home price runs approximately $380,000. The vacation rental corridor along US-192, in Kissimmee, Davenport, and Champions Gate, is one of the largest STR markets in the country. Properties near Disney World, Universal Studios, and SeaWorld produce $40,000-$80,000+ in gross annual revenue for well-furnished 4-6BR vacation homes. Osceola County's combined Tourist Development Tax is approximately 12.5%. Short-term rental homes in resort communities like Reunion, Storey Lake, and Windsor at Westside are purpose-built for STR use, with community amenities that qualify as 15-year land improvements. A 5BR vacation home in Davenport purchased for $450,000 with $45,000 in furnishings typically yields $180,000-$220,000 in eligible cost segregation basis.

Miami and South Florida

Miami's median home price runs approximately $550,000-$600,000, with Miami Beach, Wynwood, Brickell, and Coconut Grove commanding premium pricing. Miami Beach enforces strict STR regulations with a minimum 6-month rental period in most residential zones, while unincorporated Miami-Dade County allows STRs with a Certificate of Use. Fort Lauderdale (median ~$420,000) is more STR-friendly with a registration and licensing process. The combined Tourist Development Tax in Miami-Dade is 13%. The Palm Beach County corridor (Delray Beach, Boca Raton, Jupiter) offers luxury rental demand from seasonal residents and golf tourism.

Tampa Bay

Tampa's median home price runs approximately $380,000-$420,000. St. Petersburg, Clearwater Beach, and the Gulf beaches of Pinellas County are the region's top STR performers, with beachfront condos and cottages generating $50,000-$90,000 annually. Hillsborough County's combined Tourist Tax is 12%. The Seminole Heights, Ybor City, and Channel District neighborhoods in Tampa proper attract business traveler and event-driven STR demand. Anna Maria Island and Siesta Key (Sarasota County) are premium beach vacation rental markets with median prices of $800,000-$1.5M.

Gulf Coast and Panhandle

The Emerald Coast (Destin, 30A, Panama City Beach, Fort Walton Beach) is Florida's highest-performing beach STR corridor outside South Florida. Walton County (30A/Destin) combined Tourist Tax runs 11.5%. Properties along Scenic Highway 30A (Rosemary Beach, Seaside, WaterColor, Alys Beach) command $500-$1,000+ per night in peak season. Gulf Shores-adjacent markets in Northwest Florida produce strong seasonal revenue from Memorial Day through Labor Day. Naples and Marco Island (Collier County) serve a luxury snowbird market with median home prices above $600,000.

Jacksonville and Northeast Florida

Jacksonville's median home price of approximately $310,000 makes it one of Florida's most affordable metros. The Beaches communities (Jacksonville Beach, Neptune Beach, Atlantic Beach, Ponte Vedra) and the San Marco and Riverside neighborhoods drive STR demand. St. Augustine, one of the oldest cities in the US, has strong tourism-driven STR performance year-round. Duval County's combined Tourist Tax is 12%.

Choose the review that fits your property

  • Airbnb and short-term rentals: review furnishings, basis, personal use, and participation records.
  • Long-term residential rentals: evaluate passive income and suspended losses before projecting immediate savings.
  • Duplexes, triplexes, and fourplexes: reconcile shared systems, unit improvements, and any owner-occupied portion.
  • Small multifamily: reconcile buildings, common areas, site assets, and each renovation.

The documents that drive a defensible estimate

  1. Closing statement, purchase agreement, land-allocation support, and ownership records.
  2. Original and current depreciation schedules, service dates, and prior elections.
  3. Construction, renovation, and furnishing invoices, photographs, and plans.
  4. Booking and personal-use records, participation records, and management agreements where relevant.
  5. Tax residency, entity classification, passive income, expected holding period, and planned sale or exchange.

A city name or purchase price alone cannot establish the classification or deduction. Use the complete document checklist before requesting a proposal.

Separate federal depreciation from state treatment

The federal study and the Florida return are separate parts of implementation. The analysis should identify the owner's residency, ownership entity, source of income, relevant tax year, and any state or local filing obligations. Do not assume the federal bonus deduction transfers unchanged to every state return, or that a property location determines the owner's entire tax treatment.

Current federal rules generally permit 100% bonus depreciation for qualifying property acquired and placed in service after January 19, 2025, subject to the applicable requirements and elections. Older properties require the rules for their original dates. The building shell and land do not become bonus-eligible simply because a study is completed.

Have the return preparer confirm the current state instructions and retain separate adjustment schedules where required.

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve rental owners throughout Florida?

Yes. AE Tax Advisors coordinates rental-property cost segregation and tax advisory nationwide, including Orlando and Kissimmee, Miami and Fort Lauderdale, Tampa and St. Petersburg, Destin and Panama City Beach. Service coverage does not imply an office in each market.

What does an AE study cost?

Published standard study pricing is $1 per square foot with a $2,000 minimum per study. Confirm complex work, filing responsibilities, and any additional fees in the written engagement.

Does a study automatically offset W-2 income?

No. The study classifies property costs. Current deduction usability depends on activity classification, participation, basis, at-risk rules, and other loss limitations.

Can you review an older rental?

A prior-year review starts with historical basis, service dates, elections, and depreciation actually claimed. The preparer must choose the applicable correction method, which may involve Form 3115 rather than amended returns.
