# AE buyer-intent publishing workflow

Adapted from the BNB buyer-quality concept for AE Tax Advisors on October 7, 2026.

Prioritize people actively selecting tax advisory services: fees and complete project cost, advisor selection, written scope, preparer coordination, engagement timing, and relevant business-owner or investor decisions. Brand queries remain a priority. Read existing substantive answers before creating a URL; improve a matching existing article instead of competing with it. Do not use keyword, city, year or provider swaps to meet a volume target.

Score each candidate: distinct intent 25; imminent buyer relevance 30; evidence of a real question 15; AE service fit 20; original decision support 10. Require at least 85 total, 27 buyer relevance and 17 service fit. Record reasons, sources, overlap decisions and a practical tool or worked example. Scores are editorial judgments, not Google scores or ranking forecasts. A score never overrides an unresolved factual or technical problem.

Each substantive article needs a concrete decision, useful explanation, sources for changing claims, limitations, and a next step appropriate to the reader. Use current primary sources for tax or legal claims. Keep examples hypothetical unless verified client evidence is authorized. Do not promise refunds, savings, deadlines or service inclusions unsupported by the written scope. Keep branded links and commercial calls to action on AE; primary-source citations remain appropriate.

Preserve existing URLs and original publication dates when refreshing. Update modified dates only for substantive work. Include a self canonical, one H1, unique metadata, relevant breadcrumbs, accurate authorship, matching visible FAQ answers and contextual links. Preserve the homepage and established booking flow. Review the entire scoped diff.

For each completed batch: run the public build and relevant repository checks, verify internal destinations, JSON-LD and sitemap parity, then commit, create a reviewable PR and publish the validated work. Verify the actual live changed pages before IndexNow submission and GSC sitemap submission. Submission acceptance is discovery evidence, not proof of indexing. Record deployment, live validation and submission results.

Measure later using confirmed URL inspection where available, relevant query impressions and clicks, and qualified enquiries when supported by connected analytics. Do not invent conversions or infer deindexing from no impressions. This file documents the workflow; it does not schedule autonomous runs.
