Understanding Indiana's Tax Environment

Indiana offers one of the lowest flat income tax rates in the nation at 3.05%, making it highly attractive for business owners. County income taxes add an additional 0.5% to 2.9% depending on location. Indiana conforms to federal depreciation rules. The state provides a significant manufacturing tax environment with various credits and exemptions. Indiana does not impose an estate or inheritance tax. The state's low tax rates, combined with a reasonable cost of living and strong manufacturing base, make it competitive for business expansion and relocation.

For business owners earning $200,000 or more, the interplay between federal and Indiana state taxes creates both complexity and opportunity. The right combination of entity structure, retirement plan funding, real estate strategies, and timing decisions can produce dramatic differences in your total tax bill. The key is working with an advisor who understands both the federal code and Indiana's specific rules well enough to optimize across both layers.

Core Tax Planning Services for Indiana Business Owners

Entity Structure Optimization

The choice between operating as a sole proprietorship, single-member LLC, multi-member LLC, S corporation, or C corporation has enormous tax consequences. Many Indiana business owners are operating under the wrong entity structure simply because their CPA set it up years ago and never revisited the decision. We analyze your current and projected income, self-employment tax exposure, retirement plan goals, and state tax implications to determine the optimal structure. For many business owners earning over $100,000 in net business income, an S corporation election can save $15,000 to $40,000 per year in self-employment taxes alone.

S-Corp Reasonable Compensation Analysis

If you operate an S corporation, the IRS requires you to pay yourself a reasonable salary before taking distributions. Set it too high and you overpay in payroll taxes. Set it too low and you invite an IRS audit. We perform a documented reasonable compensation analysis using industry data, geographic factors, and your specific role to establish a defensible salary that minimizes your payroll tax burden while staying within IRS guidelines. This single strategy typically saves our Indiana clients between $10,000 and $30,000 per year.

Retirement Plan Design and Maximization

Most business owners are dramatically underutilizing retirement plans as a tax reduction tool. Beyond the standard 401(k), we design and implement cash balance plans, defined benefit plans, and combination structures that allow business owners to defer $100,000 to $350,000 or more per year in tax-deductible contributions. For a Indiana business owner in the combined top federal and state bracket, a $200,000 cash balance plan contribution can reduce current-year taxes by $70,000 to $90,000.

Cost Segregation Studies

If you own rental property or commercial real estate in Indiana, a cost segregation study can accelerate hundreds of thousands of dollars in depreciation deductions into the current tax year. Under current bonus depreciation rules, qualifying components identified in a cost segregation study can be depreciated 100% in the year placed in service. We provide engineering-based, audit-ready cost segregation studies for properties throughout Indiana, priced at $1 per square foot with a $2,000 minimum. Learn more in our complete cost segregation guide.

Real Estate Tax Strategy

Real estate investors in Indiana face complex tax questions around passive activity rules, material participation, real estate professional status, 1031 exchanges, and the interaction between rental losses and other income. We build comprehensive real estate tax strategies that coordinate across your entire portfolio, maximizing current deductions while planning for long-term wealth building. Our real estate tax planning services cover everything from single rental properties to large portfolios.

Multi-Year Tax Projections

Tax planning is not a once-a-year exercise. We create rolling three-year tax projections that model different scenarios for income growth, entity changes, property acquisitions, retirement plan contributions, and timing decisions. This forward-looking approach allows Indiana business owners to make decisions with full visibility into the tax consequences before committing, rather than discovering expensive surprises at filing time.

If you receive an IRS notice, audit letter, or state tax inquiry, our team of enrolled agents and CPAs can represent you directly before the IRS and Indiana state tax authorities. We handle correspondence audits, office audits, field audits, appeals, and collections matters. Tax Return Preparation and Amended Returns

Beyond strategy, we handle the full spectrum of tax return preparation for Indiana business owners, including individual returns (Form 1040), S corporation returns (Form 1120-S), C corporation returns (Form 1120), partnership returns (Form 1065), and trust and estate returns (Form 1041).
