Rental property travel record Workbook

Organize rental property travel record with AE Tax Advisors’ editable workbook, evidence checklist and CSV export. Prepare the records for review.

When to use this workbook

Use this rental property travel record workbook to connect trip purpose, property visits and travel evidence before the next review. Keep the three records distinct so a missing item is visible.

Reconcile the lease or manager statement to receipts, expenses and bank activity. Keep costs attached to the correct property and reporting period.

Review checkpoint: A manager’s net remittance is not necessarily the full income and expense picture. Keep gross collections, withheld charges and owner transfers separately visible.

Turn the record into a review handoff

For rental property travel record, start the handoff with the connection between trip purpose and property visits. Then identify what travel evidence still needs to establish. The exported sheet should make that relationship clear without replacing the original documents.

List the person collecting evidence separately from the professional deciding treatment. A blank field is a request for follow-up, not a zero, an approval or a concluded answer. Keep the supporting documents in your agreed secure record system; this public page accepts no document uploads.
