QBI Deduction for 2026: Income Limits, Rentals and Owner Pay

By AE Tax Advisors

Updated November 8, 2026.

The Section 199A qualified business income deduction can reduce taxable income for eligible owners of pass-through businesses. It is a deduction, not a 20% tax credit or a guaranteed 20% reduction in your tax rate. Employee wages and income earned through a C corporation do not qualify as QBI.

Start with qualified income

Business profit is the starting point, not necessarily the final QBI amount. Owner wages, guaranteed payments and specified investment items are treated differently. Obtain the business's Section 199A statement and reconcile allocable deductions and prior QBI losses before estimating the deduction.

2026 taxable-income thresholds

These thresholds use taxable income before the QBI deduction. They are not gross-revenue or adjusted-gross-income limits.

2026 Section 199A phase-in ranges
Filing statusThresholdEnd of range
Married filing jointly$403,500$553,500
Married filing separately$201,775$276,775
All other returns$201,750$276,750

Above the threshold, wage and qualified-property limits phase in for qualifying businesses. Specified service trade or business restrictions also phase in; a non-service business does not simply lose all eligibility at the end of the range. Use the current form instructions for the calculation.

What does a 20% deduction mean?

In a simplified example with $100,000 of QBI, a 20% calculation starts at $20,000. That is a potential deduction, not $20,000 of tax saved. The final amount depends on taxable income, net capital gain, other business items and applicable limitations. Eligible active businesses also have a new minimum-deduction rule for years beginning after 2025: the 2026 minimum is $400 when the statutory conditions, including at least $1,000 of aggregate QBI from businesses in which the taxpayer materially participates, are met.

Do rental properties qualify?

Rental income can qualify when the activity is a Section 162 trade or business or satisfies an applicable alternative rule. The rental real estate safe harbor requires more than 250 hours: separate books, qualifying services, records and a return statement are part of the analysis. The hours test depends on how long the enterprise has existed. Certain arrangements, including triple-net leases and property used as a residence, are excluded from that safe harbor.

Failing the safe harbor does not automatically mean the rental fails the underlying trade-or-business test. This test is also separate from real estate professional status and passive-loss material participation.

Coordinate salary, deductions and business structure

An S-corporation owner's reasonable compensation is not optional. Changing salary affects payroll taxes, retirement-plan capacity and potentially the QBI calculation. Buying equipment or funding retirement contributions may reduce taxable income but can also reduce QBI; model the completed return instead of assuming each deduction stacks independently.

Separating a service activity into another entity does not automatically avoid the specified-service restrictions. Related-business rules and the actual services matter. Compare total tax and implementation cost before changing an entity.

What to bring to a QBI review

  • Business returns, K-1s and Section 199A statements.
  • Owner payroll, retirement and health-insurance deductions.
  • Qualified-property records and business ownership details.
  • Prior QBI loss carryforwards and expected capital gains.
  • Rental books, service records and prior safe-harbor statements.

The deduction generally does not reduce AGI or self-employment tax. AE can review how it interacts with your business and household return. Book a planning call with the relevant records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the QBI deduction a 20% tax credit?

No. It reduces taxable income when available. The tax saving depends on your completed return and applicable rates.

Does S-corp owner salary qualify as QBI?

Reasonable compensation paid to an S-corporation shareholder is excluded from QBI. Remaining eligible business income and wage limitations require a separate calculation.

Is 250 rental-service hours enough for the safe harbor?

No. The safe harbor also has books, records, statement and property eligibility requirements. The applicable hours test depends on the enterprise history.

Does QBI reduce adjusted gross income?

Generally no. The deduction is calculated after AGI and does not reduce self-employment tax.
