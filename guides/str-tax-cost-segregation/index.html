Three distinctions that prevent expensive mistakes

  • Seven-day average stays and material participation are separate tests; Schedule C and self-employment tax are separate questions too.
  • A new study does not reset old acquisition dates, original bonus rates, asset basis or passive-loss carryforwards.
  • A deduction is not tax savings, and a computed deduction is not necessarily usable this year.
