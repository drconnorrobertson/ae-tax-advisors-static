Rental Property Tax Questions: A Practical Guide

Find the tax treatment, documentation, and common errors for 50 situations rental owners encounter.

Request Your Free Tax Assessment

How to use this guide

Rental income, basis, improvements, short stays, refinancing, and sales are connected. Use these focused articles to classify an actual transaction, then return to the broader real estate tax planning guide for the full portfolio context. Start with the question that matches your facts. Each article identifies a decision, records to keep, an illustrative example, and a primary IRS reference.

For a broader plan, see Real estate tax planning.

Rental receipts and acquisition

  • Is a rental security deposit taxable when received?
    A refundable tenant deposit is generally a liability rather than rent when the landlord expects to return it; an amount kept for unpaid rent or damage becomes income when retained.
  • How is prepaid rent taxed to a rental owner?
    Advance rent is generally included in rental income when received, even if it covers a later occupancy period; a refundable security deposit has different treatment.
  • Are expenses paid by a tenant taxable to the landlord?
    When a tenant pays an expense that is the landlord's obligation, the payment is generally rental income to the owner and the owner may separately deduct an eligible expense.
  • How are tenant services in exchange for rent taxed?
    The fair market value of services a tenant provides instead of rent is generally rental income, with the related expenditure analyzed separately for deduction or capitalization.
  • Is a lease cancellation payment rental income?
    A payment received from a tenant for cancelling a lease is ordinarily rental income to the property owner.
  • Can landlords deduct utilities they pay for tenants?
    Utilities paid by a rental owner are generally deductible rental operating expenses when the property is held for rent; amounts reimbursed by tenants are rental income.
  • Can you deduct expenses while a rental sits vacant?
    Ordinary expenses may remain deductible while a property is vacant if it is genuinely held out for rent; personal use and improvements require separate analysis.
  • When does rental-property depreciation start?
    Depreciation begins when the property is ready and available for its intended rental use, not necessarily when a tenant first moves in or when the purchase closes.
  • How do you split a rental purchase between land and building?
    Land is not depreciable, so the purchase price and qualifying acquisition costs must be allocated between land and depreciable improvements on a reasonable basis.
  • Which rental-property closing costs add to basis?
    Costs directly tied to acquiring the property generally add to basis; loan costs and prorated operating items may receive different treatment.

Improvements and property costs

  • How are loan origination fees on a rental deducted?
    Borrowing costs are generally recovered over the loan term rather than treated as the building's depreciable basis or a full current deduction.
  • Can rental-property mortgage points be deducted at closing?
    Points on a loan for rental property are generally spread over the loan term, unlike the limited immediate deduction sometimes available for a principal residence.
  • Is a replacement roof on a rental an expense or improvement?
    Replacing a roof is generally a capital improvement recovered through depreciation; isolated repairs to a small damaged area can differ.
  • How do landlords depreciate replacement appliances?
    A separately identifiable appliance placed in a rental may have a different recovery period from the building; the acquisition and placed-in-service facts determine treatment.
  • Is a new fence on a rental property deductible immediately?
    A new fence is generally a land improvement that must be capitalized and depreciated under its applicable class rather than deducted as ordinary maintenance.
  • How is a new rental-property driveway depreciated?
    A newly installed or substantially improved driveway is generally a depreciable land improvement, while routine patching may be a repair.
  • Can a landlord depreciate landscaping costs?
    Certain land improvements such as landscaping features may be depreciable, while the underlying land itself is not; classification depends on the asset created.
  • How are rental insurance proceeds handled after damage?
    Insurance recovery and the property's adjusted basis must be compared; a casualty loss, gain, repair deduction, or basis adjustment can result depending on facts.
  • What happens when rental land is condemned?
    A condemnation can create a taxable gain or loss, with possible deferral under involuntary conversion rules if qualifying replacement property is acquired on time.
  • Who deducts prorated property taxes at a rental closing?
    Real property taxes at a sale are generally allocated between buyer and seller according to the period of ownership, even when one party remits the bill.

Management, ownership, and basis

  • Is a street or sewer assessment on a rental deductible?
    A local assessment for a specific property improvement generally increases basis; a charge for ordinary maintenance may be deductible as a rental expense.
  • Are HOA fees on a rental property deductible?
    Regular association dues related to an income-producing rental are generally operating expenses; a special assessment for a capital project may need capitalization.
  • When is travel to manage a rental deductible?
    Travel must have a genuine rental business purpose and be substantiated; mixed personal trips require allocation, and improvements may require capitalization of related costs.
  • Can landlords deduct mileage between rental properties?
    Transportation between rental activities may be deductible when tied to rental operations, subject to the applicable vehicle and substantiation rules.
  • Can a landlord claim a home office for rental management?
    A dedicated management space may qualify only if the business-use-of-home tests are met; rental activity alone does not automatically satisfy them.
  • Does using a property manager affect rental tax losses?
    Management fees are generally rental expenses, but outsourcing operational work may leave an owner without enough participation for certain passive-loss exceptions.
  • How do co-owners split rental income and deductions?
    Co-owners generally report their shares of rental income and expenses based on beneficial ownership and actual arrangements; a joint venture may require partnership analysis.
  • What is the tax basis of a gifted rental property?
    A donee's basis often carries over from the donor, with special rules for property received at a loss and for gift tax paid; fair market value alone is not the default.
  • How does inherited rental-property basis work?
    Inherited property's basis is generally tied to date-of-death value, subject to applicable estate and alternate-valuation rules; a new depreciation schedule may begin when held for rent.
  • What basis applies when a home becomes a rental?
    For depreciation after a personal residence converts to rental use, the starting basis is generally the lower of adjusted basis or fair market value at conversion, excluding land.

Short stays and lease arrangements

  • What happens when a rental becomes a personal home?
    Rental expenses and depreciation end when the property is no longer held for income production; basis and depreciation history remain relevant to a later sale.
  • How do you allocate expenses for a partly personal rental?
    A property used both personally and for rent requires allocation of shared costs; vacation-home limits can apply when personal use exceeds statutory thresholds.
  • Is a rental to family at below-market rent a rental activity?
    Renting to a family member below fair rental value can be treated as personal use for vacation-home rules, limiting rental deductions.
  • Are cleaning fees on short-term rentals taxable income?
    Guest cleaning fees are generally receipts of the rental activity; payments to cleaners are separately analyzed as expenses.
  • How should short-term rental occupancy taxes be recorded?
    The treatment depends on whether the tax is imposed on the host or collected from the guest as an agent for the taxing authority; platform remittance adds another layer.
  • How is average guest stay measured for short-term rental passive rules?
    The average customer use period is based on actual rental periods and can affect whether the activity is classified as a rental activity under Section 469 regulations.
  • When do services turn a short-term rental into a different tax activity?
    Substantial services primarily for guest convenience can change how an accommodation activity is reported and may affect self-employment tax; routine cleaning between stays differs.
  • Are guest damage charges rental income?
    Amounts retained from guests for damage may be rental receipts, while the repair, insurance, and basis consequences need separate analysis.
  • How is an option payment on rental real estate taxed?
    A genuine option payment is usually held for later characterization: it may enter the sale proceeds if exercised or become income if the option expires.
  • How do rent credits in a lease-purchase arrangement work?
    Amounts called rent may partly function as purchase consideration when they are credited toward a binding purchase; the contract's economics matter.

Financing, losses, and sale

  • Can a landlord deduct abandoned tenant property cleanup?
    Reasonable cleanup after a tenant leaves may be an operating expense, while replacement or restoration of a major component can be capital.
  • Are eviction legal fees deductible for a rental?
    Legal fees tied to collecting rent or removing a tenant are generally rental operating expenses; fees to acquire or defend title may be capital.
  • Can a landlord deduct unpaid rent that was never received?
    A cash-method landlord generally has no bad-debt deduction for rent never included in income; an accrual-method landlord may face a different analysis.
  • Is cash-out refinancing of a rental property taxable income?
    Loan proceeds normally are not income because they must be repaid; interest deductibility can depend on how the borrowed funds are used.
  • Can rental owners deduct HELOC interest used for property costs?
    Interest treatment generally follows the use of borrowed proceeds, not simply the property that secures the debt.
  • Can you claim a loss when selling a rental to family?
    A loss on a sale to a related person may be disallowed even when the property was genuinely rented and sold for fair value.
  • What if you failed to claim rental depreciation before selling?
    Gain calculations generally reflect depreciation allowed or allowable, including depreciation that could have been claimed; omitted deductions do not simply disappear at sale.
  • Can depreciation recapture be spread with an installment sale?
    Depreciation recapture under Section 1245 and certain Section 1250 rules may be recognized in the year of sale even when sale payments arrive later.
  • Do real-estate commissions reduce taxable gain on a rental sale?
    Selling expenses generally reduce amount realized in computing gain or loss, but loan payoff is repayment of debt rather than a selling expense.
  • How does a free-rent concession affect rental income?
    A landlord using the cash method generally reports rent actually received; a negotiated free month does not create cash income, while related expenses still require normal analysis.
