Compare Tax Advisor Engagements by Scope and Total Cost

Send every shortlisted firm the same brief, then compare what it commits to deliver. A fee or marketing claim alone does not establish the scope of an engagement.

Book a Call

Download the comparison worksheet

Download the blank engagement scorecard (CSV). Open it in a spreadsheet, add each provider’s written responses, and keep the source and date beside every material statement. Blank fields mean unresolved questions, not missing services.

Use the same buyer brief

  • Describe the business, ownership entities, properties, states and upcoming transaction.
  • List the services you need and the professional who currently prepares each return.
  • State the deadline and the records available. Send sensitive records only through the agreed secure process.
  • Request an itemized first-year quote and a separate schedule for future work.

Compare the deliverables

Service or decisionQuestion to confirm
Initial strategyWritten plan, alternatives, assumptions and delivery date
ImplementationWho performs each action and who confirms completion
Entity returnsEntity count, states, preparer and separate fees
Personal returnOwner return, K-1 coordination, states and separate fees
Prior-year reviewYears, correction procedure, amendments and separate fees
Cost segregationStudy methodology, report reviewer and property-specific fee
Study implementationDepreciation reconciliation, Form 3115 responsibility and fee
Bookkeeping and payrollWho changes the books or payroll and what is excluded
Ongoing accessAdvisor contact, meeting cadence and response expectations
Future workRequired renewal, optional scope and separate annual returns
Examination supportExactly which questions, notices or examinations are covered
Full priceFirst-year total, future-year total, installments and cancellation terms

Choose the provider that fits the work

A study specialist can fit when your existing CPA handles the owner-level analysis and filing. A coordinated advisory engagement can fit when you need someone to connect business decisions, rental records and implementation. A larger firm may fit when the project needs a wider specialist team. Confirm the relevant capabilities directly rather than treating an unconfirmed item as unavailable.

AE publishes advisory, return preparation and cost segregation as separately scoped services. Check AE’s current service pricing and the comparison methodology before comparing quotes. AE publishes this worksheet and is a provider you may compare.

What to do with unanswered questions

Ask the provider to identify the responsible professional, deliverable, due date and fee. Record any exclusions and required future commitments. Do not assign a numeric quality score from incomplete information. Keep written proposals so a later decision can be traced to the scope offered.
