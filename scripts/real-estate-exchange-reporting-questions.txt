# Documenting identification and receipt
1031-signed-written-identification-street-address|How do I identify replacement property in writing for a Section 1031 exchange?|Sign a written identification that unambiguously describes the property and deliver it within the identification period to a permitted recipient, such as your qualified intermediary. A street address or legal description can identify real estate.|An investor considering two units in one building lists the full street address and the specific unit number of each candidate in the signed identification delivered to the intermediary.|What proof should I keep?|Save the signed identification and delivery evidence showing what the recipient received and when.|1031-exchange-planning|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p544
1031-revoke-replacement-property-identification-in-writing|Can I withdraw a replacement property from my Section 1031 identification list?|You can generally revoke an identification by signing and delivering a written revocation to the recipient of the original identification before the identification deadline. Check the separate rules if you identified the property in the exchange agreement itself.|On day 30, an investor decides against a listed duplex. The investor delivers a signed revocation to the original recipient and separately identifies a replacement candidate before day 45.|Can I simply leave the property off the closing documents?|Keep the written revocation and delivery evidence. A decision against closing does not by itself remove the property from the identification list.|1031-exchange-planning|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p544
1031-replacement-property-received-within-45-days|Do I have to separately identify a 1031 replacement property I receive within 45 days?|Replacement property actually received within the 45-day identification period is generally treated as identified.
