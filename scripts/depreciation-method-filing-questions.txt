# Before filing a method change
depreciation-change-one-year-history|Why does the number of filed returns matter for a missed depreciation correction?|The filing history helps determine whether the taxpayer adopted an accounting method and which correction procedures are available. Some depreciation changes have special rules for property placed in service in the immediately preceding year.|One rental asset was reported incorrectly on a single return while another was treated the same way on three consecutive returns. Record both histories and check the applicable procedure for each asset before deciding whether to amend or request a method change.|What should the file show?|Record the taxpayer, service date, actual depreciation on each filed return and whether any election or prior correction affected the asset.|amended-return-vs-form-3115|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946
lookback-study-excluded-asset-reconciliation|What should you do if your cost segregation study excludes renovations already on the depreciation schedule?|Keep the study scope and existing asset ledger separate until they are reconciled. An excluded renovation may still need to remain on the taxpayer’s schedule or receive its own review.|A study classifies assets included in the original purchase but excludes a later $32,000 improvement. Locate that improvement on the filed schedule. Verify that none of the study’s asset costs duplicates the later project before using the classifications.|Which records help verify exclusions?|Compare the signed scope, original closing allocation, improvement invoices and fixed-asset ledger through the proposed year of change.|form-3115-cost-segregation-lookback|https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p5653.pdf
form-3115-owner-versus-entity-filing|Who files Form 3115 when a partnership owns the rental building?|Identify the taxpayer whose accounting method is being changed. A partnership’s depreciation method generally belongs to the partnership, even though changes to reported income can affect partners.|A partner commissions a study on real estate held by a partnership. Confirm who placed the property in service and which return has claimed depreciation before asking who signs or files the method change.|What should partners keep for their own returns?|Keep the entity’s updated reporting and any supporting statements relevant to the partner’s basis or loss limitations. Review separately whether any owner-level correction is necessary.|form-3115-cost-segregation|https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i3115
