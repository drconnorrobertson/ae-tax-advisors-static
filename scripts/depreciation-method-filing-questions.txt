# Correcting depreciation after a disposition
form-3115-disposed-rental-disposition-year|Can I file Form 3115 for a rental asset I already sold?|The IRS has a specific automatic change procedure for certain disposed assets with omitted or insufficient depreciation. Under Section 6.07, the year of change is the tax year of disposition. Check eligibility under the procedure applicable to your filing.|A landlord sells depreciable equipment in 2025 and discovers that an impermissible method caused underdepreciation. If the asset and change qualify under Section 6.07, the owner evaluates Form 3115 for the 2025 return.|Can I put the adjustment on the year I discovered the error?|Use the disposition year for a change under Section 6.07. The discovery date helps explain the correction but does not select the year of change.|real-estate-depreciation|https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-23.pdf
disposed-rental-form-3115-amended-return-collateral-adjustments|What must I update on an amended return with Form 3115 for a sold rental asset?|An eligible amendment under Section 6.07 must include taxable-income and related tax adjustments resulting from the method change. Recompute the sale reporting, including any change to the amount or character of gain or loss.|A rental owner identifies omitted depreciation after filing the sale-year return. The owner reconciles the method-change adjustment, corrected asset basis and resulting disposition reporting before submitting the amendment.|Can I file only the depreciation adjustment?|The procedure requires the applicable collateral adjustments too. Tie each affected amount to the amended schedules and the original sale workpapers.|real-estate-depreciation|https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-23.pdf
disposed-rental-form-3115-amendment-duplicate-deadline|When is the duplicate Form 3115 due for an amended disposition-year return?|For a change under Section 6.07 on an amended return or applicable AAR, file the signed duplicate with the IRS no later than the date the original Form 3115 is filed with that return or AAR. Check the filing address and the applicable assessment or adjustment limitation before filing.|An eligible landlord plans to submit a disposition-year amendment on August 5. The owner schedules the signed duplicate to be filed by August 5 and retains evidence of both submissions.|Which deadline should I verify first?|Check the applicable limitation for the disposition year's amendment or AAR, as well as any refund claim deadline, before fixing the submission date.|real-estate-depreciation|https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-23.pdf

The owner checks whether the election to treat the entire replacement basis as newly placed in service and the relinquished basis as disposed was made before relying on Section 6.07.|Does filing Form 8824 itself make that depreciation election?|Review the election requirements and depreciation records separately.
