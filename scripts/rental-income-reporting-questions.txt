# Deposits, co-ownership and changes during the year
refundable-rental-security-deposit-taxable-income|Is a refundable rental security deposit taxable when I collect it?|A deposit you intend to return under the rental agreement generally stays out of rental income when collected. Track the deposit separately from rent and record the amount later refunded or retained.|A host collects $900 in refundable security and $2,400 in rent. The initial rental income is $2,400. If the host returns the full deposit, none of the $900 becomes rent.|Is last month's rent treated the same way if the lease calls it a deposit?|A deposit designated for the final rent payment is advance rent. Include it in income when received.|rental-property-tax-planning|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527
retained-rental-security-deposit-year-income|Which year do I report a security deposit I keep for rental damage?|Include the portion kept because the guest or tenant failed to meet the rental terms in the year you retain it. Evaluate the associated repair cost under the usual rental expense and capitalization rules.|An owner receives a refundable $1,200 deposit in December. In January, the owner retains $350 for qualifying damage under the lease and refunds $850. The $350 is rental income in January's tax year.|What should I retain in the file?|Keep the lease, deposit ledger, dated itemization, proof of refund and repair documentation so you can trace the retained amount separately from the work.|rental-property-tax-planning|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527
lease-purchase-option-rental-income-cutoff|How do I report payments when a tenant exercises a lease option to buy my rental?|Under a lease with an option to buy, payments for use before the sale are generally rental income. Payments received under the agreement for periods after the sale date are considered part of the selling price.|A tenant pays $1,800 a month and exercises the option with a June 30 sale. Regular payments through June are generally rent. A payment under the agreement for July after ownership has transferred belongs in the sale reconciliation.|What if the agreement also credits amounts against the purchase price?|Reconcile the lease, option terms and signed closing statement to determine how the credit changes the reported amounts. Review the treatment of any upfront option fee separately.|rental-property-tax-planning|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p527
co-owned-rental-income-paid-to-one-owner|How do co-owners report rent paid into one owner's account?|Each direct co-owner generally reports their share of rental income. One account receives $30,000 of property rent. Assuming the property is held as direct co-ownership and the split follows those interests, they report $18,000 and $12,000 respectively.|What if the rental is held through a partnership?|Review the partnership reporting and Schedule K-1.
