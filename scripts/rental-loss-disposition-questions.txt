# Sale-year reporting and former passive activities

Assuming no other loss limits apply, report the separate amounts on their usual forms without entering them on Form 8582.|Does the overall loss turn the sale gain into a Schedule E item?|Keep the sale gain and rental operating amounts on their applicable forms. # Passive activity credits
unused-passive-rental-credits-after-complete-sale|Do unused passive credits become deductible when I sell the entire rental activity?|Selling the entire activity does not automatically release unused passive activity credits. After determining any credit allowed under the regular rules, suppose $3,000 remains. If no basis election applies, the owner carries forward that $3,000.|Where are the prior credits tracked?|Use Form 8582-CR to reconcile current and prior unallowed credits. Keep the calculation with the asset and disposition records.|rental-property-tax-planning|https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i8582cr
rental-credit-property-basis-election-form-8582-cr|How much can I add to rental property basis using the Form 8582-CR election?|For an eligible fully taxable disposition of the entire interest to an unrelated person, you may elect to increase credit-property basis immediately before disposition. Assuming the other conditions are met, the election can increase basis by $3,500 and reduce the available unallowed credit by the same amount.|Where do I make the election?|Use Part VI of Form 8582-CR.
