# Establishing the disposed component
partial-disposition-retirement-before-replacement|What date matters if you remove a building component before its replacement is ready?|Track the disposition date of the old component separately from the placed-in-service date of the replacement. The partial disposition analysis generally follows the year the old portion is disposed of.|A landlord removes an old HVAC component in December but does not commission the replacement until February. Document when the old component was permanently withdrawn and separately establish when the replacement was ready and available.|Which records can help establish the dates?|Keep removal and disposal invoices, work orders, contractor records and commissioning evidence for the new component.|depreciation-and-fixed-asset-review-for-rental-properties|https://www.irs.gov/pub/fatca/int_practice_units/examining-tp-electing-partial-disposition.pdf
partial-disposition-original-basis-missing-invoices|How do you document original basis for a removed component when invoices are missing?|If the original unadjusted depreciable basis of the disposed portion cannot practicably be determined from existing records, review the reasonable methods allowed by the regulations. Preserve the inputs and calculation used to identify the amount.|An owner has the original building depreciation schedule and current replacement invoices but no original component invoice. Document why the original cost cannot be identified and evaluate a permitted estimation method before using any amount.|Can you simply use today’s replacement price as original basis?|Analyze the reasonable method and any required adjustments. The replacement invoice is evidence of current cost and does not by itself establish historical basis.|depreciation-and-fixed-asset-review-for-rental-properties|https://www.irs.gov/pub/fatca/int_practice_units/examining-tp-electing-partial-disposition.pdf
# Election timing and records
partial-disposition-timely-return-election|How is an ordinary partial disposition election made for a replaced rental component?|The election is generally made by reporting the gain or loss on a timely filed original return, including extensions, for the year of disposition. Review whether any special rules apply before relying on a later correction.|An owner removed an old component during the year. The preparer finishes the disposed-component basis calculation and reports the result on the timely original return while retaining the supporting workpapers.|Is a standalone election statement ordinarily required?|The IRS practice unit describes the election as made through reporting the gain or loss. Retain the supporting records even though a separate statement is generally not required.|depreciation-and-fixed-asset-review-for-rental-properties|https://www.irs.gov/pub/fatca/int_practice_units/examining-tp-electing-partial-disposition.pdf
