# Search recovery measurement

The recovery inventory contains public canonical paths only. Private Search Console
exports and results stay outside source control under ignored research/gsc-20261108/.
Run `python3 scripts/audit-gsc-indexing.py PATH_TO_LOCAL_INVENTORIES` after a staged build.

Use the verified `https://www.aetaxadvisors.com/` URL-prefix property under the AE team
account. Compare equal complete 28-day periods with the same Web, country and device
filters; avoid mixing an incomplete day into one side. Export Pages and use
`python3 scripts/report-gsc-content-groups.py CURRENT.csv PREVIOUS.csv`.

Track: indexed status of intended canonical pages, last crawl, Google's canonical,
clicks/impressions/CTR/position by landing page and content group. Inspect page-query
pairs before merging pages; shared words alone do not establish cannibalization.
Alternatives are option discovery, comparisons are a specific shortlist, pricing is
scope/cost, and reviews disclose the commercial editorial relationship.

Booking intent events now include content_group. They use the existing local
integration and do not establish GA4 ingestion or completed bookings. Confirmed,
qualified calls require the CRM appointment outcome and approved attribution setup.
Do not report an iframe view as a lead or a new event hook as collected conversions.

Speed Insights runtime is already included by the release build. PageSpeed lab
scores, CrUX origin measurements and Search Console URL-group reports are separate
measurements. Keep report URLs, timestamps and device modes with results. Field data
and Google recrawl cannot be accelerated into a same-day outcome claim.

Keep true publication dates. Substantive changes may update visible update dates,
Article dateModified and the reviewed-lastmod inventory; no synthetic historical dates.
