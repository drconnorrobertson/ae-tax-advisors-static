# Depreciation and gain character
unrecaptured-section-1250-straight-line-rental|Can straight-line depreciation affect the tax rate on a rental building gain?|Yes. An individual’s gain attributable to depreciation on qualifying Section 1250 property can be unrecaptured Section 1250 gain, subject to a maximum federal rate of 25% and the applicable netting rules.|Assume a qualifying building has $40,000 of depreciation and $60,000 of gain. Track the depreciation-related portion separately when working through the gain worksheet. The full $60,000 is not automatically taxed at 25%.|Is this the same as ordinary income recapture on equipment?|Review the rules by asset. Section 1245 equipment and Section 1250 real property can produce different categories of gain.|how-does-depreciation-recapture-work|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p544
installment-sale-report-recapture-year-of-sale|When is depreciation recapture reported if a rental sale is paid in installments?|Depreciation recapture that is treated as ordinary income generally is reported in the year of sale, even if you receive some payments later. Review installment treatment separately for the remaining eligible gain.|An installment sale includes appliances that produce $6,000 of Section 1245 ordinary income recapture. Track that $6,000 in the year of sale before calculating the remaining gain eligible for installment reporting.|Which schedules should tie together?|Reconcile the proceeds and adjusted basis by asset, then tie the Form 4797 reporting to the installment sale workpaper as applicable.|installment-sale-tax-strategy|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p537
# Foreclosure and lender reporting
rental-foreclosure-recourse-deficiency-tax|How do I report a recourse foreclosure on a rental when the lender cancels the deficiency?|Compute the disposition and the canceled debt separately. When canceled recourse principal exceeds fair market value and no debt remains due after the transfer, fair market value generally enters the disposition calculation. The remaining canceled amount may be ordinary income unless an exception or exclusion applies.|A lender forecloses on a rental worth $250,000, cancels the full $310,000 recourse balance, and pays the owner nothing. With $230,000 of adjusted basis, the owner has $20,000 of disposition gain and separately evaluates $60,000 of canceled debt.|What if the lender can still collect part of the deficiency?|Use the debt the lender actually discharged and separately track the amount still owed. Check the settlement terms and dates before assigning income to a tax year.|rental-property-tax-planning|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p4681
nonrecourse-rental-foreclosure-debt-above-value|How do I calculate gain on a rental foreclosure with nonrecourse debt above market value?|When the lender takes the secured rental and the nonrecourse debt is extinguished, the amount realized generally includes the outstanding nonrecourse balance even if it exceeds the property value. The transferred property generally produces a disposition calculation without separate canceled-debt income from that debt.|A rental worth $220,000 has $275,000 of nonrecourse debt and $190,000 of adjusted basis. The transfer extinguishes the debt and the owner receives nothing else. The amount realized is $275,000 and the resulting gain is $85,000 before character and other adjustments.|Does the owner need to look at depreciation history?|Yes. Reconcile allowed or allowable depreciation to adjusted basis and evaluate the tax character of the resulting gain on the relevant disposition forms.|rental-property-tax-planning|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p4681
form-1099-a-rental-foreclosure-boxes|What do the loan balance and fair market value on Form 1099-A mean for a rental?|Box 2 reports outstanding principal and Box 4 generally reports the lender's fair market value. rental-foreclosure-debt-canceled-following-year|Can a rental foreclosure and cancellation of the remaining debt be reported in different tax years?|A completed property transfer and later cancellation of a recourse deficiency are separate events.
