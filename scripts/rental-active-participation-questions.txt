rental-active-participation-with-manager|Can a landlord actively participate while using a property manager?|Potentially. # Calculating the special allowance
rental-special-allowance-magi-phaseout|How does modified adjusted gross income reduce the rental loss special allowance?|For a taxpayer subject to the ordinary $100,000 starting threshold, reduce the maximum allowance by 50% of modified adjusted gross income above that threshold. Check filing status and the applicable definition of modified income.|Assume an otherwise eligible joint filer has $120,000 of modified adjusted gross income. The $20,000 excess reduces the $25,000 maximum by $10,000, leaving a $15,000 maximum under these assumptions.|Does a $15,000 maximum mean every filer deducts that amount?|The result is a cap. Review the actual eligible loss and other applicable limits to determine the deduction.|passive-activity-loss-rules-real-estate|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p925
rental-special-allowance-married-filing-separately|Can married taxpayers filing separately use the special rental loss allowance?|A married taxpayer filing separately who lived apart from their spouse for the entire year can generally use up to $12,500 before phaseout. The allowance generally is unavailable if the spouses lived together at any time during the year.|A married taxpayer filing separately lived apart all year and meets the other requirements. With $60,000 of modified adjusted gross income, the $10,000 excess over $50,000 reduces the $12,500 cap to $7,500 in this example.|Which record should the preparer confirm first?|Confirm filing status and living arrangements for the full year, then compute modified adjusted gross income under the applicable instructions.|passive-activity-loss-rules-real-estate|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p925
rental-special-allowance-remaining-loss|What happens to rental losses above the special allowance?|An otherwise disallowed passive rental loss generally carries forward. Assuming no passive income or other adjustments, the remaining $8,000 carries forward under the passive rules.|Should you reduce the carryforward by the maximum or the actual deduction?|Use the amount actually allowed.
