# Acquisition timing and used property
bonus-binding-contract-before-cutoff|Can a pre-cutoff binding contract affect bonus depreciation on a later delivery?|Yes. The acquisition timing rules for a written binding contract can put property outside the post-January 19, 2025 rules even if delivery or installation occurs later. Check the contract and special acquisition rules before selecting the applicable percentage.|An owner orders equipment under a contract signed in early January 2025 and installs it in March. Determine whether and when the contract became binding and track the separate placed-in-service date before computing bonus depreciation.|Which documents matter beyond the invoice?|Keep the signed contract, cancellation terms, amendments, delivery records and evidence of when the property was ready and available.|bonus-depreciation-rental-property|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946
bonus-used-furniture-unrelated-purchase|Can used furniture bought for a rental qualify for bonus depreciation?|Certain used property can qualify if it meets the acquisition and property requirements. Verify prior use by the taxpayer or predecessor, the relationship to the seller and the basis of the property.|A landlord buys secondhand furnishings from an unrelated seller and installs them in a furnished rental. Document the sale, classification and service date, and review the applicable used-property requirements before claiming a deduction.|What if the owner previously used the property?|Document the prior use and ownership history. The fact that the item is newly used in this rental does not establish that it meets the used-property acquisition requirements.|bonus-depreciation-rental-property|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946
bonus-related-seller-equipment-sale|Does paying fair market value to a related seller establish bonus depreciation eligibility?|Fair market pricing does not by itself satisfy the used-property acquisition rules. Review the related-party tests and other acquisition requirements separately.|An owner buys used equipment from a business under common ownership and obtains an appraisal. Use the appraisal to support value, then evaluate the buyer and seller relationship before including the asset in the bonus calculation.|What records should be kept?|Retain the purchase agreement, seller information, ownership structure and tax basis support. Document which acquisition rule applies.|bonus-depreciation-rental-property|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946
# Elections and depreciation schedules
bonus-election-out-by-property-class|Can you elect out of bonus depreciation for just one item within the same class?|An ordinary election out generally applies to all qualified property in the elected class placed in service in the same tax year. Identify the classes before deciding what the election would cover.|A rental operator places two eligible assets in the same five-year class into service and also adds eligible 15-year property. An election out for the five-year class would generally cover both five-year assets, while treatment of the other class is considered separately.|Where should the preparer document the decision?|Reconcile the asset list by class and retain the filed election statement for the tax year.|bonus-depreciation-rental-property|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946
bonus-ads-required-asset-screen|Can an asset required to use ADS qualify for bonus depreciation?|Property required to be depreciated under the alternative depreciation system generally cannot claim the special depreciation allowance. Determine why ADS applies before removing the item from the bonus schedule.|An asset list contains a shorter-life item that otherwise meets bonus timing requirements but is required to use ADS. Classify that asset under the applicable ADS rules while evaluating the other assets independently.|Does an ADS rule for one asset automatically control every asset?|Identify which assets the requirement applies to and document classifications separately. Do not assume one schedule method applies to the entire property.|real-estate-depreciation|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946
