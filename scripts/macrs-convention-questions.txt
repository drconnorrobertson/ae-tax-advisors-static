# Applying a convention to real property

Compute the partial year using the applicable method.|A calendar-year landlord places a residential rental building into service in October. The mid-month convention generally gives half of October plus November and December, equivalent to 2.5 months in this simplified example.|Does an earlier purchase date add another month?|Use when the building was ready and available for rental use.
