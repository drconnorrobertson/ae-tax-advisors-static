# Classifying qualified improvement property
qip-existing-buildout-bought-with-building|Does buying a building with an existing interior buildout make that buildout QIP for the buyer?|Generally, an improvement must be made by the taxpayer to qualify as QIP. A purchase allocation to an existing interior improvement does not by itself satisfy that requirement.|A buyer acquires a building with a recently remodeled interior and later pays for a separate project. Keep the acquired assets and later work on distinct schedules, then review the treatment of each.|Does this decide the recovery period for every acquired asset?|No. Review the classification and acquisition rules for each asset, including separately identifiable equipment, independently of the QIP question.|real-estate-depreciation|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946
qip-elevator-and-interior-scope|Can an elevator modernization qualify as QIP because it is inside a commercial building?|QIP excludes costs attributable to elevators and escalators. Identify those costs separately when an interior project contains both elevator work and potentially eligible improvements.|An invoice includes elevator controls and new finishes in an adjacent tenant area. Obtain a supported breakdown and apply the QIP tests to the distinct interior finishes separately.|Does QIP exclusion make the cost immediately deductible?|Evaluate the elevator work under the ordinary capitalization and asset classification rules. The QIP exclusion does not establish whether the cost is a repair or a capital improvement.|real-estate-depreciation|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946
qip-residential-rental-interior-ineligible|Can an interior renovation of residential rental real property qualify as QIP?|The QIP definition applies to eligible interior improvements to nonresidential real property. Determine the building’s tax classification before applying the QIP rules.|An owner remodels the interior of a building classified as residential rental property. The interior location does not make the renovation QIP. Evaluate separate assets and applicable capitalization rules independently.|What if rental use or building classification is uncertain?|Review the actual property use and depreciation classification and keep the records supporting how the building was classified.|rental-property-tax-planning|https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946
qip-15-year-gds-20-year-ads|Does qualified improvement property use the same recovery period under GDS and ADS?|QIP generally has a 15-year recovery period under GDS and a 20-year period under ADS.
