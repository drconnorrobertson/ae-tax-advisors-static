## Inventory and selection rule

The site currently has 46 comparison-hub entries before the Aprio addition. Existing pages already cover Hall CPA/The Real Estate CPA, Anderson, Mark Kohler, Tax Alchemy, Cherry Bekaert, Moss Adams, Deloitte, KPMG, WCG, KBKG, Capstan, CSSI, Bedford, and several other named firms and service-model comparisons. A reversed brand order, a different city, or a superficial “alternative” page is not a new buying problem. Existing published comparisons need ongoing source audits; the Anderson consolidation demonstrates why.

For each proposed URL, document the owner who can hire AE, the near-term deal or filing decision, consequential tax/asset exposure, AE's plausible paid work, the competing provider's current official offering, the exact difference in buying scope, and any AE canonical already serving the intent. Score buyer intent (30), distinctness (25), query evidence (20), paid-service fit (15), and authority gap (10); require both 70+ and premium fit. An official competitor service page validates its offering, **not** search volume or performance. Do not claim exact-query demand without export or Search Console evidence. Named-provider pages should be neutral, specific, and willing to say when that provider is the better fit.

Do not create low-stakes consumer or thin name-swap pages; existing service-model pages already capture much of that intent.
